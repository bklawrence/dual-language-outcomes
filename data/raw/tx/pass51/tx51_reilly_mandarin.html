Two-Way Dual Language Mandarin at Reilly Elementary

We are home to Austin ISD’s only PK-4 Mandarin program. 

We opened as a Two-Way Dual Language Mandarin Chinese program in the Fall of 2018. 
We have Two-Way Dual Language Mandarin from Pre-K4 through 5th Grade.


AISD’s Dual language program is open to all AISD students who are interested in a multilingual academic experience when entering Pre-Kindergarten until the first ten days of first grade. 

 

The three goals of Austin ISD's dual language program are for students to:

  1. Excel academically in two languages 
  2. Become bilingual and biliterate 
  3. Develop positive cross-cultural competency

Read more about the AISD Dual Language Mandarin Chinese Program(s) here: https://www.austinisd.org/mandarinchinese

Please check https://www.austinisd.org/dual-language/application to register and contact our campus to find out about tours.
