Dual Language Immersion

Our district's Dual Language Immersion (DLI)  program teaches standard curriculum subjects primarily in Spanish. Through engaging, interactive lessons, the students master core academic concepts while naturally developing fluent Spanish language skills from elementary through high school.

Participating Schools

  • Elementary: Cardinal Valley, Liberty, and Maxwell 
  • Middle School: Bryan Station Middle
  • High School: Bryan Station High

Research shows that students of all backgrounds and ability levels thrive in dual-language environments. Starting early enhances cognitive development, leading to:

  • Increased mental flexibility and problem-solving skills
  • Stronger overall language awareness and literacy
  • Improved listening comprehension and focus

DLI Brochure

The DLI goal is to help every student achieve high-level proficiency, literacy, and academic success in both Spanish and English.

 

  • Enrollment
  • In Practice

Enrollment

Our Dual Language Immersion (DLI) programs have no formal admission criteria. Enrollment processes vary by school and grade level.

Maxwell Magnet Elementary

  • Kindergarten and first grade: Students are admitted via lottery.
  • Grades 2-5: Admission is subject to space availability and a Spanish proficiency assessment (reading, writing, speaking, and listening).

Visit our districtwide Application webpage for details and deadlines.

Cardinal Valley & Liberty Elementaries

  • Kindergarten: Families can choose either the DLI track or the standard curriculum.
  • Grades 1-5: New applicants must contact the school directly to ask about entry.

Bryan Station Middle & Bryan Station High

  • Continuing students: DLI students automatically transition to the middle and high school programs; no reapplication needed.
  • New students (Grades 6–10): They must complete a magnet program application and take a Spanish proficiency assessment. Visit our  districtwide Application webpage for details and deadlines, and email Adan Henriquez. 

In Practice

Our K-12 DLI program introduces students to a second language early in their education, building fluent Spanish and English skills as they progress through graduation.

Elementary Schools

  • Maxwell Magnet Elementary (full-school model): Every student at Maxwell participates in the DLI program.
  • Cardinal Valley & Liberty elementaries (strand model): Some students split their school day, spending 50 percent of their time learning core subjects in Spanish and 50 percent in English.

Secondary Schools

  • Bryan Station Middle: DLI students take Math (6th grade), Science, Social Studies, and Language Arts in Spanish.
  • Bryan Station High: DLI students advance their skills through high-level Spanish coursework, including:
    • Freshman Seminar
    • Advanced Biology, Chemistry, and Physics
    • Advanced Spanish courses, Spanish AP courses, and dual credit courses in Spanish
