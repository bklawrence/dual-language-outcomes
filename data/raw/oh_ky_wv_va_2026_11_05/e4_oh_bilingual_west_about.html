About our school

Columbus Bilingual West is a tuition-free public charter school serving students in Kindergarten (Early Entry) through 8th grade across West Columbus, Ohio, from the Hilltop out to far west Grove City. The school delivers daily instruction in both English and Spanish, building bilingual students who carry two languages and the cultural confidence that comes with them. Principal Odaliz Freytes leads a building where Spanish isn't a weekly enrichment block. It's part of how the school operates, every day, for every student.

The academic program is standards-based and rigorous: reading, math, science, and social studies, with instruction adjusted to meet each student's level. Classes are hands-on, emphasizing critical thinking and real understanding rather than memorization. Teachers use the data to know where each child is and what he or she needs next.

The school runs on the Jag Awards system, recognizing students quarterly for growth and character. The VALOR Student of the Week program celebrates individual effort. Art classes and cultural activities support creativity and expression. Field trips and school events bring learning beyond the classroom walls.

After-school programming runs until 6:00 p.m. Counseling services are provided through a partnership with SANOS Counseling, the school's bilingual counseling provider. Transportation is provided through Columbus City Schools. Every enrolled student receives free breakfast and lunch.

Community partnerships run deep. The school works with Columbus Metropolitan Library (Hilltop Branch), Simakovsky Law, Columbus Public Health, Franklin County Sheriff Department, Central Ohio Worker Center / Justice for Migrant Women, Alianza 614, Coats4Childrens, Kroger, Smile Programs (mobile dentists), Girl Scouts of Ohio, Ohio Optometric Association, Nationwide Children's Hospital (Center for Healthy Weight and Nutrition), and OSU Extension (College of Food, Agricultural, and Environmental Sciences). Events include Latino Heritage Month, the Latino Heritage Parade, Back to School Event, Turkey Trot, Holiday Celebration, Winter Clothing Drive, Books and Badges Girl Scouts Program, EFNEP Nutrition Education Celebration, Realeyes Program, Jag Awards assemblies, and Parent Teacher Conferences.

Free full-day Kindergarten is available, with early entrance for students who turn 5 by December 31 through a readiness assessment.

Columbus Bilingual West is located at 680 Harrisburg Pike, Columbus, OH 43223. Call (380) 266-0055 or visit cbwschool.org.

Community School

Columbus Bilingual West is a public, community school. We are our own school district sponsored by Richland Academy of the Arts which provides oversight and enforces academic, fiscal, and legal accountability. We are managed by ACCEL Schools, a high-performing network of over 50 charter schools located throughout the United States.

Community schools offer an alternative to the local public school. They are free and open to all students and focused on meeting student needs for success. Community schools are among the top-performing schools in the nation.

Learn more about community schools.

Managed by ACCEL Schools

Founded in 2014, ACCEL Schools is a K-12 public charter school management organization (CMO) serving students across the United States in brick-and-mortar, virtual, and hybrid school settings. We focus on improving academic outcomes of students regardless of geographic location or socioeconomic factors. The leadership team at ACCEL Schools has a combined 1000+ years of experience as global school management operators with expertise in curriculum research and design, assessment, student support services, and education consulting. Our entire team fundamentally believes that all children deserve a great education and students should be supported as they pursue a wide variety of post-secondary options. Our robust technology increases engagement, access to resources, efficiencies, and academic results helping our students become productive global citizens.
