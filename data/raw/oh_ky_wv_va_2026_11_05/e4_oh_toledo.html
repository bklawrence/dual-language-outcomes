About US

Escuela SMART Academy mission: We are a family who prioritizes education, values bilingualism, takes pride in our cultures and empowers one another.


Our school offers:

  • A bilingual/bicultural atmosphere
  • Daily Spanish classes
  • English as a second language (ESL) service
  • Dress code – no uniform required
  • Transportation within the TPS district
  • Free breakfast and lunch
  • Free vision and hearing screening
  • After-school programs
  • After-school tutoring
  • Academic Nights
  • Family events
  • Parent leadership opportunities
  • Sofia Quintero Arts and Cultural Center programming
  • Mental health services
