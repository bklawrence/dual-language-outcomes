About the School

Northern Elementary, which lies on the north side of Lexington, opened in 1969. Our school serves a diverse population of students from preschool through fifth grade.

We seek to build a strong sense of community at our school. We implement the House System inspired by the Ron Clark Academy where every student and staff member is sorted into one of five houses: Altruismo, Amistad, Isibindi, Reveur, or Sollevare. Five Houses, One Family! We are also excited to have a new mascot, the Pizzly Bear. Just like our school community, a Pizzly Bear is unique, resilient, and adaptable. We are built different!

Northern emphasizes systems of support that include proactive strategies for defining, teaching, and supporting appropriate student behaviors to create positive school environments. We strive for our students to follow these schoolwide expectations:

C - Community First

U - Understand Safety

B - Bear Responsibility

S - Show Kindness

In addition, Northern offers several extracurricular activities such as Girls on the Run, Basketball, Cheerleading, Academic Team, and STLP. We have an active PTA, too, and love to have families involved in our school!

Hours

  • Doors open: 7:55 a.m.
  • School day begins: 8:25 a.m.
  • Dismissal: 3:15 p.m.

Vision

Northern Elementary is a school where every child belongs as they actively engage in learning. We hold high expectations for all students, empowering them to reach their fullest potential.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Community
  • Commitment
  • Perseverance
