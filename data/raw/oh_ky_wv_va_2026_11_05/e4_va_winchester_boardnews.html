    Winchester School Board expands dual language immersion program

    • By MOLLY WILLIAMS The Winchester Star

      Molly Williams

      • Author email
    • Feb 27, 2025
    • Feb 27, 2025
    • Facebook
    • Twitter
    • WhatsApp

    WINCHESTER — The Winchester School Board unanimously approved a proposal to expand Winchester Public Schools’ dual language immersion program during its meeting Monday night.

    That means that at the start of the 2025-26 school year, dual language immersion opportunities will be available to Garland R. Quarles Elementary School kindergartners.

    The following school year, 2026-27, the program will be expanded to include John Kerr Elementary School kindergartners and first graders.

    The program is already offered at Frederick Douglass and Virginia Avenue Charlotte DeHart elementary schools, as well as the Daniel Morgan campus.

    Elementary and intermediate school dual language immersion students receive 50% of their daily instruction in Spanish, whereas seventh and eighth graders in the program take their U.S. History class in Spanish and/or a Spanish elective.

    High school world language opportunities vary, including Spanish III, Spanish IV, Advanced Placement Spanish and Advanced Placement Spanish Literature.

    High School students can also access world language opportunities like French or German online through Virtual Virginia. 

    “I’m really excited that we can bring this to the entire school division,” said WPS Superintendent Jason Van Heukelum. “... To be bilingual, how cool is that, right? To have that access for all of our students to be bilingual. And when we say bilingual, it’s not just speaking. It’s listening, speaking, reading and writing. It’s formal, and so I’m really proud that we’ll be able to offer that.”
