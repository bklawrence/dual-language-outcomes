What sets our community apart

Language is the key to unlocking a child’s full potential.

At Columbus Bilingual Academy North, we provide a rigorous academic foundation for students in grades K–8, ensuring every child is fully prepared for the challenges of high school and beyond.

01Dual-Language Mastery: we bridge the gap for English learners, delivering core content in both English and Spanish so no student falls behind.
02Expert Educators: our teachers specialize in bilingual education and foster classrooms where students are truly excited to learn.
03Holistic Growth: through our Kids Thrive program, social-emotional learning is part of daily life, nurturing resilient, empathetic and engaged citizens.
04Global Readiness: our multicultural curriculum enriches learning and prepares students to lead in an interconnected world.
