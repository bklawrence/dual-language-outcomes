What Is Dual Language Immersion?

Dual Language Immersion (DLI) is an educational model in which students learn academic content and literacy in English and another language, known as the target language.

Unlike traditional world language classes, where students may study a language for only a short period each day, DLI integrates language learning throughout the school day. Students use both languages to communicate, solve problems, explore ideas, and learn grade-level academic content.

Immersion teachers use visuals, modeling, gestures, collaboration, repetition, and other research-based strategies to make instruction understandable while helping students develop listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills.

Decades of research consistently demonstrate that well-implemented Dual Language Immersion programs can support bilingualism, biliteracy, academic achievement, and intercultural competence.

Benefits of Dual Language Immersion

Students in high-quality DLI programs gain academic, linguistic, cognitive, cultural, and personal benefits that extend well beyond the classroom.

01
Bilingualism and Biliteracy

Students develop proficiency in listening, speaking, reading, and writing in two languages. They learn to use language for meaningful communication and increasingly complex academic work.

02
Academic Achievement

Research shows that students in well-designed immersion programs perform as well as or better than their peers in traditional programs while also developing proficiency in another language.

03
Cognitive Growth

Learning through two languages can strengthen flexible thinking, problem-solving, attention control, working memory, and the ability to consider ideas from more than one perspective.

04
Intercultural Competence

Students develop deeper awareness of other cultures, perspectives, and communication practices. They learn to approach differences with curiosity, respect, and empathy.

05
Heritage Language Development

Students who speak Mandarin Chinese or Korean at home can continue developing their language in an academic setting, including advanced vocabulary, literacy, literature, and cultural knowledge.

06
Future Opportunities

Bilingualism can expand students' future educational and professional opportunities in fields such as education, healthcare, government, technology, business, and international relations.

What Research Shows

Students in Dual Language Immersion programs can achieve strong academic outcomes while developing high levels of proficiency in two languages. The greatest benefits are associated with consistent participation in a high-quality program over multiple years.

YHALE's Dual Language Immersion Model

YHALE offers Mandarin Chinese-English and Korean-English pathways. Our instructional model evolves as students progress from elementary to middle school.

Kindergarten–Grade 5

50/50 Instructional Model

Approximately 50% in English
  • English Language Arts
  • Mathematics
  • Social Studies
Approximately 50% in Mandarin or Korean
  • Target Language Arts
  • Science
  • Music
  • Art
  • Physical Education
Grades 6–8

70/30 Instructional Model

70% English
30% Target Language

As students transition to middle school, the model provides additional time for increasingly rigorous academic coursework while maintaining meaningful, sustained instruction in Mandarin Chinese or Korean.

  • Continued target-language literacy development
  • Advanced academic vocabulary
  • Cultural and interdisciplinary learning
  • Preparation for high school coursework
Why Start Young?

Early, consistent, and meaningful exposure gives children time to develop strong pronunciation, communication skills, academic language, and literacy in both languages.

K
Early Exposure

Students become familiar with new sounds, vocabulary, and classroom routines.

1–2
Building Foundations

Students expand oral language and begin developing literacy skills in both languages.

3–5
Academic Language

Students use increasingly complex language to read, write, discuss, and learn.

6–8
Advanced Biliteracy

Students refine academic communication and apply both languages across disciplines.

Biliteracy Develops Over Time

Developing strong bilingualism and biliteracy is a gradual process. Research suggests that students often experience the greatest language and academic benefits after participating in a high-quality Dual Language Immersion program for five to seven years.

Consistent enrollment gives students the time and sustained practice needed to develop advanced literacy, academic vocabulary, and confidence in both languages.

Language Growth Through Immersion

YHALE's program supports students who are learning a new language as well as students who are continuing to develop a language spoken by their families.

For New Language Learners

English-speaking students learn Mandarin Chinese or Korean while continuing to strengthen their English literacy and grade-level academic skills.

For Heritage Language Learners

Mandarin- or Korean-speaking students develop English while expanding the academic vocabulary, reading, writing, and cultural knowledge associated with their home language.
