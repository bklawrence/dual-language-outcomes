Dreamers Academy

SPANISH IMMERSION PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL

Dreamers Academy brings dual-language Spanish immersion education to Sarasota and Manatee Counties families who want the benefits of multilingualism for their children:

SMARTER

…than their peers at other schools

HEALTHIER

…with less brain decline as they age

WEALTHIER

… significantly higher career earnings

This education model has been proven in 4,000 schools across the US, directly correlating to greater academic achievement across all content areas, higher levels of divergent thinking/problem solving, and greater cultural awareness versus traditional English-only programs.

ABOUT DREAMERS ACADEMY

Program Features

  • K – 8th Grade
  • Bilingual teachers & staff
  • Become fluent in both English and Spanish across all subjects
  • Caring environment with high academic expectations
  • Full-range curriculum
  • No language experience required
  • Tuition-free public charter school

Goals for Your Child

  • High academic achievement in both English and Spanish
  • Read, write, speak and listen in both languages
  • Higher levels of divergent thinking and problem solving
  • Cultural awareness and sensitivity
  • Being ahead of grade level by 5th grade

Our Core Beliefs

  • Language learning is a natural process for children
  • Being bilingual improves intellectual and social-emotional development
  • Being biliterate opens professional opportunities
  • Being bicultural values and engages in different perspectives
