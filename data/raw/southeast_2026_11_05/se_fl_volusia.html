Dual Language

Multilingual Learners Department

  • Dual Language

The Dual Language Immersion Program provides students the opportunity to learn English and Spanish, while maintaining high academic standards. All instruction in English and Spanish is aligned with state standards and provides students literacy and content instruction in both English and Spanish. Students will experience half of their academics in English and the other half in Spanish, allowing them to acquire the languages naturally and experience continuous opportunities for meaningful interactions and practice. Students will develop bilingual skills and cross-cultural competencies necessary for an ever-changing global society and economy.
