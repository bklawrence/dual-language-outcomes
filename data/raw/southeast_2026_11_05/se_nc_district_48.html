KINDERGARTEN Dual Language: Message for Parents
Kindergarten Dual Language Staff:  Jessica Lozano-Ramirez - Lead Teacher / Ashley Fox - Instructional Assistant  

August 5 - September 4th - Mrs. Lozano will be on family leave.  Her return is scheduled for September 8th.  
During Mrs. Lozano's absence, Mrs. Patricia Powell will serve as the lead teacher.  Mrs. Powell has 32 years of experience as a kindergarten teacher and will provide instruction in English with the support of Ms. Fox. 
Spanish - Dr. Hicks will provide a Spanish language instructional block each afternoon.
Together, we will ensure that every student gets off to a successful start to the 26-27 school year.

Upon her return, Mrs. Lozano will implement the traditional 50/50 schedule to ensure standards are taught in the blended, dual language format.
