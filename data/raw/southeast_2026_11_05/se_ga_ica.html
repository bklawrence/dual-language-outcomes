Welcome to ICAGeorgia

Your educational journey begins with us

International Charter Academy of Georgia (ICAGeorgia) is a statewide charter school available free of charge to any K-8 student residing in Georgia. ICAGeorgia offers a Dual Language immersion program in both English and Japanese. Cognia-accredited school.

Our Mission

To broaden the horizons of students in Georgia to a diverse international community so that they may become global citizens who promote peace around the world.

Our goal is to equip students with an education that fully harnesses their unique talents and sets them up for success in a technology-focused and information-rich 21st Century economy.

About Our School

An international approach to individual learning

ICAGeorgia offers a Dual Language immersion program in both English and Japanese in areas such as Math, Science, Social Studies, Art, Music and Physical Education.

Accreditation

ICAGeorgia is dedicated to the highest academic standards

International Charter Academy of Georgia is now a COGNIA-accredited school. Accredited by the North Central Association of Colleges and Schools (NCA CASI), the Northwest Accreditation Commission (NWAC) and the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Council on Accreditation and School Improvement (SACS CASI).
