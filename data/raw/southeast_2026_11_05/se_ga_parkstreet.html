Dual Language Immersion Program

Dear Park Street Families,

I'm writing to share an important update about the Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program at Park Street.

After careful consideration of student performance data, the Superintendent and I, in collaboration with our Director of Multilingual Learners and Director of Structured Literacy, have decided not to enroll new kindergarten students in the DLI program next year. This decision does not impact students currently enrolled. We are committed to continuing the program for these students and will ensure it remains available to them through the completion of their time at Park Street.

We understand that the DLI program is important to many of our families, and we are committed to honoring and supporting the experience for all students currently participating.

If you have questions, please do not hesitate to reach out.

Warm regards, 

Chuck Gardner, Ed.D.
Deputy Superintendent
