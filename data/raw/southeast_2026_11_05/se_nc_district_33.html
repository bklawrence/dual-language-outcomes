Dual Language Program at Bruce Drysdale

Dual language is a form of bilingual education in which language learning takes place primarily through content instruction. All students learn to read, write, and speak in both languages.

Click for more information on:

  • Our Mission
  • What Is Dual Language?

All teachers in the Dual Language/Immersion Program are Highly Qualified. The program follows the Common Core Standards for Mathematics and Language Arts.

Dual Language classes begin in kindergarten. One bilingual teacher per classroom will teach all academic content. Lessons are not interpreted; only one language is used for lessons in each content area.

Further resources are available through the North Carolina World Language site.
