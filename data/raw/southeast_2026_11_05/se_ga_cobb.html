    Dual Language Immersion

    SHARE



    Program Availability 

    Which schools will offer the DLI program to entering kindergarteners? 

    • Nickajack ES, Smyrna ES, Russell ES, Mableton ES, and Dowell ES

    Which schools will offer the middle school DLI program?

    • Campbell Middle School and Floyd Middle School

    Which schools will offer the high school DLI program?

    • Campbell High School and South Cobb High School

    How many students will be able to participate in the DLI programs at Campbell MS, Floyd MS, Campbell High School, and South Cobb High School?

    • Availability for student participation varies and it is determined by grade-level cohort size.

    Course Availability

    What language course options are available to students at their local MS for those who cannot attend Campbell MS or Floyd MS? 

    • Course options vary by school. Check with your local school for a course recommendation based on the student’s language proficiency and the courses available at the local school.

    At what grade level in MS will students be able to take language courses?

    • Course availability varies. Please check with your local school.

    Notes on Translation

    The electronic translation service is hosted by Google Translate. The quality of the translation will vary in some of the languages offered by Google. Google Translate is a free service and currently offers translation in over 50 languages, although an impressive number, this does not capture all languages or dialects. The basic translation’s goal is to capture the general intention of the original English material.

    The Cobb County School District does not guarantee the quality, accuracy or completeness of any translated information. Before you act on translated information, the District encourages you to confirm any facts that are important to you and affect any decisions you may make.

    The Cobb County School District is committed to parent, family and community engagement, and it is our hope that by providing this tool on our website that we are making our information more accessible to families whose first language is not English and thereby enabling better engagement in public education.
