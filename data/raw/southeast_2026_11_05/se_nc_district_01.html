Dual Immersion Program

A.C.E. Academy has expanded our academic program by incorporating a Spanish Dual Immersion class in Kindergarten and in 1st grade. One of the Kindergarten and 1st grade classes will be a Partial Dual Immersion Program that will primarily serve native English students. Students will participate in an environment where the targeted immersion language is used for a portion of the day. Content is delivered in the target language and in English.

The A.C.E. Academy Spanish Dual Immersion Classes will be for students attending lower school (K-4th Grade) Kindergarten was the first class introduced to dual immersion during the 2018-2019 and has expanded to 1st grade in the 2019-2020, 2nd grade 2020-2021, 3rd grade 2021-2022 and 4th grade in 2022-2023.

Parents of students enrolled in Kindergarten for the 2020-2021 school year interested in signing their child up for the Dual Immersion class must submit the required form by June 1, 2020. If we receive more interest forms than available seats, a lottery will be held in June to confirm student acceptance into the class.

Dual Immersion application forms will be emailed to parents directly. Please direct all questions to Kylese Hammond at k.hammond@aceacademycharter.org.
