Dual Language

What is Spanish Immersion?

Spanish Immersion is an exciting program where students are taught by a bilingual teacher for the majority of the school day in the Spanish Language, hence the term immersion. Students are immersed in the Spanish Language.

Spanish Immersion is also known as Dual Language Immersion (DLI) because students are taught in Spanish and English with English instruction beginning in 2nd grade.

Students learn to understand, read, and write in Spanish as well as English.

The Program begins in kindergarten or first grade and continues through middle school.

What is the objective of Spanish Immersion?

Dual Language/Immersion classes are an exciting option for parents who want their children to have the advantage of a rigorous academic program while also learning a second language. The language instituted in all of our dual language/immersion classes is Spanish. Across our nation and in North Carolina, language immersion programs have been thoroughly studied and present a comprehensive body of research that demonstrates significant benefits for our students.

What is the Spanish Immersion Curriculum?

  • All Spanish Immersion students are taught in the same standards, concepts and skills as other elementary students.

  • The pacing and sequencing will vary during the K-2 years.

  • Students learn from research-based materials that are specifically tailored to the Spanish language as well as English resources beginning in 2nd grade.

How do I know if this program is right for my student?

  • You want your child to have an edge in tomorrow's global economy.

  • You want your child to experience productive challenges in a diverse classroom setting.

  • You are willing to commit to 3 years in the program to see long-term benefits.

What are the benefits of Spanish Immersion?

  • Students will become bilingual and are proficient in two languages.

  • Students in DLI programs tend to score better than non-DLI peers on standardized tests.

  • Immersion students are comfortable interacting with students of other backgrounds due to exposure to other cultures.

  • DLI students have a positive sense of self.

  • DLI students will have more career advantages and opportunities.

Who is Eligible?

  • All students entering Kindergarten or First Grade

  • Students (grades 2 - 4) whose first language is Spanish and wish to transfer into a DLI class.  (per principal approval)

  • Students who live outside the school attendance zone are eligible to enroll in any of the three programs with an approved transfer and transportation provided by the parent/guardian

If you are interested in receiving more information about Dual Language / Immersion or enrolling in one of our programs, please contact the principal of the school.

What are the program locations?

Nash County Public Schools provides Dual Language/Immersion programs at the three school sites linked below:

  • Middlesex Elementary

  • Winstead Avenue Elementary

It’s also cool because you can learn Spanish dances and traditions.” - Malia Gupton

“It makes me feel special and like I belong with my classmates it makes life easier. And now i can say Hola como estas? Muy bien y tu. it is fun and all we speak in the class is Spanish !!!" - Lauren

“I like the class so I can communicate with my great-grandmother and my other family members. And I can learn it, the reason I learned it was because my parents didn’t learn Spanish. And it was a lifetime opportunity for me. And I like to learn about other Hispanic countries. My ancestors probably knew Spanish. I always wondered what would happen if I knew Spanish and that is why I learned Spanish.” - Victor Neives

 

“Puedo aprender el idioma que quiero aprender. También es para aprender nuevas cosas importantes que me pueden ayudar cuando a grande. También es para no olvidar mi idioma natal así aprendo inglés y español.” - Karen, Middlesex Elementary
