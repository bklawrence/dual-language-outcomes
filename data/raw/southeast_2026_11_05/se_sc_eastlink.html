Standards and Curriculum:

Exploring the world through language, culture and communication

East Link Academy is a Chinese-English language immersion public charter school committed to providing a rigorous academic education while developing bilingual and biliterate skills, cultural understanding, and the knowledge and dispositions students need to thrive in an increasingly interconnected world.

Our educational program integrates language learning, academic content, and cultural understanding throughout the student experience. Students are expected to build strong academic skills across all content areas while developing proficiency in English and Mandarin Chinese, along with curiosity, perseverance, empathy, respect, and an appreciation for diverse cultures and perspectives.

Will my student learn the same content as a traditional public school? YES. Yes. All students in South Carolina schools are instructed according to the same set of educational standards, also referred to as academic or learning standards. These standards establish specific learning objectives and provide clear guidance for teachers, students, parents, and the broader community. South Carolina’s educational standards comprehensively delineate the knowledge and skills students are expected to attain at each grade level and in every subject area to promote their academic success. SC Standards and Learning
​
What is the difference between standards and curriculum? Standards outline the knowledge and skills students should acquire, while curriculum refers to the instructional resources and the planned sequence teachers use to help students learn those standards.
​
Who is a global citizen? A global citizen:
• Feels connected to a growing “global community” and works actively to help create a better world
• Appreciates other cultures, valuing both ethnic and cultural diversity
• Recognizes that their actions affect others locally and globally
• Acts thoughtfully toward themselves, their society, their country, and the wider world
• Keeps a positive outlook and aims to be well-rounded and aware
• Embraces risks with optimism, working to make a difference in people's lives
• Shows the capacity to reflect, maintain hope, and take meaningful action
• Values ethical responsibility and has strong social awareness
