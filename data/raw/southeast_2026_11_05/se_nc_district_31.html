Northern High Student's Perspective on Hosting an International Student
Oct 20 2026

In August of 2025, Henry Cooper, a student at Northern High, welcomed a German exchange student into his life. A rocky start ended with a brotherhood and an experience neither student will forget.

"At the end of my sophomore year, I learned about the opportunity to host a foreign exchange student. A flyer was distributed around Guilford County Schools to advertise a 16-year-old German boy who needed a home for the upcoming year. The flyer showed a picture of Frederik (Freddy, as I would come to know him) on a podium at a swim meet.

I had no idea what I was getting myself into, but out of everyone in my family, I was the most eager to host Freddy. After a year without my older brother living at home, I thought having another person around would make things less lonely."

Choose and Enroll

School Registration

Choice School AppLication (Login to Avela)Opens October 9, at 12 p.m. - December 4, at 5 p.m.

Imagine what your child can do with the freedom to choose.

Choice empowers students and builds a sense of ownership. When students pursue an academic path that excites them, they become curious about the world around them, committed to their learning, engaged as leaders and collaborators, and ready to confidently meet the next challenge. At Guilford County Schools, we recognize that students have unique interests, goals, and learning needs. We’ll be there for your family along every phase of your student’s academic journey, with learning options that help them find their path and soar. 

No other school choice will give you the breadth of options and experiences you’ll find at GCS. Some schools have specialized themes, like STEM or the arts, while others serve neighborhoods — and every option gives students a strong foundation for the future. It’s all about what you and your child need and what kind of learning environment excites and supports them. Our offerings make GCS a national leader in educational choice and unlock every student’s potential with academic and extracurricular paths that build each student’s success.

What we mean by choice 

Every family has choices at GCS, whether your student is gifted, interested in a trade, homeschooled, loves a specific subject like music or robotics — and even if they want to fly! From PreK/Kindergarten through senior year, students have all kinds of paths open to them. Take time to learn about the many possibilities — from your outstanding neighborhood community school to exploring our Choice Programs and Schools, CTE programs, dual enrollment offerings and more. 

What are the main types of schools and programs available to me?

Get to know the different types of schools and paths we offer. Then, see what’s available for your child’s age and grade level.

Your Neighborhood School: the assigned school in your attendance zone

Choice Schools and Programs: Sometimes known as “magnet”, these are themed schools that give K-12 students the option of learning in an environment with a specific area of focus.

CTE Pathways, Signature Career Academies and Dual Enrollment (College and Career Promise): for high school students looking to excel in an in-demand field and/or earn college credits.
