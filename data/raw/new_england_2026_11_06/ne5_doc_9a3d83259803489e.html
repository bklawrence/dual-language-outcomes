Programa Bilingüe / Dual Language Program

Dual Language integrates native English and Spanish speakers, promoting academic achievement, language development, and cross-cultural understanding. Subjects are taught in both languages, helping students maintain their first language while acquiring a second language.
Participating Schools: Emerson Elementary and Silver Lake Elementary

El modelo de Lenguaje Dual integra a hablantes nativos de inglés y español, promoviendo el logro académico, el desarrollo de idiomas y la comprensión intercultural. Las asignaturas se enseñan en ambos idiomas, lo que ayuda a los estudiantes a mantener su primer idioma mientras adquieren un segundo idioma.
Escuelas participantes: Emerson Elementary y Silver Lake Elementary.

Benefits of being Bilingual:

  • Has research-based cognitive and academic advantages
  • Improves understanding of one’s native language
  • Enriches and enhances intellectual development
  • Opens doors to other cultures and fosters an understanding and appreciation for multicultural diversity
  • Encourages analytic and creative thinking
  • Develops higher order thinking skills Increases career opportunities

Language Allocation Update!

  • Beginning in 2026–27, the program will implement updated language allocations in grades 1–4, maintaining strong Spanish development alongside earlier and more consistent English instruction.
  • Aligned with proven Dual Language models: These adjustments continue to align with the standard 90/10 Dual Language model, providing students with earlier and more consistent English literacy instruction while continuing to strongly develop Spanish language skills. This approach supports multilingual learners and strengthens the path toward full bilingualism and biliteracy.
  • Informed by years of collaboration and feedback: This change reflects several years of program review and input from grade-level teams, school leaders, Dual Language stakeholders, and staff, who identified a need for earlier and more consistent English instruction to better support literacy development.
Current (2025-26) → New! (2026-27)

 K: 90/10 → 90/10

 1st: 90/10 → 80/20

 2nd: 80/20 → 70/30

 3rd: 70/30 →  60/40

 4th: 60/40 →  50/50

 5th: 50/50  → 50/50

Registration Information / Información para registraciones

  • English
  • Español

English

  • We encourage families of prospective applicants to attend an informational meeting to gain a clear understanding of the expectations and responsibilities before applying.
  • Applications are available electronically on the Dual Language website in Spanish and English.
  • Complete a new student enrollment form.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee a space in the Dual Language Program
  • Students on the waiting list will be placed into the Dual Language program as openings become available
  • Priority will be given to Emerson or Silver Lake families.
  • If your student is accepted into the Dual Language program but lives outside the boundary zone of Emerson or Silver Lake, transportation can be provided by the district. If you have questions, please contact the Transportation department at 425-385-4144.

Mission / Misión

  • English
  • Español

English

Inspire, educate, and prepare each student to achieve high standards, contribute to our community, and thrive in a global society. 
We believe, and research validates, that Two-Way Bilingual Immersion programs are consistent with these beliefs and will help us achieve our goals for Everett Public Schools students. 
We will:

  • Promote high academic achievement of all students and close the achievement gap
  • Achieve true bilingualism and biliteracy for participating students
  • Increase culturally responsive teaching throughout the school
  • Foster, promote, and sustain cultural diversity and respect among students and their families\
  • Promote a sense of unity throughout the neighborhood school

Español

lnspirar, educar y preparar a cada estudiante para que alcance altos estandares, contribuya con nuestra comunidad y prospere en una sociedad mundial. 
Creemos en el poder de los programas de Inmersión Bilingüe para: 

  • Promover un alto rendimiento académico y cerrar la brecha de desempeño
  • Alcanzar un verdadero bilingüismo y alfabetización bilingüe
  • Aumentar la enseñanza culturalmente sensible
  • Fomentar la diversidad cultural y el respeto dentro de la escuela y de la comunidad
  • Promover la unidad en la escuela del vecindario

Parent and School Partnership / Participatión de padres/escuela

  • English
  • Español

English

Parents are an integral part of the Dual Language program. The success of the students depends largely on the commitment and support of the parents involved. Initially, you will need to consider the following: 

  • At least a six-year commitment (K-5)
  • Regular school attendance by your child
  • Strong commitment to your child by being supportive and involved in your child's education

Español

Los padres de familia son parte integral del programa de dos idiomas. El éxito de los alumnos depende en gran parte de la entrega y el apoyo de los padres. Para empezar deben de tomar en cuenta lo siguiente:

  • Comprometerse por lo menos a 6 años (de Kínder a 5to grado)
  • Comprometerse con su estudiante dándole apoyo y participar en su educación
  • Asistir con regularidad a la escuela 

Lottery Selection / Seleccion por lotería

  • English
  • Español

English

Levels of boundary priority for the EPS Dual Language Lottery:

  • Attendance area students ​​
  • Attendance area siblings​​
  • Students residing in the district boundary, but outside the attendance area, whose siblings attend the school ​​
  • Students residing in the district boundary, but outside the attendance area​​
  • Students of full-time certificated or classified employees​

Español

Niveles de prioridad para la lotería del Programa de Doble Inmersión en las Escuelas Públicas de Everett:​

  • Estudiantes que residen en el área de asistencia​
  • Hermanos de estudiantes que residen en el área de asistencia​
  • Estudiantes que viven dentro de los límites del distrito, pero fuera del área de asistencia y que tienen hermanos que asisten a la escuela​
  • Estudiantes que viven dentro de los límites del distrito, pero no en el área de asistencia​
  • Hijos de empleados de tiempo completo, ya sean certificados o clasificados​
