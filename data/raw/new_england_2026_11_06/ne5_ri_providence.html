Dual Language Program

WHAT IS A DUAL LANGUAGE PROGRAM?

Providence Public Schools offers a One-Way and Two-Way Dual Language model enrolling students who are Spanish speakers and English speakers. Students receive instruction in English and in Spanish every day. Dual language Bilingual Education fosters bilingualism, biliteracy, cultural competence and high levels of academic achievement through instruction in two languages. 

Why Dual Language?

  • High proficiency in two languages
  • Increased performance on Standardized Test 
  • Better memory & attention control
  • High School Seal of Biliteracy

21st Century Skills

  • Prepared for the global community
  • A step ahead in the job market 
  • Developes greater cognitive 
  • flexibility and problem solving
  • College readiness 

Engagement

  • Increased understanding and 
  • appreciation of all cultures
  • Promotes higher self-esteem
  • Higher levels of engagement 
     

Where is the Dual Language Program Offered?

The Dual Language program is at the following schools:

Schools Grades
William D’Abate Elementary School K-5
Sackett Elementary School K-5
Young & Woods Elementary School K-3, 5
Spaziano Elementary School K-7
Leviton Dual Language School K-5
Lima Elementary School K-5
Roger Williams Middle School 7-8
Hope High School 9-10
