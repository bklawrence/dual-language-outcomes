Chelsea Public Schools - Caminos Dual Language Program

Caminos Dual Language Program

Celebrating Language. Empowering Culture.

About the Program

The Caminos Dual Language Program provides students with the opportunity to develop bilingualism, biliteracy, and multicultural competence in English and Spanish. Our program serves students from kindergarten through eighth grade, creating a pathway to global citizenship and academic excellence.

Preparing global citizens

Grades: 6 - 8

The 3 Pillars

Bilingualism & Biliteracy

Students develop proficiency in both English and Spanish, gaining the ability to read, write, speak, and understand in both languages at grade-appropriate levels.

Rigorous Grade-Level Academics

All instruction meets or exceeds grade-level standards, ensuring students achieve academic excellence while developing their bilingual abilities.

Sociocultural Competency

Students develop cross-cultural understanding, respect for diversity, and the skills to navigate and contribute to our multicultural world.

How to Join Caminos

Apply via Parent Information Center

Contact the PIC to learn about enrollment opportunities and application requirements.

Commit to K–8 Journey

Make a commitment to the full program experience from kindergarten through eighth grade.

Earn the Seal of Biliteracy

Graduate with official recognition of bilingual proficiency on your high school diploma.

Seal of Biliteracy

Students who complete the Caminos program earn the prestigious Seal of Biliteracy, which appears on their high school diploma and transcript. This recognition demonstrates their achievement in bilingual proficiency and multicultural competence, opening doors to college and career opportunities worldwide.

Ready to Begin Your Caminos Journey?

To learn more about the Caminos Dual Language Program, contact the Parent Information Center (PIC).
