A New Era: Dual Language from the Ground Up

At Sarah Roberts Elementary, we are proud to launch our Spanish Dual Language Immersion Program, which will begin in the 2025–2026 school year. This program is an exciting step toward honoring the languages, cultures, and identities that make our community so vibrant.

We will begin with our Kindergarten 1 (K1) students, who will learn in both English and Spanish each day. Every year after that, the program will grow to include the next grade level, eventually expanding through Grade 6. This thoughtful approach enables us to establish strong foundations for students as they progress through the program together.

Our Dual Language Program is about more than language learning. It is about belonging, pride, and access. We want every student to see their culture and language reflected in their learning, and to graduate confident in communicating in two languages.

At Sarah Roberts, we believe bilingualism and biliteracy are gifts. Our students will leave us ready to lead, connect, and succeed in a world that values diversity, empathy, and global understanding.

What is Dual Language (DL)?

Students learn in both English and Spanish. Classes include English learners and English-dominant students learning together.The goal: To prepare all students in K1-6th to develop a solid foundation of bilingual education as they work towards Massachusetts State Seal of Biliteracy at the end of high school (12th grade). 

Program Roll-Out Timeline

School Year Grades Offered
2025–2026 K1
2026–2027 K1, K2
2027–2028 K1–1st Grade
2028–2029 K1–2nd Grade
Continues yearly until 6th Grade is included by SY 2032–33

Language of Instruction

  • K1: 90% Spanish / 10% English | K2: 80% Spanish / 20% English
  • 1st Grade: 70% Spanish / 30% English | 2nd Grade: 60% Spanish/ 40% English 
  • Grades 3–6: 50% Spanish / 50% English

 This model helps students strengthen their Spanish early, then balance both languages as they grow.

Who Teaches?

  • All classrooms have bilingual certified teachers.
  • Each K1 class also has a bilingual paraprofessional.
  • Content teachers are trained to teach in Spanish and English.

Inclusive & Supportive Classrooms

  • Class sizes are small (20–22 students).
  • DL classrooms are inclusive, serving all learners including students with disabilities.
  • The district provides curriculum and resources in both languages.

What Will My Child Learn?

  • English Language Arts (ELA) & Spanish Language Arts (SLA): Benchmark, FOCUS, EL Curriculum
  • Math: Building Blocks K1 & Illustrative Mathematics K2-6 (Taught in English) (IM resources available in Spanish) 
  • Science: FossKits & OpenSciEd (being adapted for Spanish)—District Approved Curriculum adopted for Spanish
  • Specialist Classes (Music, Art, Dance, Physical Edu) taught in English. 
  • Daily phonics, reading, writing, and math activities in both languages.

Family Support & Involvement

  • Monthly Dual Language Family Workshops
  • Home-school partnerships to support bilingualism at home
  • Opportunities to share and celebrate family culture
