Hoboken Public School District
Dual Language Program Plan




Program Overview


Program Overview:  The Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program will offer English and Spanish speaking students an exciting, enriching, and challenging educational opportunity. The Dual Language Program, which will be operating in a total of five (5) Kindergarten classrooms across the district, is an educational model that provides content-based instruction to students in two languages. The goal is for students in the program to become proficient and literate in both English and Spanish, while also meeting high academic standards and succeeding in our multicultural society and global economy as a result of growing cross-cultural competencies. It is the intent of the Hoboken Public School District to expand this  program annually, reaching the entire K-8 population by 2031-2032.


Preliminary Actions


Preliminary Actions: The Hoboken Public School District began investigating Dual Language Programming during the summer of 2022 to see whether it could be a viable program option in the district. During this time, programs from near and far were studied. District personnel examined the requirements associated with teaching and certification, various instructional models, and curricula. Input was also gathered from our current bilingual staff members. In late February of 2023, the district released a transition survey to all PK 4 families. There were 480 responses to the survey. Data revealed that 352 families would be interested in having their child participate in a dual language classroom in the Hoboken Public School District, while 128 families would not be interested in participation. Based on this data, the Office of Curriculum and Instruction framed the original work, developed a preliminary plan, and arranged for two Information Sessions. These sessions were held on Monday, March 27th at 6:15 PM in the Wallace School Gymnasium and on Wednesday, March 29th at 6:15 PM via Google Meet. As a result of these sessions and questions gathered, the Office of Curriculum reworked the plan, shifting the instructional model from 90% delivery of instruction in Spanish and 10% delivery of instruction in English to an equal 50% delivery of instruction in Spanish and English during academic time. In addition, a number of dedicated seats will be allocated for English Language Learners and higher weighting will be given to low income students in the lottery. On Monday, April 17th, a final notice was sent to all PK 4 families, including a program plan/comprehensive FAQ along with a form to participate in the lottery. The intent to participate in the lottery is due no later than Monday, April 24th at 3:00 PM. If needed, the lottery is scheduled for Friday, April 28th at 9 AM.


Implementation Plan


Implementation Plan: During the Months of May and June, the Hoboken Public School District’s Office of Curriculum will be translating Mathematics, English Language Arts, Social Studies, and the Individualized Learning Period curricula into Spanish and publishing each document on the website. Simultaneously, the Assistant Superintendent will ensure that the paired Spanish version of all instructional materials are ordered. The Office of Curriculum will update its Three-Year English Language Learner Plan, and also create a comprehensive instructional guidance document and a professional development plan to support the implementation of the Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program. Professional development will be provided by Montclair State University via their Bilingual/Bicultural Certificate Program, the Center for Teaching Biliteracy, and the Center for Applied Linguistics. Prior to the end of the school year, the Human Resources Office will participate in two job fairs to recruit bilingual teachers on March 30th and on April 19th. Currently, the district’s plan is to employ two bilingual educators who are already on staff, and hire three new bilingual teachers. These three positions are not newly created. Rather they are positions that will become unoccupied at the end of the school year. 


Dual Language Model


Dual Language Model:  The Dual Language Model that the Hoboken Hoboken Public School District will use is the 50/50 model. Students in the 50/50 model receive 50 percent of instruction in English and 50 percent in the partner language. The Hoboken Public School District intends to achieve this by splitting up the day’s academic subjects, teaching 50 percent of them in Spanish and 50 percent in English. It also intends to employ this model simultaneously, by delivering reading instruction in both languages. 


Support Systems


Academic Support: The academic programs and supports that students enjoy in the traditional classroom will be available to those in the Dual Language Program. For example, students with an IEP will be serviced per their individualized plan. Services will be equally available and determined by the Child Study Team. 504 and Individualized health plan accommodations will be implemented as per the student’s needs. Dual LanguageProgram teachers will utilize differentiated strategies throughout the school day and during the individualized learning period, just as teachers in the traditional classroom. A bilingual reading specialist is available for extended reading support, as well. A partnership and ongoing dialogue between parents, the principal, the school counselor, and other service providers will be critical in determining the best possible support systems or learning environment for all students. If, collaboratively, a parent and the school finds that a student is not thriving academically in the district’s Dual Language  Program, the student may simply be transferred into a traditional classroom in the same school. There is no need to transfer out of the school or district. 


Family Support: The Hoboken Public School District will support the family along this Dual Language Program journey. While it may seem daunting for non-Spanish speaking parents to support children in a dual language program, the district will use a multitude of vehicles to communicate with families and provide the same level of support to those parents of children in our Dual Language Program as we do in traditional classroom settings. 


Selection Process


Criteria: The Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program is open to all enrolled, rising Kindergarten students who reside in Hoboken. 


Lottery:  There are a total of 36 seats available in both the Brandt and Wallace Elementary Schools, and 18 seats available in the Connors Elementary School via lottery.  Low income families will be weighed doubly in the lottery. The current lottery system may be altered as this Dual Language Program extends to additional grade levels in the years to come. Brandt and Wallace Elementary Schools will allocate 4 non-lottery seats each, and Connors Elementary School will allocate 2 non-lottery seats to Spanish Speaking ELL learners, per the native test. On Monday, April 17th, all families enrolled in our Kindergarten program for September will receive a Google Form including a link to the Dual Language Program Plan/FAQ Document. After reviewing the plan, families interested in participating in the lottery system will submit their intent no later than Monday, April 24th at 3:00 PM. Those submitting an intent to participate in the lottery will receive a lottery number. The Hoboken Public School District’s lottery for each elementary school will be held on Friday, April 28th at 9 AM. The lottery will be accessible via Google Meet for those participating. The Hoboken Public School District will post the results on the district’s website. Those that do not receive a seat via the lottery process will remain on the pending placement list in the order in which their number was drawn. These students will remain there until the family chooses to remove them from the list or a seat is provided at some point in Kindergarten or 1st Grade. At this time, it is the intent of the Hoboken Public School District to only admit students into this program during Kindergarten and 1st Grade (when the program expands). The district will not admit any students other than Spanish Speaking English Language learners in grade levels above 1st Grade. 


Frequently Asked Questions:
* When will the Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program Begin?  The district’s Dual Language Program will begin in September 2023.


* What will the target language of the Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program be?  The district’s Dual Language Program will be Spanish. 


* Which grade level(s) will the district’s Dual Language Program service?  The district’s Dual Language Program will begin with Kindergarten.


* How many classes will the Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program be implemented in during the first year of implementation?  The district’s Dual Language Program will start in two Kindergarten classrooms at Brandt Elementary School, two Kindergarten classrooms at Wallace Elementary School, and one Kindergarten classroom at Connors Elementary School. 


* Is Kindergarten the only opportunity for students to enter Hoboken’s Dual Language Program? In the first year, the Hoboken Public School District is only operating the Dual Language Program in Kindergarten. In the second year, the program will operate at both the Kindergarten and 1st Grade levels. If there are seats available when the program transitions to 1st Grade, the district will draw from the pending placement list generated from this inaugural year. The district will not admit new enrollees to the program beyond 1st Grade, unless a student is a Spanish speaking English Language Learner. 


* If my family is selected in the Dual Language Lottery but I requested a transfer to another school and it is later approved, what will that process look like for us? If you are selected in the Dual Language Lottery and have your transfer to another school approved, you will be added to the pending placement list of the new school. We must be respectful of those that have already been selected or added to the pending placement list of the approved school. These lists are shared on the website for full transparency in this process. School transfers are aligned to the school location and not to a specialized program like Dual Language.


* How many Kindergarten Dual Language Program seats will the Hoboken Public School District offer in September 2023?  Each Kindergarten Dual Language Program classroom will have a total of 18 seats drawn via lottery, plus three allocated seats for Spanish Speaking English Language Learners as per the ACCESS Test. 


* Who will Supervise the Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program? The Assistant Superintendent of Schools will supervise the district’s Dual Language Program. Questions may be directed to Ms. Sandra Rodriguez-Gomez at srodriguez@hoboken.k12.nj.us. 


* Will Dual Language teachers need to hold a specific certification? Teachers in the district’s Dual Language Program must hold a P3, a K-6, or an N-8 New Jersey Teacher Certification. In addition, teachers in this program must hold proof of bilingual proficiency by taking and passing the Oral Proficiency Interview (OPI) and the Writing Proficiency Test (WPT) both offered through the American Council on the Teaching of Foreign Languages (ACTFL). Finally, these teachers will also enroll in Montclair State University’s Bilingual/Bicultural Certificate Program and participate in professional learning before, during, and after the academic year to continue to grow and refine their practice. This is the same professional development model enjoyed by our teachers in traditional classrooms.
