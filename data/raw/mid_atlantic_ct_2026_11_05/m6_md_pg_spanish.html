Spanish Immersion

AT-A-GLANCE

GRADES SERVED: K-12

LOCATIONS (view disclaimer): 

  • Dual 50/50 Spanish Immersion- Cesar Chavez Dual Spanish Immersion School
  • Maintenance Bilingual- Cool Spring Elementary School (Spanish)
  • Full Spanish Immersion - Overlook Full Spanish Immersion School, Phyllis E. Williams Spanish Immersion School
  • Partial Immersion- Capitol Heights Elementary School (Spanish), Largo High School
  • Spanish Multiple Pathways- Kettering Middle School

Specialty Program locations are determined based on district needs and may be relocated as those needs evolve.

TRANSPORTATION: Transportation provided.

Please note that while PGCPS provides bus transportation to and from all Specialty Programs, routes for our lottery programs (Cesar Chavez, Overlook, and Phyllis E. Williams), as well as Kettering Middle School and Largo High School cover larger geographic areas. Families should plan for commute times of approximately 90 minutes each way.

PROGRAM DESCRIPTION

Students learn Spanish, while learning the same grade level material as other PGCPS students.   Immersion Programs schools prepare students to bilingual, biliterate, and bi-cultural.  Many of our graduates have earned the Maryland State Seal of Biliteracy on their diploma.

Program Features

  • Many students qualify for the Maryland State Seal of Biliteracy
  • Immersion Programs schools prepare students to be college and career ready by learning another language and learning about another culture.
  • Middle School students can choose the pathway that best supports their current language proficiency

Video: Learn more about our Language Immersion Programs

Choose this program if your child...

  • Shows interest in learning another language
  • You would like your child to learn another language

APPLICATION INFO

Who can apply

Students can apply for our lottery programs the school year before they will be Kindergarteners.  Students can test in for any available spaces in Grades 1 and above for our lottery programs.

When to apply

  • Kindergarten lottery applications are accepted during the lottery window. Lottery information can be found here.
  • The application for testing into the program for Grades 1 and above opens in October and closes in March for the next school year. 

Access Grades 1 and above testing into the Spanish Immersion Program
