Mission & Vision

RMMS is a community that is committed to empowering students to become active, compassionate, lifelong learners. We work with families as well as local and global communities to implement challenging interdisciplinary programs of international education. We develop students that are respectful, inquiring, knowledgeable, and reflective change agents who are empowered to create a sustainable planet and a more just and peaceful world. 

Tools for Success

By The Numbers

Grade 1 - 6th

Magnet School

Regional Multicultural Magnet School (RMMS) is expanding!  Currently a grade 1-6 magnet school, RMMS will add grade 7 for the 2026-2027 school year.  Over the next three years, RMMS will transition to a full grades 3-8 school!

500

Students

RMMS was established in 1992 to offer the diverse student population of our region an innovative program demonstrating best educational practices within a multicultural curriculum.

20:1

Student to teacher ratio

Along with one class per grade in the Puentes program

120

Puentes program students

Puentes is a highly rigorous dual language program where children learn to read, write, and converse in English and Spanish by studying literacy, science, social studies and math in both languages.
