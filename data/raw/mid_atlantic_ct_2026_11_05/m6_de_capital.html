Spanish Immersion

About the Capital School District Spanish Immersion Program

Capital School District's Dual Language Spanish Immersion Program provides students with the opportunity to become bilingual, biliterate, and culturally competent learners.

Beginning in Kindergarten and continuing through high school, students develop Spanish language proficiency while learning academic content in both English and Spanish.

Through meaningful learning experiences, students strengthen their communication, critical thinking, and academic skills while developing an appreciation for diverse cultures and perspectives.

Our goal is to prepare confident, globally minded students who can communicate effectively in two languages and succeed in an increasingly interconnected world.

Schools Offering Spanish Immersion

Our current K–12 program includes:

  • Kindergarten: East Dover ECC and Fairview ECC

  • Grades 1–5: Central Elementary and South Dover Elementary

  • Grades 6–8: Middle School of Excellence and Middle School of Innovation

  • Grades 9–12: Dover High School 

Language Proficiency and Student Growth

Our goal is to help students improve their listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills in Spanish. We also use assessments such as STAMP to monitor their progress and identify areas where they need additional support.

Student Activities and Cultural Experiences

Throughout the school year, students participate in activities such as Hispanic Heritage Month celebrations, cultural field trips, cultural projects, and Spanish Immersion Showcases. These experiences give students opportunities to use their Spanish skills, explore different cultures, and share what they have learned.

What are the Benefits of Spanish Immersion?

Students develop communication skills in two languages, learn about different cultures, and strengthen their academic skills. Being bilingual can also provide more opportunities for students in college and their future careers.

Academic benefits include:

  • Achieving high levels of language proficiency in the immersion language
  • Satisfying the World Language graduation requirement by the time students leave eighth grade
  • Performing as well as, or better than, their non-immersion peers on standardized tests of English and mathematics, based on research

Cognitive benefits include:

  • Developing greater cognitive flexibility
  • Demonstrating increased attention control
  • Developing better memory and superior problem-solving skills
  • Enhancing understanding of their native language

Economic benefits include:

  • Being better prepared for the global community and job markets where world language skills provide an economic advantage
  • Having the opportunity to complete college-level coursework in languages while still in high school

Sociocultural benefits include:

  • Becoming more aware of and showing a more positive attitude toward different cultures
  • Demonstrating a greater appreciation of people who have different cultural perspectives and practices

How do I enroll my student in Spanish Immersion? Who can participate?

Who can participate?

The Spanish Immersion Program is open to all rising kindergarten students. Students will be selected based on the following guidelines:

  • Forty-eight students will be selected for each kindergarten Spanish Immersion Program at both Fairview Elementary School and South Dover Elementary School.
  • A minimum of 30 students will be selected from the school’s attendance area.
  • Up to 18 students will be selected through the School Choice process.
  • The cohort of students will generally reflect the demographics of Capital School District.
  • Students will be equitably chosen based on gender and ethnicity.
  • While the immersion program is open to all kindergarten students, academic screening will be used to determine students’ potential success in learning a second language and/or eligibility.

Are IEP students included?

Yes. The Spanish Immersion Program is open to any interested rising kindergarten student.

Is this a Gifted Program?

No. The Spanish Immersion Program is not a gifted program and is open to any interested rising kindergarten student.

Enrolling in the program:

Families interested in enrolling their child in the Spanish Immersion Program should begin by registering their child for Kindergarten at their assigned home school. Children must be five years old on or before August 31 of the school year they plan to attend.

The Spanish Immersion Program uses a lottery-based application process. Families may also need to complete a School Choice application depending on their school attendance zone or district of residence.

Parents are encouraged to review the application requirements, important deadlines, and available school locations before applying.

For additional information, please visit:

  • Capital School District Enrollment and Registration

  • Capital School District School Choice Information

Families may also contact their child's school for assistance with the enrollment process.

How are Kindergarten Students selected for the Program?

Parents of incoming kindergarten students are invited to attend an informational meeting held at each school immersion site in the winter or early spring. During this meeting, the Spanish Immersion Program is introduced.

Application information is available at the informational meeting, in the school offices, and at the district office.

If more students apply than the program can accommodate, a lottery is held for the available spots in the class.

How does the Lottery Work?

If more students apply than the Spanish Immersion Program can accommodate, the curriculum office will hold a student selection lottery during the first week of May.

The results will be forwarded to the Capital School District Board of Education for review at its May meeting, and parents will be notified shortly afterward.

Students will be formally accepted into the Spanish Immersion Program once their parents or guardians sign the Family Commitment Form. Parents and guardians who do not sign the commitment form will relinquish their child’s eligibility. The student will then be assigned to a typical kindergarten classroom and replaced with another student of similar demographics who expressed interest in the Spanish Immersion Program.

Students who are not selected through the lottery process will be placed on a waiting list.

How does Spanish Immersion Work in the Classroom?

Students learn different academic subjects in both English and Spanish. Teachers work together to help students make connections between the two languages while learning grade-level content. Students in the Spanish Immersion Program are taught using the same curricula as their peers in grades K-5. The same standards and performance expectations are targeted.

On a typical day in the Spanish Immersion Program in grades K-5, half of the day is taught in Spanish and the other half is taught in English.

During the Spanish half of the day, students are taught:

  • Math, 45-60 minutes
  • Science and/or Social Studies, 45-60 minutes
  • Spanish literacy, 30-45 minutes

During the English half of the day, students are taught:

  • English Language Arts, 120 minutes
  • Math and Content Area Reinforcement in English, 20-30 minutes

At each grade from kindergarten through grade 5, the Spanish Immersion Program consists of certified teachers, including a Spanish-speaking teacher and an English-speaking teacher.

Some Spanish-speaking teachers have been educated in local universities and have graduated with an elementary education and language degree. Others may be international guest teachers from other countries. These teachers have been hand-selected by their respective governments to come to Delaware to teach in our schools.

What Happens After Kindergarten?

Schools Providing Spanish Immersion:

Our current K–12 program includes:

  • Kindergarten: East Dover ECC and Fairview ECC

  • Grades 1–5: Central Elementary and South Dover Elementary

  • Grades 6–8: Middle School of Excellence and Middle School of Innovation

  • Grades 9–12: Dover High School 

High School Spanish Immersion Pathway (9-12)

At Dover High School, students have opportunities to continue developing their Spanish language proficiency through advanced coursework, including AP Spanish Language and Culture.

The Delaware Bridge Program provides an opportunity for eligible students to continue studying Spanish beyond the AP level while earning both high school and college credits. Through this program, students can further develop their language skills, explore Hispanic cultures, and prepare for future college and career opportunities.

 

 

Is there a Cost to Participate in Spanish Immersion?

The Spanish Immersion Program is free to all students. Materials, supplies, equipment, technology, and other classroom resources are free, just as they are in other regular school programs.

Field trips and other co-curricular academic or cultural opportunities may require a fee.

How is the Spanish Immersion Program Funded?

The district funds the Spanish Immersion Program as it funds all classrooms, including paying the teacher’s salary and providing classroom space.

The Spanish Immersion Program receives the same funding for supplies, books, and other materials as other classrooms in the district.

The Delaware Department of Education provides additional funds yearly to each school with a Spanish Immersion Program. These funds can be used to purchase textbooks, classroom supplies, or other curricular needs.

Is transportation provided for students in the Spanish Immersion Program?

Capital School District will provide transportation only to students who live in the attendance zone of the school they attend.
