Culture is Celebrated, and Language is a Superpower

Welcome to the Sonia Sotomayor Dual Language Immersion Academy, a premier theme-based school within the Allentown School District! Named after the first Latina Supreme Court Justice, our academy is a leading bilingual school in Pennsylvania where culture is celebrated, and language is a superpower.

Our Mission for Bilingual Excellence

We provide a rigorous, inclusive environment where scholars achieve academic excellence in Spanish and English. Our goal is to foster bilingualism, biliteracy, and cross-cultural competence, preparing students to thrive in a global society.

Why Choose Our Dual Language Program?

  • Biculturalism: We integrate the history and values of the Spanish-speaking world into our elementary curriculum.
  • Family Engagement: From our "Lions Voice" parent group to adult Spanish classes for parents, we build a strong school community.
  • Academic Growth: Our students consistently demonstrate high literacy growth, proving the cognitive benefits of dual language immersion.
