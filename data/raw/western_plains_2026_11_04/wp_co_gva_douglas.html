Bilingual Brilliance Starts Here!

Now Enrolling for 2026–27 at Global Village Academy Douglas County

Where your child learns to think, speak, and succeed in more than one language.

 
Looking for a top-rated bilingual K–8 school in Parker, Colorado? Global Village Academy Douglas County is a tuition-free K–8 public charter school where students learn in English and a second language every day, building fluency, strong academics, and real-world skills for the future.
 
Our language immersion program helps students excel in reading, writing, and math while developing confidence, critical thinking, and cross-cultural understanding.
 
Families choose Global Village Academy Douglas County for its proven results, supportive community, and focus on college and career readiness.
 
Enrollment for the 2026–27 school year is now open, and space is limited.
