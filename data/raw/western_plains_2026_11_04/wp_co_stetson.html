OWL Dual Language Immersion

OWL Inmersión Bilingüe

Stetson Elementary will be discontinuing the Dual Language Immersion (DLI) Program. The program will conclude at the end of the 2025 - 2026 school year. As part of this transition, we will no longer be accepting interest forms or enrollment requests for the program.
