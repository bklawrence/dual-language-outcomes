Bilingual Brilliance Starts Here!

Global Village Academy Aurora

Where your child learns to think, speak, and succeed in more than one language.

Looking for a top-rated bilingual K–8 school in Aurora, Colorado? Global Village Academy Aurora is a tuition-free K–8 public charter school where students learn in English and a second language every day, building fluency, strong academics, and real-world skills for the future.
 
 
Our language immersion program helps students excel in reading, writing, and math while developing confidence, critical thinking, and cross-cultural understanding.
 
Families choose Global Village Academy Aurora for its proven results, supportive community, and focus on college and career readiness.
