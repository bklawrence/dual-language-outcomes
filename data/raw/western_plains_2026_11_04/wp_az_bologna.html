Spanish Dual Language Immersion

About Spanish DLI at CUSD

Chandler Unified School District is proud to offer dual language immersion (DLI) programs in Spanish at Bologna.

We implement a 50/50 research-based model in elementary schools, a 70/30 model in middle/junior high schools and a world languages continuation in high school. Students have the opportunity to start at an early age learning a new language while learning grade level content.

Students participating in the program will become bilingual, bi-literate, develop cultural awareness, and global citizenship skills. 

How to Register Your Child

For Kindergarten through 5th grade

  1. Please complete this form for the DLI school of your choice. A staff member from the school will reach out to you to follow up.

2. If residing outside of Bologna Elementary School boundaries, please complete an Open Enrollment Application.

Resources for Practicing Spanish at Home

  • Allegro Mágico

Podcast on classical music for children and families, with which you can listen and learn about composers and classical musical pieces in a fun and entertaining way.

 

  • Bartolo

Games, stories, and videos that supports Spanish learning.

 

  • BrainPOP Español

English is also available.

 

  • Calico Learning

Calico provides children with quality Spanish learning experiences through songs and videos.

 

  • Duolingo

Over 200 million people around the world use Duolingo to help them learn languages. The fun, game-like lessons keep students motivated and excited about language.

 

  • Epic!

Epic! is a free digital library where kids can find books, videos, and quizzes in both English and Spanish.

 

  • PBS Learning Media

Explore the Spanish language and Spanish language culture with videos, audio clips, and more. Reinforce Spanish vocabulary and grammar with sixty full lessons from the ¡Arte y más! collection.

 

  • Pipo Club

At Pipoclub you will find the most successful children's educational games, created for children around the world. The games are available online, as downloads, or using Apps. Most games can be used without registration.

 

  • The Spanish Experiment

Well-known children's stories translated into Spanish and spoken by a native Spanish speaker. Great for kids... and adults too! Read along in Spanish or English.
