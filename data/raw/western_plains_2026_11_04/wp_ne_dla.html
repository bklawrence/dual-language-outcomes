Discover More About DLA

¡Somos Familia!
We are Family!

The Dual Language Academy (DLA) provides a rigorous bilingual and multicultural education that nurtures faith and academic excellence. Our mission is to help children grow into confident, capable future leaders, using their gifts to serve God and others.

Starting as early as age 3 in preschool, your child will experience an immersive Spanish dual-language program designed to build strong academic skills while fostering cultural awareness. Since opening in Fall 2018, we’ve continued to grow, currently offering preschool through 7th grade, which will expand to include eighth grade in 2026-2027. 

As part of the Omaha Catholic School Consortium, our school thrives within a close-knit, faith-driven community that provides students and families with the support needed for success. Come join us and experience the lifelong benefits of bilingual Catholic education!

**The DLA Preschool and Kindergarten classes for the 2026-2027 school year are currently FULL.  We appreciate your interest and we encourage you to still complete the inquiry form so we can learn more about your student and stay in touch should any openings arise.  Please also consider exploring our other Consortium schools for enrollment.

A MESSAGE FROM OUR PRINCIPAL

Dear Families,

I’m excited that you are interested in learning more about the Dual Language Academy. It is our desire to shape confident, compassionate leaders who serve God and others. We do this through a bilingual curriculum that helps students develop into biliterate learners and prepares them for success in a changing world. Students also have many opportunities to develop their relationship with Christ through school Masses and other celebrations of the Catholic faith. 

Our school community is unique, that’s for sure. I invite you to explore all that the DLA has to offer. Contact us to tour anytime and see firsthand how our approach helps students reach their fullest potential. We look forward to welcoming your family into our growing community!

Angie Smith, Principal

PARISH PARTNERSHIP

We are grateful for the support of the priests and parishioners at our Consortium partner parishes who support the Consortium schools. Students here have the benefit of learning from faith role models and engaging in other faith-based activities throughout the school year. Registered parishioners are also eligible to receive a $500 tuition discount.
