  • Dual Language Immersion

Chandler Unified School District is proud to offer dual language immersion (DLI) programs in Spanish and Mandarin Chinese. These programs of choice are currently offered at four elementary schools and two junior high schools.  We implement a 50/50 research-based model in elementary schools, a 70/30 model in middle/junior high schools and a world languages continuation in high school.  Students have the opportunity to start at an early age learning a new language while learning grade level content.

CUSD Program Goals

Students participating in the program will become:

  • Bilingual
  • Bi-literate
  • Develop cultural awareness
  • Global citizenship skills

How to Register Your Child

  1. For K-6, please complete this form for the DLI school of your choice. A staff member from the school will reach out to you to follow up.
  2. If residing outside of your CUSD school's boundaries, please complete an Open Enrollment Application.
  3. For Preschool and Pre-K Dual Language Exploration programs, start here.
