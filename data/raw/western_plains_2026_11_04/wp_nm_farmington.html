Announcements & Events

FMS Is Building Something Big for Skilled Trades

FMS families and students, here's a first look at something we're really excited about: the Construction Skilled-Trades Lab coming to our technical center.

This new 4,000-square-foot facility will be built on the site of the former PreK West building and is set to open by Fall 2027. The equipment for the Lab has already been purchased and is on site at FMS, ready for installation once construction is complete.

FMS Spelling Bee Crowns a Champion After 26 Rounds

The winning word in this morning’s FMS Spelling Bee was “ototoxic,” which means “poisonous to the ear.”

Hispanic Heritage Month Closes with a Joyful Night of Música y Tradiciones

We closed Hispanic Heritage Month in style last night with a wonderful evening of música y tradiciones at Esperanza Elementary. A packed house of families, friends, and community members filled the room with energy and pride as students from every grade level took the stage.

Esperanza Inducts 13 Students into Equity Council

This week, Esperanza Elementary welcomed parents and families to a special celebration honoring thirteen students inducted into the school’s Equity Council. The ceremony, complete with pins, cookies, and refreshments, gave families a chance to see firsthand the importance of the council and the meaningful work this student-led group is doing for their peers.

Walk & Roll to School Day Is a Reminder to Drive Carefully Near Schools

This morning, students and staff at Mesa Verde Elementary joined communities across the country in celebrating National Walk & Roll to School Day along College Boulevard. The annual event encourages students to build healthy habits that support physical health, mental alertness, and traffic safety, especially in busy areas near our schools. Go, Wildcats!

Bilingual Programs

FMS Bilingual Multicultural Education Program provides support and resources for all students to become bilingual and biliterate in English and a second language, following rules and regulations as per the New Mexico State Bilingual Multicultural Education Act, Chapter 22, Article 23 NMSA.

FMS offers BME services in Spanish and Navajo languages while emphasizing the history and cultures associated with the students' home or heritage language. The district bilingual teachers are specialized in elementary or secondary education and have received specialized training in bilingual education, holding a Bilingual endorsement.

Bilingual Multicultural Education programs are offered at the following school sites:

McCormick Elementary

  • Spanish Dual Language K, 1, 2
  • Spanish Language and Culture pull-out K-5
  • Navajo Language and Culture pull-out K-5

Animas Elementary

  • Spanish Language and Culture pull-out K-5

Bluffview Elementary

  • Spanish Language and Culture pull-out K-5

Apache Elementary

  • Navajo Language and Culture Dual Language K, 1
  • Navajo Language and Culture pull-out K-5

Esperanza Elementary

  • Spanish Language and Culture pull-out K-5
  • Navajo Language and Culture pull-out K-5

Country Club Elementary

  • Spanish Language and Culture pull-out K-5

Heights Middle School

  • Spanish Language and Culture
  • Navajo Language and Culture

Hermosa Middle School

  • Spanish Language and Culture
  • Navajo Language and Culture

Tibbetts Middle School

  • Spanish Language and Culture
  • Navajo Language and Culture

Mesa View Middle School

  • Spanish Language and Culture
  • Navajo Language and Culture

Farmington High School

  • Spanish Language and Culture
  • Navajo Language and Culture

Piedra Vista High School

  • Spanish Language and Culture
  • Navajo Language and Culture

For more information regarding Farmington Municipal School District’s BMEP services, please contact your child’s principal or the Language & Culture Department.
