About Us

Welcome to Columbine Elementary School in Fort Morgan, Colorado, serving grades KG-5.

Columbine Elementary is home to about 217 students and about 43 staff members. Each student belongs to one of 4 Houses : Adjudante (red), Cumplidor (green), Fide'le (yellow), or Pembaru (purple). Our mascot is the cougar, and our motto is "cougar strong."
