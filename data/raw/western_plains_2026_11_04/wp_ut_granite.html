Dual Language Immersion Schools

These are the schools that currently have Dual Language Immersion (DLI) programs.  Most programs begins in first grade and continue through graduation. In the new Spanish DLI Academy Magnet School, students can begin Dual Immersion in Kindergarten. For more information regarding the application process, please see the page Dual Language Immersion Application Process.

Students in grades K-6 for the current 2025-2026 school year who are interested in applying, can contact the school directly to see if there is space available.  Student in grades 3-6 must already speak the language at a level equal to their DLI peers in order to enter the program at that point.
