Dual Language Immersion

Learn to speak, read and write in two languages!

Scottsdale Unified School District is proud to provide students an opportunity to become multilingual through the Dual Language Immersion (DLI) programs. Dual Language Immersion capitalizes on the human brain’s natural development of language during critical periods, especially during early childhood. Children naturally acquire language through social interactions with meaningful environments.

The DLI program is designed to use the language strengths of two teachers to co-teach curriculum in their target languages.

Students spend half of their time in English learning reading, writing and content skills such as social studies.  The other half of their instructional time is spent learning content such as mathematics and science through target languages such as Spanish or Mandarin as taught by a native speaker.

The DLI program at Pueblo Elementary targets Spanish and Desert Canyon Elementary School’s DLI program targets Mandarin.  Each school has teacher teams that coordinate instruction so students master the grade level standards while integrating the language arts skills of the target languages.

Spanish Immersion Program

Pathway for Language Learning: Pre-K–12th Grade
Pueblo: Pre-K–5 Mohave: 6–8 Saguaro: 9–12

 

Grade Spanish Immersion Pathway
Pre-K Exposure
K–3 50% of daily instruction is conducted in Spanish – Math, Science, Spanish Language Arts
4–5 50% of daily instruction is conducted in Spanish – Social Studies, Science, Spanish Language Arts
6th 40% of daily instruction (2 class periods) is conducted in Spanish – Social Studies, Science
7th 40% of daily instruction (2 class periods) is conducted in Spanish – Social Studies, I HS course
8th 40% of daily instruction (2 class periods) is conducted in Spanish – Science, Spanish Language
9th Honors Spanish III – Optional college credit as staffing allows
10th Honors Spanish IV – Optional college credit as staffing allows
11th AP Spanish Language & Culture
12th Spanish Capstone Course (Advanced Low/Mid Proficiency Target)

Requirements to Receive the DLI Cord at Graduation

Dual Language Immersion Distinction earned through continuous participation K-12

  1. Begins immersion study by third grade at Pueblo Elementary School
  2. Continues in middle school and matriculates from the Mohave MS program
  3. Successfully completes the SUSD DLI High School WL trajectory – Substitution courses will not be accepted. (courses listed above)

Student who demonstrate proficiency in a language may qualify for direct entry into DLI program by taking a placement test.

Mandarin Immersion Program

Pathway for Language Learning: Pre-K–12th Grade
DCES: Pre-K–5 DCMS: 6–8 DMHS: 9–12

 

Grade Mandarin Immersion Pathway
Pre-K Exposure
K–3 50% of daily instruction is conducted in Mandarin – Math, Science, Mandarin Language Arts
4–5 50% of daily instruction is conducted in Mandarin – Social Studies, Science, Mandarin Language Arts
6th 40% of daily instruction (2 class periods) is conducted in Mandarin – Science, Mandarin IA
7th 40% of daily instruction (2 class periods) is conducted in Mandarin – Science, Mandarin IB
8th 40% of daily instruction (2 class periods) is conducted in Mandarin – Science, Mandarin Chinese II HS
9th Honors Mandarin Chinese III Pre-Diploma – Optional college credit as staffing allows
10th Honors Mandarin Chinese IV-/IB SL – Optional college credit as staffing allows
11th AP Chinese Language & Culture
12th Mandarin Chinese Capstone Course (Advanced Low/Mid Proficiency Target)

Requirements to Receive the DLI Cord at Graduation

Dual Language Immersion Distinction earned through continuous participation K-12

  1. Begins immersion study by third grade at Desert Canyon Elementary School
  2. Continues in middle school and matriculates from the DCMS program
  3. Successfully completes the SUSD DLI High School WL trajectory – Substitution courses will not be accepted. (courses listed above)

Student who demonstrate proficiency in a language may qualify for direct entry into DLI program by taking a placement test.

Desert Canyon Middle School

Middle school students in the Dual Language Immersion program will receive two periods a day in taught in Mandarin.

DESERT CANYON MIDDLE SCHOOL

40% of the academic school day will be conducted in Mandarin.  The two courses are:

6th Grade: Science and Mandarin 1A
7th Grade: Science and Mandarin 1B
8th Grade: Science and Mandarin II

Dual Language Immersion students are eligible for the Middle School Pathway to Biliteracy Award
