Mandarin Immersion Program

PVSchools students in our Mandarin Immersion classrooms, are fully immersed in the rich language and culture of China.
 
The structured school day is thoughtfully designed to ensure that students spend 50% of their time learning subjects in English and the other half of the instructional day learning subjects in Mandarin.
 
Mandarin Chinese prepares children to be globally competitive and ready for 21st-century success. It is spoken by one-sixth of the world's population in one of the world's strongest economies. The US State Department has identified it as one of 13 languages in high demand.

Elementary School

At Whispering Wind Academy

The Mandarin Chinese immersion program at Whispering Wind Academy is open to children entering Kindergarten to 6th grade.  

Kindergarten through 4th grade students receive a well-rounded education in Math and Science with a strong focus on developing their literacy skills in Mandarin. Additionally, they learn Reading, Writing, and Social Studies in English. As they enter 5th grade, students will continue to develop their Mandarin literacy skills while studying Science and Social Studies. For Math, they switch to English instruction.

Visit Whispering Wind Academy Mandarin Website

Middle School

At Sunrise Middle School

The Mandarin Immersion program at Sunrise Middle School offers two engaging courses taught entirely in Mandarin.

The Mandarin Language Arts course not only meets world language standards, but also emphasizes communication, culture, and community to help students master the language. Students learn Mandarin in a holistic way that integrates culture into the instruction process.

The second course, Chinese Culture, it takes students on a captivating journey exploring various themes, from holidays and festivals to the intriguing history of China's great figures and inventors. Students even get to explore modern media and communication in present-day China.
