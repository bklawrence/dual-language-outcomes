Russian Student Award Winners

Global Village Academy Students Win Gold at 2026 Federal Russian Language Olympiad in Denver

 

Seven students from Global Village Academy won gold medals at the 2026 Federal Russian Language Olympiad in Denver. The competition was held at the University of Colorado Denver and included participants from across the region. Each Global Village Academy student placed first.

 

Gold Medal Winners:

  • Arina Davydova
  • Nikolai Gigiiak
  • Sergey Shcherbak
  • Oksana Shuda
  • Peter Sinitsky
  • Andrei Sologoub
  • Anzhelika Tarassova

The Federal Russian Language Olympiad evaluates students in reading, writing, listening, and speaking. First-place winners demonstrate advanced proficiency and cultural understanding.

 

Global Village Academy offers a Russian immersion program where students learn core subjects in both English and Russian. This approach helps students build strong academic skills while becoming bilingual.

 

Teachers Ksenia Appel-Whitney and Alena Darsky led the group and prepared students for the competition.

 

Russian Spelling Bee

Students at Global Village Academy North recently showcased their language skills during the school’s Russian Spelling Bee held on March 12, 2026.

 

The event brought together students from several grade levels who demonstrated impressive spelling, focus, and dedication to learning Russian. As part of the school’s language immersion program, students build strong literacy skills while developing the ability to read, write, and communicate in another language.

 

Events like the Russian Spelling Bee highlight the benefits of language immersion education, where students strengthen academic skills while also gaining cultural awareness and confidence. Through daily instruction in both English and Russian, students are developing abilities that will serve them well in an increasingly connected world.

 

Global Village Academy North is a public charter school in Colorado offering language immersion programs in Russian, Mandarin Chinese, and Spanish. These programs give students the opportunity to become bilingual while building strong academic foundations.

 

We are proud of all the students who participated and grateful to the teachers and staff who helped make the event such a success.
