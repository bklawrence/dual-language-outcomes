Ecole Bilingue is the only private, independent French immersion school in Louisiana. Located in the heart of Uptown, New Orleans, we offer programs for students 18 months – 8th grade.

Founded in 1998, our mission is to develop globally literate students through a rigorous, bilingual French-American curriculum, set in a nurturing and multicultural community. We guide our students to excel academically, foster their curiosity, and prepare them to become creative and innovative contributors to the world.
