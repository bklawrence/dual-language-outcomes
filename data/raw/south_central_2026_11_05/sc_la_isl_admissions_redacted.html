Welcome


Thank you for your interest in International School of Louisiana


The International School of Louisiana (ISL) is a tuition-free, public charter school offering K-8 education with a focus on French or Spanish language immersion, international awareness, and diversity. Founded in 2000, ISL aims to break barriers in public education by immersing students in French and Spanish from kindergarten, resulting in multilingual graduates. With multiple campuses, the school serves a diverse student body to prepare them for a globalized marketplace.

If your child is placed at ISL, the primary parent/guardian will receive an email from ISL’s Admissions department.

This email will go to the address the parent/guardian entered when they completed their application, and should be received within the next business day of receiving the placement notification from NOLA PS.



Claiming Your Seat


Parents/Guardians must complete the instructions listed in the email sent from ISL's Admissions department in order to claim your child(ren)'s seat.


If you have any questions or concerns, please email Admissions at admissions@isl-edu.org.


AGE REQUIREMENT:

Applications are only valid for the following school year. Children must be at least five years old by September 30, 2026, to enroll for the 2026-2027 school year.


ABOUT LANGUAGE IMMERSION:

Kindergarten and 1st Grade  applicants* do not require experience with French or Spanish.

*First grade students who apply after September 1st of their 1st grade year,  must already read, write, speak, and understand French or Spanish. Target Language Assessments will be scheduled as needed. Students must be present (in-person) to take the assessment.


TARGET LANGUAGE ASSESSMENT (2nd-8th grade):

2nd - 8th Grade applicants must already read, write, speak, and understand French or Spanish. Target Language Assessments will be scheduled as needed. Students must be present (in-person) to take the assessment.


ONLINE APPLICATION:

Applications are online-only. Families must submit an application to www.enrollnolaps.com.


Students in grades 2-8 who are interested in available seats at ISL should visit www.enrollnolaps.com for information about how to apply.


ANTI-DISCRIMINATION POLICY:

ISL does not discriminate against students on the basis of race, color, national origin, gender, disability, family situation, intellectual or athletic ability, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, natural, protective, or cultural hairstyles, or any other basis that would be illegal if used by a district board of education. ISL complies with all applicable Louisiana statutes concerning public schools.
