French Immersion at NLE

French Immersion at NLE

French immersion offer students a unique educational experience through the integration of bilingual instruction and cultural enrichment.

The curriculum, infused with French language instruction, is rooted in state and local standards. This curriculum prioritizes creativity, individualized learning, and extracurricular opportunities, while also preparing students for standardized testing and college readiness programs.

French immersion equips students with bilingual fluency, academic excellence, and the cultural understanding needed to thrive in an increasingly globalized world.

North Lewis offers French Immersion in grades K-6th. Immersion students move on to Belle Place Middle for grades 7th - 8th.

 

    Congratulations Mr. Tim!

  Mr. Tim Rosamond, principal, was recently inducted into the prestigious L’Ordre des Palmes  Academiques based on his outstanding work with the French Immersion Program.
