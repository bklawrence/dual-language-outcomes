French Immersion Now in PRE-K

French Immersion In Pointe Coupee Parish

Pre-Kindergarten (4 Year Olds), Kindergarten, and First Grade Students

Rougon French Immersion is expanding lower to serve students in Pre-Kindergarten!

Interested parents can attend an information night Wednesday, March 20th at 6:00

French Immersion is a free program for students in Pointe Coupee Parish at Rougon Elementary. Transportation is provided for students in the program.

Seats are limited to 20 pk students and will be chosen on a first come, first served basis. Kindergarten and 1st grades are limited to 25 seats and are chosen on a first come, first served basis.

Students learn how to read, write, and speak French throughout the day. They spend the majority of the day with a French teacher learning the language in French Language Arts, Math, Science, and Social Studies. They will also learn how to read and write in English with an English teacher.

Check out our Rougon French Immersion FaceBook page to see Immersion students and teachers in action!
