Dual Immersive Learning

Casa Castillo is a Spanish Immersion Preschool and Childcare center. With developmentally appropriate activities, modeled in an atmosphere of play, love and excitement, our kids learn Spanish in their everyday activities and are completely unaware that they are being “taught”. In this natural learning environment children will develop the skills of a second language.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to establish a safe and inviting environment, promote diversity and a broad worldview, and provide high-quality experiences to promote language development in Spanish and English. Our vision centers around the knowledge that our children’s future is determined early. Research proves that language learning happens in the earliest years of a child’s life.
