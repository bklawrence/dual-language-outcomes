On Indian reservations in the United States, a disproportionate number of Native American children are trapped in entrenched, generational poverty with very few resources or opportunities. St. Labre is so much more than a school; it is where systemic change begins and where Proud, Prosperous and Free futures are built. To help break the cycle of poverty and hopelessness, we provide a completely tuition-free private education alongside essential social services that support the whole child and their family. St. Labre relies on donations from friends like you for 94% of its funding. Join us today in creating a ripple effect of change through this life-changing work!

94% of St. Labre's annual operating budget is funded through the generous and faithful support of our donors.

Gifts from donors throughout the country make it possible for St. Labre Indian School to be more than a school. Here, Native American children from our impoverished reservation communities are empowered through faith-based tuition-free private education, nourished with healthy meals, provided essential social services and holistic programs that embrace and celebrate cultural heritage and traditions. It’s where Proud, Prosperous and Free futures are built, dreams are nurtured and bright tomorrows take shape.
