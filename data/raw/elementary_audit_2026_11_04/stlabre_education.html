Our Schools

At St. Labre, we believe all students deserve a transformative education that breaks the cycle of generational poverty and also enriches their lives and opens doors to opportunities. From preschool through high school, St. Labre is committed to providing a tuition-free, culturally-rich education that prepares students for future success, promotes spiritual growth and embraces the cultural identities of Native American children across three Southeastern Montana campuses.

In addition to a strong academic curriculum, St. Labre also offers classes and activities shaped by the Northern Cheyenne and Crow cultures. These include courses that teach Native languages, literature, history, tribal government and more. Our holistic approach to education focuses on the “whole student” by developing their mind, body and spirit – equally.

Our high school is fully accredited by the Montana State Office of Public Instruction and the Western Catholic Education Association. Motivated by our Catholic faith, every student has the opportunity to attend weekly Mass to feed the spirit as well as the mind.

St. Labre Indian School

Pre-K–12, Ashland, MT

Our main campus located in Ashland serves children from preschool through high school with a tuition-free, private education inspired by Catholic faith while honoring Native American cultures and traditions.

St. Charles Mission School
Pre-K-12, Ashland, MT

Nestled at the foot of the beautiful Pryor Mountains on the Crow Reservation just south of Billings, Montana, St. Charles Mission School, founded in fall 1891, provides students a tuition-free education from preschool through 8th grade.

Pretty Eagle Catholic Academy
K-8, St. Xavier, MT

Named in honor of Crow leader Pretty Eagle, the Academy is located on the Crow Reservation 23 miles south of Hardin, Montana. Our school has served Native American children and families since 1887 and provides a tuition-free education from kindergarten through 8th grade.

Alumni Spotlight

Your generosity helps our graduates build a brighter future! Here are a few alumni who are giving back to their communities so that more Native American students achieve a future that is Proud, Prosperous and Free.

Katie - Class of 2020

Katie earned her Bachelor of Social Work degree from the University of Montana in May of 2026—a milestone that caps off an incredibly impressive academic journey. While managing a full-time course load, Katie also dedicated her time to St. Labre's Youth and Family Services Department, proving herself to be an invaluable member of our team.  Katie was recently hired as the new St. Labre Outreach Coordinator. There is nothing quite like welcoming one of our own back into a new professional role, and we are incredibly grateful to have her continued passion and expertise driving our mission forward.

Allyson – Class of 2015

Allyson earned her Bachelor of Arts Degree in Liberal Arts at Mount St. Mary’s University in 2019; Allyson completed her Master of Science in Social Work from the University of Montana in 2022. She is currently the Group Home Coordinator of the St. Labre Group Homes. Before she came back to work at St. Labre, she worked as a Youth and Family Therapist at an organization providing outpatient mental health and addictions counseling.

Xavier – Class of 2017

Xavier, AKA Sonny, earned his Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from Haskell Indian Nations University in 2022. Sonny is currently enrolled in a Master of Science in Anthropology program at the University of Wyoming. Xavier is a World Champion Fancy Dancer and a member of the World Champion pow wow drum group, Showtime Singers.

Bryan – Class of 2006

Bryan earned his Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, Accounting, from Montana State University-Billings in 2026. He is currently enrolled in a Master of Business Administration program through Gonzaga University. Bryan is working as the Native Entrepreneur Development Specialist for the Office of Indian Country Economic Development within the Montana Department of Commerce.

Tiara – Class of 2018

Tiara earned her Bachelor of Science in Forestry Management from the University of Montana in 2025. She currently works as the Outreach and Engagement Program Officer for the Buffalo Nations Grassland Alliance, which helps ensure the diversity of life on the Northern Great Plains for current and future generations.

Sara – Class of 2018

Sara earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Montana State University in 2026. She currently works for the Indian Health Service Hospital in Crow Agency as a Med-Surg Nurse.

Amaya – Class of 2019

Amaya earned her Bachelor of Science in Human Services from Montana State University-Billings in 2025. She currently works as the Guidance Counselor at St. Labre High School.  

Jonē – Class of 2013

Jonē earned his Associate of Applied Science in Diesel Mechanics from Montana State University-Billings in 2019. He currently works as a mechanic for ONEOK, Inc, for which he helps maintain and repair essential natural gas infrastructure across North Dakota.

Kaylannah – Class of 2022

Kaylannah earned her Bachelor of Science in Health Sciences at Dominican University in 2026. She is currently taking Emergency Medical Training (EMT) courses in Lame Deer, Montana, and plans on working for the Northern Cheyenne Ambulance Service. Kay started the first ever Indigenous Student Organization on the Dominican campus.
