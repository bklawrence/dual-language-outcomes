Dual Language Immersion Programs

Programas de Inmersión en Dos Idiomas



Program Models & Goal

Program Models per TEA

Dual Language Immersion One-Way

A bilingual/biliteracy program model in which students identified as emergent bilingual students are served in both English and Spanish and are prepared to meet reclassification criteria in order to be successful in English-only instruction not earlier than six or later than seven years after the student enrolls in school.

Dual Language Immersion Two-Way

A bilingual/biliteracy program model in which students identified as emergent bilingual students are integrated with students proficient in English and are served in both English and Spanish and are prepared to meet reclassification criteria in order to be successful in English-only instruction not earlier than six or later than seven years after the student enrolls in school.

Goal

Emergent bilingual students attain bilingualism and biliteracy to reach high levels of literacy and academic achievement in Spanish and English. 

Liberty Hill ISD's Dual Language Immersion (DLI) Programs are available in grades PK-5. These DLI Programs are currently hosted at Liberty Hill Elementary School. 

Classroom instruction is taught in both Spanish and English according to the 90/10 Program Model. This means the program begins with 90% Spanish/10% English instruction and ends in 5th grade with 50% Spanish/50% English instruction. Students must meet the eligibility requirements to participate. 


ANNOUNCEMENT:

DLI One-Way will be implemented and phased in starting with the 2025-2026 school year. 

DLI Two-Way will be phased out starting with the remaining cohorts in the 2025-2026 school year.


Frequently Asked Questions:

  • If my child opted in as a Non-Emergent Bilingual or English Proficient student with another DL Two-Way Program in a Texas public school, are they eligible to transfer into LHISD's DL Two-Way Program?

Following verification of program alignment and consistent participation, a transfer will be approved only if there is an available seat in the classroom, and with the approval of the District Bilingual/ESL Coordinator. Please note the DL Two-Way is being phased out and participating grade level classes are limited.


  • Is there an evaluation or special consideration for admission if my child has participated in a Spanish Immersion private program or daycare?

Unfortunately, we cannot accommodate that request. Admission is based on eligibility.


  • Will I be able to re-enroll my Non-EB student back into the DLI Two-Way Program after withdrawing from the LHISD DL Program?

Non-EB students who opted into the DL Two-Way Program, and either withdrew from the program and/or Liberty Hill ISD, are not eligible to re-enroll in the program. 


For detailed information, please contact the Bilingual/ESL Coordinator.
