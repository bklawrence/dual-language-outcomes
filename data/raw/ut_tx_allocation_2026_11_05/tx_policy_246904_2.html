Dual Language

 

Welcome!

Over the next few weeks, we will be hosting Dual Language Parent Information Sessions for families interested in learning more about our Dual Language (DL) program. These sessions are for families of students that are newly enrolling in school and are interested in learning more regarding the program services offered in Georgetown ISD. 

    

Virtual Sessions

Join us via Google Meet by choosing the date and time that works for you below:

Updated May Dates:

May 19th 5:00 pm  

May 20th 10:00 am  

May 20th 5:00 pm  

 

The Goals of our Time will be:

  • Inform families of our 90/10 Dual Language Program model

  • Share out the process regarding lottery selection for families interested in applying to the program for their incoming Kindergartner

  • Create an opportunity to answer questions and connect with one another

We look forward to connecting with you and sharing more about our Dual Language program!
