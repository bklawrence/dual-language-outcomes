Dual Language - Two-Way at Elementary

  • Program Overview: The Two-Way Dual Language (TWDL) program is an additive, enrichment bilingual model that integrates native English speakers and Spanish-speaking Emergent Bilingual students within a shared instructional setting, delivering literacy and content instruction across both languages. Rather than transitioning students toward English-only instruction, the model develops bilingualism and biliteracy concurrently, targeting grade-level academic achievement and full proficiency in Spanish and English.
  • Student Population and Program Design: The model is designated “two-way” because it serves two language groups concurrently, in balanced cohorts that position each group as a linguistic and cultural model for the other. As an additive program, it cultivates a second language without displacing students’ home language. 
  • Language Allocation ( The 50/50 A/B- Week Framework) : Instructional time is allocated evenly across the two program languages — 50% Spanish, 50% English — through a two-week (A/B) rotation in which the language of instruction alternates each week.
  • Dual Language Two-Way at Elementary Framework 

Participating Schools

These schools participate in the dual language program. If your child's school is listed below, your child can enroll in the program, pending space availability.

If your child's school is not listed below, you will need to submit a transfer application to enroll your child in one of the participating schools. To learn about the transfer process, visit the Transfer website. 

Elementary Schools

  • Brandenburg
  • Farine

 

How to Enroll

Students must be entering kindergarten or first grade in August.

Step 1

Contact Brandenburg, Farine to be placed on the waitlist.

Step 2

The campus will notify you of your student's acceptance into the program and will provide an Acceptance Letter for you to sign and return.

Transfers

If your Dual Language school of choice is not your resident school, please submit an in-district transfer request beginning March 1 at IrvingISD.net/Transfers.
