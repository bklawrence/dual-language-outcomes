Multilingual Programs

Programas Multilingües

  • Vision
  • Visión

Vision

Inspiring and empowering students to be innovative bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural thinkers in the global community.

Visión

Inspirar y empoderar a los estudiantes para que sean pensadores innovadores bilingües, biliterados y biculturales en la comunidad global.

  • Mission
  • Misión

Mission

Our mission is to deliver high-quality instruction that develops students’ bilingualism, biliteracy, and cultural competence, while fostering critical thinking, creativity, and innovation. Through strong collaboration with educators, stakeholders, families, and the community, we prepare students to thrive in a global society.

Misión

Nuestra misión es brindar instrucción de alta calidad que desarrolle el bilingüismo, la biliteracidad y la competencia cultural de los estudiantes, al mismo tiempo que fomenta el pensamiento crítico, la creatividad y la innovación. A través de una sólida colaboración con educadores, partes interesadas, familias y la comunidad, preparamos a los estudiantes para prosperar en una sociedad global.

  • Overview
  • Descripción General

Overview

Multilingual programs are committed to promoting academic excellence, equity, and access for emergent bilingual (EB) learners in Castleberry ISD. Programs include: 

  • One-Way Dual Language (elementary)
  • English as a Second Language Program (elementary and secondary)

These programs are structured to ensure every child’s native language, culture, background, and interests are respected and utilized in the learning process. Additionally, best practices for emergent bilinguals (EBs) are implemented to accelerate the academic progress in all content areas. This will build and develop communication skills and cognitive academic language naturally.

Castleberry ISD serves more than 1,500 emergent bilingual students across grades PK–12, with Spanish being the most commonly represented. The district embraces the diverse population of students and strives to provide high quality, differentiated instruction to meet the needs of all EB students.

Descripción General

Los programas multilingües están comprometidos con promover la excelencia académica, la equidad y el acceso para los estudiantes bilingües emergentes (EB) en Castleberry ISD. Nuestros programas incluyen:

  • Programa de Lenguaje Dual de una vía (primaria)
  • Programa de Inglés como Segundo Idioma – ESL (primaria y secundaria)

Dentro de estos programas, nos aseguramos de que el idioma nativo, la cultura, el origen y los intereses de cada estudiante sean respetados y aprovechados en el proceso de aprendizaje. Además, se implementan prácticas educativas efectivas para estudiantes bilingües emergentes (EB) con el fin de acelerar su progreso académico en todas las áreas de contenido. Esto contribuye al desarrollo natural de habilidades de comunicación y del lenguaje académico cognitivo.

Atendemos a más de 1,500 estudiantes bilingües emergentes desde Prekínder hasta el grado 12. En Castleberry ISD, el español es el idioma más común entre los estudiantes bilingües emergentes. Valoramos nuestra población diversa y nos esforzamos por ofrecer instrucción diferenciada y de alta calidad para satisfacer las necesidades de todos los estudiantes bilingües emergentes (EB).

Elementary Programs

  • Dual Language Program
  • English as a Second Language (ESL) Program

Dual Language Program

Castleberry ISD’s Dual Language Program provides students with the opportunity to learn academic content in two languages, supporting the development of bilingualism, biliteracy, academic success, and cultural awareness.

Through this program, students build strong literacy skills in both languages while mastering grade-level academic standards.

  • Program Goals
  • Program Model

Program Goals

  • Bilingualism and Biliteracy
  • Academic Achievement
  • Sociocultural Competence

Bilingualism and Biliteracy

Students develop the ability to listen, speak, read, and write in both languages for both social and academic purposes.

Academic Achievement

Students learn the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) and meet grade-level expectations across all subjects.

Sociocultural Competence

Students explore diverse perspectives and develop an understanding and appreciation of their own culture and the cultures of others.

Program Model

To empower students to become biliterate, achieve academic excellence, and develop cross-cultural competence, all elementary campuses in Castleberry ISD offer a One-Way Dual Language Program for students in Prekindergarten through 5th grade. The program follows an 90/10 instructional model, providing instruction in both Spanish and English to support strong language development and academic success.

The program follows a 90/10 instructional model, meaning:

  • Students receive 90% of instruction in Spanish and 10% in English in the early grades.
  • English instruction gradually increases each year.
  • By the upper elementary grades, students are learning in both languages daily.

This model helps students develop strong literacy foundations in Spanish while building English proficiency over time.

English as a Second Language (ESL) Program

Elementary campuses in Castleberry ISD provide an English as a Second Language (ESL) Pull-Out program for Emergent Bilingual students whose parents have approved ESL services. The ESL Pull-Out program provides targeted English language development through the content of English Language Arts and Reading. Teachers of Emergent Bilingual students who provide English Language Arts and Reading are certified in ESL instruction and trained in effective second language acquisition methods.

Additionally, all other content teachers (Math, Science and Social Studies) are encouraged to participate in training involving sheltered instruction methods to help support the language development of Emergent Bilingual students.

Secondary Programs

  • English as a Second Language (ESL) Program

English as a Second Language (ESL) Program

At the secondary level, Castleberry ISD provides a strong and supportive English as a Second Language (ESL) program designed to help Emergent Bilingual students thrive academically while developing English proficiency. Students whose parents approve ESL services participate in an ESL Pull-Out program, where they receive targeted English language development through English Language Arts and Reading instruction.

Emergent Bilingual students are taught by highly qualified ESL-certified teachers who are trained in effective second language acquisition strategies and are committed to helping students succeed in rigorous academic environments.

Language development does not happen in isolation. In Castleberry ISD, all teachers share responsibility for supporting multilingual learners. Teachers in Math, Science, and Social Studies participate in professional learning focused on Content-Based Language Instruction, ensuring that students continue to build academic language and content knowledge in every classroom.

Through this collaborative approach, Castleberry ISD prepares Emergent Bilingual students to develop strong language skills, succeed academically, and confidently participate in an increasingly global and multilingual world.
