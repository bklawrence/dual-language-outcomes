— Current Parent

 

Sejong Academy an authorized IB World School. Our Middle Years Program for students in grades 7-10 was authorized on June 24, 2022. Our Career-related Program for students in grades 11-12 was authorized on August 11, 2025. IB World Schools share a common philosophy—a commitment to high quality, challenging, international education -that Sejong Academy believes is important for our students.

Families, please go to our School Updates page to learn how to sign-up for announcements.

2025-2026 School Climate Survey (click here)

Dear Sejong Families,

In an effort to seek continuous improvement, the Board of Sejong Academy invites all parents/guardians to fill out the below School Climate survey.  

Your participation in this evaluation is very important, as these surveys are part of our performance framework with our authorizer and used when evaluating our school. So, it is very important that we get as much family participation as possible. So please take a few minutes to share your thoughts.  We appreciate your time and effort in this process.


If you have questions about this survey, please email board@sejongacademy.org.

BREAKFAST & LUNCH
Sejong Academy has been approved to serve free breakfast and lunch to all students under the Community Eligibility Provision with the Minnesota Department of Education. Please e-mail us to let us know if your child wants to bring their own lunch.
