Where World-Class Learning Meets Spanish Immersion

Welcome to the International Spanish Language Academy (ISLA), a tuition-free, PreK-5 public charter school and one of only two in Minnesota offering both full Spanish immersion and an accredited International Baccalaureate (IB) education. By combining deep language acquisition with a global, inquiry-based curriculum, we empower students to become confident, bilingual problem-solvers. 

At ISLA, we aren't just teaching a second language – we are shaping tomorrow's global leaders.

Academics

Top-tier inquiry learning that prepares students with full Spanish immersion and a rich, transdisciplinary curriculum

Enrich­ment

Daily opportunities for deepening in the Arts, Music, STEM, and Physical Education, alongside engaging projects and explorations

Global Community

With teachers, interns, and families hailing from over 30 countries, ISLA prioritizes a diverse and inclusive community

Beyond the Classroom

A variety of field trips, extra-curricular programs, and summer options create a robust learning environment

Our Mission

International Spanish Language Academy (“ISLA”), a Spanish immersion community, empowers the whole child by nurturing the development of personal growth, strong character, academic excellence, and global vision.
