RED CLOUD ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
MAȞPÍYA LÚTA HUKHÚČIYELA OWÁYAWA

A Heritage Language Immersion program educating the future leaders of our reservation… and our world.

Lakota Language Immersion: 

Learning academic content while learning Lakota language at the same time.

Culturally Based Curriculum Rooted in Lakȟota and Catholic Traditions

We strive to have a standards based curriculum that meets the academic, spiritual and language learning needs of all students. We assess student progress throughout the year in a variety of ways to get a complete picture of student learning.
