ML Family Night

More than 250 learners, families, and community members came together at Agassiz for Fargo Public Schools' Fall Multilingual Learner Family Night. Guests enjoyed food and connected with other families from across the district as they gathered to celebrate their cultures and strengthen their sense of belonging within the FPS community. The event included an informative session about the multilingual learner program and fun activities.

FPS Credit Union Donation

Thank you to the FPS Federal Credit Union for its generosity and continued investment in the success of Fargo Public Schools learners through its support of the Fargo Public Schools Development Foundation! The FPS Federal Credit Union is now in the fourth year of its generous pledge of $10,000 per year to the Foundation, helping provide essential resources and enriching educational opportunities for FPS learners. This year's donation will also serve as a matching gift for Giving Hearts Day on February 11, 2027, encouraging additional community support and making an even greater impact.

2025-26 Annual Report

The Fargo Public Schools 2025-26 Annual Report is a summary of the District’s efforts to carry out its mission of “Educating and Empowering All Students to Succeed”. The report is an in-depth examination of the District’s learners, educators, schools, departments, and programs and a celebration of their achievements. It features statistics regarding staffing, budget, demographics, and assessment data. Thank you for supporting FPS and allowing us to serve our learners and families!

SPED Advisory Committee (opens in new window/tab)

The Special Education Parent Advisory Committee brings together students, parents, caregivers, District staff, and community members to promote inclusion, understanding, respect, and support for learners with disabilities. SEPAC is seeking parents, caregivers, community members, and FPS staff who would like to participate in a workgroup that will look at special education data from the Level of Determination Report Card. Interested individuals should complete the SEPAC Workgroup Interest Form.

Read More about SPED Advisory Committee (opens in new window/tab)
My Life is Worth Living (opens in new window/tab)

North Dakota families are invited to a virtual event featuring My Life is Worth Living, the first animated series dedicated to mental health and suicide prevention. Through the stories of seven characters, the series models the support and connection that help young people move from negative thoughts toward hope and self-compassion. Thanks to funding from ND Health and Human Services, these monthly events are no-cost, family-friendly, and open to every North Dakotan. The next virtual event is at 7 p.m. next Wednesday, November 11.

Read More about My Life is Worth Living (opens in new window/tab)
Snacks for Students

During the month of November, The City of Fargo is partnering with MinnKota Recycling to promote the “Snacks for Students” Recycling Challenge. Proceeds from the campaign will benefit the Fargo Public Schools Foundation’s efforts to provide snacks for students in need. MinnKota Recycling will provide a one-cent-per-pound match for all aluminum cans collected in November at Fargo’s recycling sites listed below. Additionally, the Metro Sports Foundation will donate $500 to the campaign.

What is an Absence? (opens in new window/tab)

An absence is any time a student isn't presence during the school day. Whether an absence is excused or unexcused, it counts towards attendance totals and how the District responds. When students are absent, they miss valuable instruction, classroom conversations, and learning experiences.

CTE Commitments to Success (opens in new window/tab)

FPS Career & Technical Education educators are connecting their professional goals to three commitments that reflect the heart of high-quality CTE: Commit to Students, Commit to Quality, Commit to Excellence. These commitments are grounded in their work to strengthen learning, build meaningful career pathways, and ensure learners are prepared for what comes next.
