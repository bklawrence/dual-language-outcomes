Ríos Spanish Dual Language Immersion Program

The goal of Ríos Spanish Immersion program is to provide educational experiences that support bilingualism and biliteracy in Spanish and English, as well as reinforce high academic standards and increase socio-cultural competency. The program begins in kindergarten and is continued through Grade 12.

There are Ríos classrooms at the following school locations: 

  • Kindergarten at Goodview School
  • Grades 1-4 at Washington-Kosciusko Elementary
  • Grades 5-8 at Winona Middle School
  • Grades 9-12 at Winona Senior High School

It is the only dual language immersion program in Winona County.

All Winona Area Public Schools students will be offered the same rigorous core curriculum in reading, writing, math, science, and social studies. Immersion students will study the same core curriculum and Minnesota academic standards which connect subject areas.

Ríos students will also have the opportunity to graduate with a Minnesota State Seal of Biliteracy, which earns credits in the Minnesota State University system.

How to apply

Each year, there is an application window to apply to the program, usually in February. Instructions on how to apply are posted at winonaschools.org/rios and sent to families through the WAPS messaging system. 

At the end of the application window, district leadership determines if there is enough interest in the program to add an additional section (classroom) of kindergarten for the following year. If a second kindergarten section is added, additional applicants will be accepted on a first-come, first-serve basis. If there is only one section offered, and there are more applicants than spots, a lottery will be held. 

Because the circumstances surrounding each application year may differ, please reach out to the building principal or fill out the question form if you need additional information.

Features of the program

  • Students will become proficient listeners, speakers, writers and readers in both Spanish and English.
  • It is the only language immersion program in Winona County. 
  • Students will master content in the same subject areas as their counterparts who do not attend the program.
  • Students will develop an appreciation of other cultures in addition to their own.
  • The program features a mix 
  • Interested parties must apply for entrance into the program. 

 

Benefits of the program

  • Promotes bilingualism and biliteracy, increases cultural competence, and leads to high academic achievement.
  • Research shows that dual language immersion programs are an important tool in promoting equity by eliminating “achievement gaps.”
  • Improved executive functioning, better problem-solving, and increased cultural competency. 
  • Expanded employment opportunities for bilingual students

— From the Minnesota Department of Education
 

What students can expect in the program

  • Grades K-4 | Goodview School & W-K Elementary
  • Grades 5-8 | Winona Middle School
  • Grades 9-12 | Winona Senior High School

Grades K-4 | Goodview School & W-K Elementary

Students in Grades K-2 receive approximately 70% of their daily instruction in Spanish. 

A typical day will include Spanish Language Arts, Math, Science and Social Studies lessons in Spanish.  Students will also have the following subjects taught in English: Music, Art, Physical Education, Counseling, Library/Media and a 20-30 minute English Language Arts lesson daily.

In Grades 3-4 students will receive 60% of their daily instruction in Spanish and more English Language Arts minutes are added to the day.

Grades 5-8 | Winona Middle School

In Grade 5, students will receive about 150 minutes of instruction (44% of the day) in Spanish in Spanish Language Arts, Science and Social Studies. As students advance through middle school and take more electives, there will be more instruction delivered in English than Spanish. In Grade 6, students will take courses in Spanish Language Arts and Math. In Grades 7-8, students will take only a Spanish Language Arts course as they begin to explore a wide variety of electives offered in English. 

Grades 9-12 | Winona Senior High School

Students in Grades 9-10 are offered a Spanish Language Arts course, while students in Grade 11-12 can take AP Spanish. A dual language immersion elective in Spanish Language Media & Film will be offered for Grades 10-12. As of the 2023-24 school year, the first cohort of Ríos students is in Grade 10, so the high school offerings will continue to evolve as more students enter high school.

Students can also earn college credit through demonstrating proficiency on the Seal of Biliteracy assessment. 

Students are encouraged to enroll in the Service Learning class, and they will receive a community placement where their Spanish skills can be used in an authentic environment. 

An evolving program

The Ríos Dual Language Spanish Immersion program started in the 2014-15 school year and has continued to evolve since Day 1. The journey began with a vision to create a community of bilingual champions, and over the years, we’ve refined our approach to ensure a dynamic, effective and sustainable learning experience. We are committed to hearing from our families about what’s working well and what can be improved to make a program that continues to flourish. 

Native Spanish speakers

Studies have shown that native Spanish speakers enrolled in dual language programs tend to achieve academic English proficiency at a faster rate and have higher academic achievement rates compared to their peers of similar language backgrounds.

Minnesota state standards

All Winona Area Public Schools offer the same rigorous core curriculum in reading, writing, math, science, and social studies. Dual language immersion students will study the same core curriculum and Minnesota academic standards which connect subject areas.

When will my child speak Spanish?

Students will begin to understand the Spanish language and use words and phrases in kindergarten. In Grades 1-2, students will be able to speak, read and write in Spanish. 

How will new students understand?

Dual language immersion teachers will use body language, visuals, manipulatives and expressions to communicate the meaning of Spanish words with students. All teachers are bilingual, so in case of an emergency, clear instructions will be given in English as well as Spanish.
