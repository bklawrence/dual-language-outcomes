THE PROGRAM

A combination of daily routines, special activities, some language instruction will keep participants busy and engaged while having a great time.
The program is offered on two consecutive weeks. You have the option of choosing either week or both!

Two daily meals and a snack integrate vocabulary building, cultural norms around meal time and preparation and sampling of various typical dishes.
The ease of access to the Farmington river will provide opportunities for walks and an exploration of the local flora and animal life. Kayaking and fishing are also possibilities!
There will be plenty of indoor activities as well, from painting to creating music, guided either by local guests or by Sra. Parada.
