About LILA

Lakes International Language Academy provides an international education, preparing students with global competence for the 21st century. There is no tuition for students in grades K-12, and our fee-based 4-Star Parent Aware preschool program has a variety of tuition assistance options for qualifying families. Since 2004, parents have chosen LILA to help guide their children toward becoming responsible, caring, informed, curious, and engaged citizens.

Language Acquisition

Preschool students jumpstart Spanish or Mandarin Chinese language acquisition through play and guided activities. Students in grades K-5 choose a full language immersion program in either Chinese or Spanish. Grades 6-12 students continue building language skills through immersion, or they may opt for fast-track Spanish, Chinese or French. 
No second language experience
is needed to enroll at Upper School! 

Learn More

International Baccalaureate

Education at Lakes International focuses on the whole child. We don’t see students as simply test takers. Our curriculum centers around a set of ten learner attributes defined by the IB Organization. These qualities help prepare LILA students to become active, compassionate, and lifelong learners, ready to contribute to their colleges and universities, workplaces, communities, and the world. 

Learn More

Student Life

At LILA, we encourage learning beyond the academic day. Complementing our rigorous curriculum is an array of after-school choices including arts, athletics, clubs, and leadership programs. We host summer language immersion, math and reading camps, partner with the YMCA’s Youth in Government program, and more. We also offer a quality child care program with before and after care during the school year, as well as non-school day and summer care.
It’s ALWAYS busy here!

Get Involved

Families who valued a global education initiated LILA. They volunteered countless hours researching best practices, establishing policies, and so much more. We had a Parent-Teacher Organization before we even had a building! Our board of directors is comprised of parents, teachers, and a community representative, all elected by parents and staff. You’ll find many opportunities to use your time, talents and treasures to make a difference.

Careers

Our staff bring the world to our students every day - literally. Many of them have lived outside of the United States, residing on numerous continents around the world. Whether a teacher from another country, or an administrative assistant who lives in the local community, you’ll find LILA is truly a unique place.  We have a great team, and a great culture. Life After LILA: Max LaPointe, Class of 2020
Oct 27 2026

Honoring a Legacy, One Step at a Time

In October 2025, LILA alumnus Max LaPointe, Class of 2020, undertook a remarkable journey through Poland and Germany to honor his great-grandfather’s service during World War II. Max retraced approximately 500 miles associated with the Black Hunger March, a forced march of Allied prisoners of war as Nazi Germany evacuated its POW camps ahead of advancing Soviet forces.

For Max, the journey was more than a physical challenge. It was an opportunity to connect with his family’s history, better understand his great-grandfather’s experiences and reflect on the sacrifices made for freedom.
