DLI Information Sessions

Parents/guardians are strongly encouraged to attend a DLI Parent Informational Session. 

It is very important that parents/guardians understand the goals of the DLI program and understand that by enrolling your child in DLI, you are committing to keeping your student in DLI (to the best of your ability) through at least 8th grade, minimally, due to the length of time it takes to learn a second language.
