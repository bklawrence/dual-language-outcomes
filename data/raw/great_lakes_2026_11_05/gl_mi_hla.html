Welcome to Holland Language Academy

I believe every child deserves the chance to succeed, no matter their circumstances. As a proud graduate of Holland Public Schools and a parent whose children learn in our buildings, I believe deeply in the work we are doing together for my children and yours.

This is my nineteenth year in education and my eleventh as principal, yet my passion for teaching and supporting children has never been stronger. I am grateful for the privilege of serving the community that shaped me, and I look forward to all we will accomplish together for our students this year.

-Iliana Vasquéz
Principal


Holland Language Academy challenges Preschool - 5th grade students to explore core curriculum in a unique two-way, bilingual-immersion model.

Interested in attending HLA during the 2026-2027 School Year? We have limited openings in all grades.

  • Learn more about our program!
  • Complete the online enrollment.
  • Contact the HLA Office at 616-494-2600.

What's Cooking | October Newsletter from Holland Nutrition Service

Nutrition Services is excited to bring a little extra fun to our cafeterias by celebrating special National Days throughout the school year. These give us another opportunity to make mealtime enjoyable, introduce students to different foods, and encourage participation in our school meal programs.

  • National Taco Day | October 6  - We will kick off the month by celebrating with a delicious Breakfast Taco! 
  • Count Day Celebration | October 7 - All students in attendance will receive a refreshing Cool Tropics treat. 
  • National Apple Day | October 21 - We will celebrate by serving Apple Cobbler with lunch. 
  • National Pumpkin Day  | October 26 - Everyone who eats lunch with us that day receiving a delicious slice of Pumpkin Bread.

Home Football Games - Reminder of Rules and Expectations

As Holland High School prepares to host the last home football game of the season, here is a reminder about some of the rules and expectations for students and families who attend the game:

  • Middle school age and younger MUST be an accompanied by an adult.
  • No student backpacks.
  • No re-entry for any spectators.
  • All school rules apply to events held at Hope College. 
  • The stadium and parking lot is a drug free school zone which includes any vape products.
  • HHS Students with 5Star App will be admitted for FREE.
  • HMS Students with an athletics pass will be admitted for FREE.

Holland will host Godwin Heights at the Hope College Stadium, Thursday at 7pm. #GoDutch
