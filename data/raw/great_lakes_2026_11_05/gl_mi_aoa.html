Academy of the Americas

Cub Campus

Welcome to Academy of the Americas PK-3, a vibrant Spanish dual language immersion school nestled in Southwest Detroit. Our unique 90/10 model of language instruction ensures that students develop fluency in both Spanish and English, setting them on a path toward bilingualism and cultural appreciation. With over 30 years of experience, we've honed our approach to deliver an enriching academic experience that celebrates diversity and fosters a love for learning.

Beyond language proficiency, our school offers a dynamic array of extracurricular activities, including music, art, media arts, and athletics. From strumming guitars to scoring goals on the field, students can explore their passions and talents outside the traditional classroom setting. At Academy of the Americas K-3, we're not just shaping students academically – we're cultivating well-rounded individuals who are equipped to thrive. Join us on this exciting journey of discovery and growth!

Tiger Campus

Welcome to the Academy of the Americas, where education feels like home. From Pre-K through grades 4-12, we provide a nurturing environment and a rigorous Dual Language Immersion program in Spanish and English. Our innovative approach ensures fluency in both languages while maintaining a strong focus on traditional academics. In addition to our comprehensive curriculum, we offer a variety of extracurricular activities including art, sports, robotics, and more, catering to diverse interests and talents.

At our Konkel St. location, high school students benefit from our 50/50 Spanish immersion program, featuring specialized courses in Spanish Language Literature and Culture. This unique immersion experience enhances language skills and cultural understanding, preparing students for college and beyond. With a focus on academic excellence and cultural enrichment, the Academy of the Americas offers a dynamic and supportive learning environment for students to thrive and succeed.

Mr. Nicholas Brown

I have been an educator at the Academy of the Americas for 17 years as a teacher, assistant principal, and principal. I have been principal at the school for 12 years. I have also been assistant principal at Earhart Elementary Middle School for 2 years. Born in Caracas, Venezuela but raised in a bilingual household, the Academy of the Americas is the type of school I wish I had growing up as a dual language learner. As a result, I have committed my career to providing and preserving opportunities for students and families to successfully achieve second language acquisition while maintaining a strong foundation in cultural identity.
