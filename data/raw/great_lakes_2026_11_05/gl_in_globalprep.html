About Global Prep Academies

Global Prep Academies serve students in Indianapolis with a focus on Dual Language through our two campuses. Pre-K-5 is located at our Riverside campus, while our students in grades 6-8 attend our newest campus, Global Prep Middle Academy @ the Monon. The diversity and culture that exists in the corridors of GPA characterizes us, thus having personnel from more than 17 countries. Students, teachers, and staff are hearing and speaking two languages throughout the day, working daily to become bilingual.

The Global Prep Way

Global Prep Academies offers a bilingual learning experience in Spanish and English, providing all students with a rigorous, culturally relevant education rooted in state standards. Our school upholds high expectations for academic and social success and offers a range of enrichment and extended-day opportunities, including tutoring, sports, and clubs.
