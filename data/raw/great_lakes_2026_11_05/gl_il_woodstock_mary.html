Mary Endres / International Spanish Academy

Mary Endres Elementary School is proud to be an International Spanish Academy 

The International Spanish Academy (ISA) program is an educational outreach initiative of the Ministry of Education of Spain where high performing schools in the United States and Canada implement a Spanish-English dual language immersion curriculum with the support, consultation and recognition of the Ministry.

The program design includes a content based instructional curriculum for different subjects, in which the Spanish language, and the culture and history of Spain and of other Spanish speaking countries are present, within the K-12 framework.

Learn more about the ISA program here.

https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/eeuu/en/convocatorias-programas/convocatorias-eeuu/isa.html
