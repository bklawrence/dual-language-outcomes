About Us

Neinas Dual Language Learning Academy

Welcome to Neinas Dual Language Learning Academy we offer pre-K through 8th grade where the journey of academic exploration and cultural immersion awaits! As someone new to our community, we're thrilled to extend an invitation for you to discover the vibrant tapestry of opportunities that our school offers.  With over 470 students already thriving in our nurturing environment, you'll find yourself embraced by a supportive community dedicated to your growth and success.

As you embark on your journey to Neinas, you'll encounter a dynamic blend of rigorous academics. But that is just the beginning. Beyond the classroom, our Before and After School Programs offer tailored experiences to ignite your passions and bolster your skills, ensuring that your journey with us extends far beyond the confines of traditional education. And for those with a love for sports, our Athletics Program provides the perfect arena to cultivate teamwork, resilience, and a healthy competitive spirit. At Neinas, we're not just preparing you for middle school and high school—we're empowering you to embrace the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead with confidence and determination. Join us in shaping your future with boundless possibilities, where every step forward is a step closer to realizing your dreams.

Principal

Ms. Adriana Rendon

Adriana Rendón, Principal of Neinas Dual Language Learning Academy began her career in 1999 teaching at a Detroit charter school. In 2002, Ms. Rendón chose to leave the charter school system and work for the Detroit Public School Community District. Ms. Rendón taught 7th & 8th grade mathematics at Clippert Multicultural Academy for 9 years and for 2 years she held the position of Technology and Mathematics Instructional Specialist. In 2013, Ms. Rendón was given an opportunity to become the Assistant Principal at Academy of the Americas, a K-12 Spanish immersion learning community. From 2015-2018, Ms. Rendón was an Assistant Principal of Western International High School. Adriana Rendón is a proud graduate of Cass Technical High School and her credentials include a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Michigan and a Master’s Degree in Education and Administrative Certification from Wayne State University.

Adriana Rendón is a firm believer in dual language acquisition. At Neinas Dual Language Learning Academy, students will be exposed to learning Spanish in a safe, respectful, and diverse environment.

At Neinas Dual Language Learning Academy, we educate and empower every student, every day, to build a stronger community and provide the knowledge, skills, and confidence necessary for our students to ensure they are college and career ready.

In her free time, Ms. Adriana Rendón enjoys spending time with her family, friends and Delta Tau Lambda sorority sisters. Other interests include: politics, community service, exercise, shopping, traveling and boating.

Assistant Principal

Mrs. Cynthia Boyce

Cynthia L Boyce, Assistant Principal of Neinas Dual Language Learning Academy began her career in 1997 teaching in Saginaw, MI. In 1998, she then moved to Detroit to teach 4th and 5th grade at Mann Learning Community in Detroit Public Schools for 14 years. In 2012, Mrs. Boyce chose to leave the school district and work for a charter school system for 3 years then decided to come back home to Detroit Public Schools Community District in 2016. Mrs. Boyce taught kindergarten at Dossin Elementary/Middle for 6 years where she held the position of lead teacher, athletic director, school/event coordinator and mentor. Cynthia Boyce is originally from Talladega, Alabama and her credentials include a Bachelor of Arts from Alabama A and M University and two Master’s in Education and Administrative Certification from Wayne State University.

Mrs. Boyce has a mission to provide a safe and welcoming learning environment with the highest quality of service to students, parents, and staff. Her goal is to help students recognize that a quality education is the key to unlocking their world and changing their conditions so they can rise. She is looking forward to meeting each one of you personally. If there is anything, she can do to make this school year better, please do not hesitate to call, text, email, or just stop by her office, the door is always open. As always, together we can accomplish great things!
