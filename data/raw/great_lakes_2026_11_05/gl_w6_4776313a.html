Instructional Model

BCSC Pre-K to High School Vision

Kindergarten-5th grade 

  • Full immersion with increased Mandarin Literacy – Students have one homeroom, Mandarin teacher; Models differ slightly in grades 4 and 5.
  • Allied Arts (art, music, library & PE) in English
  • Mandarin: Math, Science & Mandarin Language Arts
  • English: Social Studies, Math Reinforcement & English/Language Arts

Grades 6-8

  • Mandarin Literacy (1 and 2)  and Chinese 1

High School

  • AP Mandarin or Dual Credit courses specifically designed for Immersion students
  • Chinese 2 and 3
