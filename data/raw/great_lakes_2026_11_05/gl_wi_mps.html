What Sets Us Apart

With so many specialized options, there's an MPS school for every student. We're proud of our academic, arts, and athletics programming and all the dedicated teachers and staff who make the district great. What kind of educational options are you interested in?

Music and Arts

Expect Creativity

Art unlocks a different way of thinking. Art experiences at MPS include drumlines, jazz choir, dance, filmmaking, digital media, and more. With our specialized art schools, you can practice your craft every day with a community of artists around you.

Learn More

Multilingual and Multicultural Education

Our Diversity Is Our Strength

MPS students graduate with strong cultural and linguistic identities, leveraging these assets to navigate college, career, and life as global citizens in an inclusive society. 

Learn More

Career and Technical Education

Work-Based Learning Programs

Discover career pathways for a wide range of high-wage, high-skill, high-demand careers. Nursing and healthcare, food preparation and service, construction, digital media, agriculture, and more are offered at various schools. Earn industry credentials before you graduate!

Learn More

Montessori Education

50+ Years of Hands-On Learning

We offer the nation’s largest cluster of public Montessori schools; our eight tuition-free Montessori schools enroll more than 4,000 students annually. We have the only public K–12 Montessori school in the nation, and the only public English/Spanish Montessori program in the Midwest.

Learn More

Literacy

All Means All

Learning to read well helps students understand their world and communicate clearly to others. Our new literacy program shapes reading skills across all classes, not just English. Strong literacy builds a foundation for excellence and joy in learning.

Learn More

Athletics

Milwaukee City Conference

Student-athletes from 21 high schools compete in the MCC, creating connections in the classroom and under the lights. The MCC provides students with quality resources and high-level coaching, preparing the next generation of leaders for the world around them.

Learn More
