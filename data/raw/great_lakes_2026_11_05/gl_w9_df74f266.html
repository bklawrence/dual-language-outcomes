Dual Language Education

What is Dual Language Education (DLE)?

In a Dual Language Education (DLE) setting, students learn literacy and content in two languages. At Talcott, our program integrates Spanish and English from Pre-K through eighth grade, guided by three pillars of bilingualism:
 

High Academic Achievement

Ensuring students meet or exceed grade-level standards in both languages

Bilingualism and
Biliteracy

Developing fluent speaking, reading, and writing abilities in Spanish and English.

Sociocultural
Competence

Fostering multicultural awareness, strong intergroup skills, and a positive self-image.

The Only CPS School Using

Reggio Emilia Philosophy

Our approach to learning with our DLE program incorporates the fine arts, inspiration from the Reggio Emilia philosophy, and hands-on activities. We empower students with bilingual skills and cross-cultural understanding, preparing them for a diverse world.

State Seal of Biliteracy

A Pathway for Talcott Students
At Talcott, 5th and 8th-grade students in the Dual Language program have the opportunity to earn the CPS Seal of Biliteracy Pathway Award. This award celebrates students who demonstrate proficiency in a language other than English and are enrolled in a language education program leading to the State Seal of Biliteracy in 12th grade.
 
The State Seal of Biliteracy is an honor awarded to high school seniors who can communicate effectively in two or more languages, including English. Displayed on the high school diploma, this Seal showcases students' multilingual abilities, enhancing their college applications and employment prospects.
