Two-Way Dual Language Program

Bilingual Minds, Boundless Potential - Mentes bilingües, Potencial sin límites

Two-way dual language is a unique educational model where children learn to think, read, write, and communicate naturally in two languages. Approximately half of the students are Spanish speakers and the other half are English speakers. Our instructional model in Kindergarten through 5th grade is approximately 50% Spanish instruction and 50% English instruction. The goal is full bilingualism and biliteracy for all participants, while promoting cross-cultural understanding.

Contact Us

For questions about the Two-Way Dual Language Program, please contact the GBAPS Multilingual Learner Department at (920)-448-7548. 

For questions about a school's program, contact the principal at the Two-Way Dual Language school.

Benefits of Bilingualism

  • Build relationships across linguistic and cultural lines, fostering empathy and collaboration

  • Rewire the brain for better memory, attention, problem-solving and decision-making skills

  • Process information faster and more efficiently
  • Increase in job opportunities; 80% of people in the US don’t speak another language
  • Earn between 5% and 20% more per hour
  • Graduate from the Green Bay Area Public Schools with college credits
