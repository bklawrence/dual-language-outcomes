Experience Sun Prairie

We invite you and your family to experience the Sun Prairie Area School District. We proudly serve over 8,600 students in 4K through twelfth grade. Our district includes 15 schools—nine elementary schools, three middle schools, two comprehensive high schools, and an alternative learning school. Our 4K program is held at 11 sites around the Sun Prairie community. Please visit each school's individual websites to learn more about each of them.
