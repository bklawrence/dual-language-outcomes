Kindergarten Dual Language Supply List

  

Dear Kindergarten Families,

          These are some of the supplies your child will need for kindergarten.  

Please write your child’s name on all of these items.

  • 5 plastic pocket folders (colors red, blue, geeen, purple, and yellow)
  • 1 pair of kid's blunt tip scissors (Fiskars, if possible)
  • 1 plastic pencil box (Size- 8"x 5"x 2 1/2")
  • 10 JUMBO glue sticks (1.4 OZ.)
  • 2 boxes of tissues
  • 1 package of baby wipes
  • 1 package of Clorox wipes
  • 1 roll of paper towels
  • 2 boxes of 24 crayons (Crayola, if possible)
  • 1 box of 12 washable Crayola markers (thick)
  • 1 package of colored pencils
  • 1 box of sharpened pencils (Ticonderoga, if possible)
  • 1 plastic bag filled with a change of clothes (shirt, pants, underwear, socks and a mask)
    ***Please write your child's name on the plastic bag.***
  • 1 box of Ziploc bags (Last name A = gallon size, Last name N-Z = quart size)
  • 1 large backpack (No wheels! )

***Please send your child to school with a reusable water bottle daily.***

Your child will also need these supplies at home for homework:

  • Pencils
  • Glue stick
  • Scissors
  • Crayons

We look forward to working with your child!
