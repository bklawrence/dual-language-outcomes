Our goal is to have our students grow up to be bilingual and biliterate citizens in our global society. The Central community values each student’s cultural and language needs. Our continued goal is to challenge and support all students’ cultural as well as academic needs by continuing Professional Development targeting effective methods and strategies for English Language Learners. SIOP Since 2018 our teachers have received training and been coached in Sheltered Instruction Observation Protocol better known as The SIOP Model. It is our school's and district's initiative to have every single teacher trained in this model and implement these practices and strategies within the next four years. For more information on the SIOP Model follow this link: SIOP If your child praticipates in our Dual Language program they will be in a 50/50 (English/Spanish) instructional setting as follows: Kindergarten & First Grade: Reading in child's native, Math, Science & Social Studies 50/50. 2nd grade - 5th grade: 50/50 all content areas. The goal of Dual Language programs is to enrich, challenge, and educate children to become biliterate learners, not just bilingual. Our school fosters and respects the same goals of the Las Cruces Public Schools Mission and Vision: Vision: The vision of the Bilingual/Multicultural/Migrant Education Department is to produce bilingual and bi-literate students capable of being productive members of our global society. Mission: The mission of the bilingual/Multicultural/Migrant Education Department is to provide each school: a) support and resources for implementation of a successful, research-based bilingual program b) appropriate professional development and c) guidance for optimal use of resources and training and for maintaining fidelity to the school's bilingual program model.
