TWO-WAY DUAL LANGUAGE PROGRAM ALIEF ISD Parent Information Welcome to the Two-Way Dual Language Program! Goals of this presentation ● Provide an overview of the Two-Way Dual Language Program in AISD. ● Inform about the unique opportunity for your student to participate in this program. ● Share program model, goals, components and effective strategies that make this a successful program where students become bilingual, biliterate and multicultural in Spanish and English. OUR PROGRA M Will My Child Learn Spanish ? Will My Child Learn English ? Through hardwork and dedication our students CAN learn BOTH! Two-Way Dual Language Program Offers the unique opportunity to ➔ Learn to speak, read, write and think in English and Spanish ➔ Work with students from different cultures and backgrounds ➔ Learn both languages through different academic subjects ➔ Achieve better academic performance and higher income potential, even further cultural development ➔ Not only be global citizens, but also world leaders, with added professional development opportunities and achievements TWDL Mission & Vision Program Mission Program Vision The Alief Independent School District will Students will develop positive provide ample opportunities for language cross-cultural attitudes and behaviors, and content development through advanced academic achievement and systematic scaffolded instruction delivered high levels of proficiency in listening, by well-prepared teachers. speaking, reading and writing in English and a second language (Spanish).
