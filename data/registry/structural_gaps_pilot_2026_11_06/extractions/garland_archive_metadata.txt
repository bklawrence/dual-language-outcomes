Garland ISD is so excited to give you a peek into what’s going on in our One-Way Dual Language Classrooms!<br /><br />The One-Way Dual Language is an enrichment program where children are developing their ability to speak, read, write and understand concepts in both English and Spanish in all content areas!<br /><br />The One-Way Dual Language program is for native Spanish speakers. It fosters the maintenance and love of the student’s culture and Spanish language in addition to the development of the English language. Research shows that most students who learn in two languages perform better academically.<br /><br />In Pre-kindergarten, Kindergarten and 1st grade, students receive Spanish instruction in Reading, Writing, Social Studies and Science, and they receive English instruction in Math (show language framework information). In 2nd through 5th grade, students also receive English instruction in Reading and Writing. (show 2nd-5th language framework information).<br /><br />Certified teachers deliver high-level academic instruction in English and Spanish to address the social, emotional, and academic needs of all children who are learning in two languages.<br /><br />Thank you for joining us on our mission to provide an exceptional education for all of our students!
