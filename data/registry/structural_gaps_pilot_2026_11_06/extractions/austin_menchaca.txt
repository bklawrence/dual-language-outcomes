Two-Way Dual Language Home Our Programs Two-Way Dual Language Menchaca provides a Two-Way Dual Language program for Pre-K through Fifth grade students. The Menchaca dual language program follows a 90/10 model for Spanish and English. Spanish is the primary language in PreK and Kinder for all students with English added gradually each year. By fifth grade instruction follows a 50/50 model in which half of the instruction is in Spanish and the other half in English.For questions regarding our dual language program, please email our assistant principal, Melanie Lowery: melanie.lowery@austinisd.org
