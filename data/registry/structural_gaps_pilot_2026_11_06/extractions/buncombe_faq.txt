In the BCS K-5 DL/I program, Spanish is not taught as a subject; it is the medium in which core instruction is delivered. Research shows the most effective way for children to acquire a second language is to integrate instruction into the standard curriculum children are already learning. What are the benefits of the BCS K-5 In an increasingly diverse and connected world, the ability to speak two languages gives children a broader cultural DL/I program? understanding, a greater appreciation of cultural diversity, and a competitive edge as well as career opportunities. The mental, academic, and future benefits are sound - critical and logical thinking, problem solving, and decision making are strengthened. Research shows that students in DL/I programs outperform students in traditional language classes; they do as well as or better than non-immersion students on standardized tests. Research shows that DL/I programs benefit a variety of learners, including academically and intellectually gifted students, non-native English speakers, students with exceptionalities, and students from varying socioeconomic backgrounds. What does the BCS K-5 DL/I The two models used in the BCS K-5 DL/I program are described by the North Carolina Department of Instruction program look like? (NCDPI) as follows: ● Two-way immersion programs: These programs group native speakers of English with native speakers of Spanish. The mix is approximately 50 percent of each group and both become bilingual, biliterate and bicultural. Instruction is provided both in English and in Spanish. Percentages like 90/10 and 50/50 are used to refer to how much of the instruction at different grade levels is provided in Spanish versus how much is provided in English. ● One-way/Full immersion programs: These programs typically serve children coming from English-speaking homes in an environment where Spanish is used exclusively until 2nd grade. The students become bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural and are equally proficient in both languages, with near-native fluency in Spanish. It is important to note that schools may slide up and down the scale of immersion, meaning that students may move from 90/10 to 50/50 given specific school situations and student populations. What languages can my child learn? The BCS K-5 DL/I program is a Spanish immersion program. Your child will learn both Spanish and English.
