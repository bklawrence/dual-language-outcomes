Dual Language Program Coronado K-8 School is a Dual Language Magnet School with emphasis on Service Learning and Project Base Learning through the utilization of our school garden. The Dual Language Immersion at Coronado K-8 School is 80/20. All students enrolled at Coronado form part of a state-funded Bilingual and Multicultural Education Program. This program model is the sole option for students and families that enroll as other models are not offered at this site.
