Learn more Two-Way Dual Language Alief ISD’s Two-Way Dual Language Program offers students a powerful advantage: the ability to think, learn, and lead in two languages. In a world where connection is paramount, this program builds academic excellence, cultural confidence, and essential, real-world skills that last a lifetime, preparing students to thrive on the global stage. The Alief ISD Language Programs provide students with: Full bilingualism and biliteracy in both English and Spanish Academic instruction through two languages starting in Kindergarten Cross-cultural awareness and global citizenship Higher academic achievement backed by research A pathway from elementary through high school, including college credit opportunities Increased future career opportunities and earning potential Profile of a Learner Academic Achiever: Meets high expectations across all subject areas in two languages Culturally Aware: Embraces and respects the backgrounds, perspectives, and languages of peers Resilient Learner: Persists through challenges with curiosity, ownership, and a growth mindset Global Citizen:Prepared to connect and lead in an increasingly diverse and interconnected world Whether you want to earn a free associate degree or take your learning to the next level, Alief ISD has a program for you.
