Longfellow Dual Language Magnet Elementary School Longfellow is an elementary school serving grades K-5. Longfellow Elementary is a dual language (50-50) school, which also has a strong focus in the Fine Arts. We have full-time Art and Music teachers and a Drama program that is school wide.
