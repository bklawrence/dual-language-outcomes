Mandarin Chinese Dual Program Home Mandarin Chinese Dual Program We are home to Austin ISD’s very first elementary school Mandarin program. We started as a FLEX program for Kindergarten and 1st grade in the Fall of 2012. Then we opened a 50/50 Mandarin Chinese Immersion program in the Fall of 2013 with 3 classes: a Kindergarten, a 1st grade and a 2nd grade cohort. In the fall of 2021, we became a dual language program. We currently have a K-5th grade program. Immersed in Mandarin Enriching Elementary Learning Video Austin ISD Dual Language Program AISD’s Dual language program is open to all AISD students who are interested in a multilingual academic experience when entering Pre-K (only applies to Riley Elem), Kindergarten or within the first twenty (20) days of first grade, unless a language assessment in the partner language indicates they have the proficiency necessary to participate. The three goals of Austin ISD’s dual language program are for students to: excel academically in two languages become bilingual and biliterate develop positive cross-cultural competency Please note: Austin ISD bus transportation is NOT provided to students who choose to transfer to another campus for its dual language program. Austin ISD Dual Language Mandarin Chinese Program Students are taught Chinese literacy and math & science in Mandarin.
