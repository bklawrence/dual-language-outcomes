About Us Welcome to the North Academy of World Languages, where we offer language immersion and exploratory languages in Chinese, French, and German. We are a K-8 school located in Huntersville, NC. The North Academy of World Languages (NAWL) is the home of the Narwhal. We are excited to welcome our new cohort of students and staff as we explore language immersion together. If you are interested in learning more about our school, feel free to browse our website. If you're interested in becoming a part of the NAWL family, feel free to contact us.
