# School names and campus participation, November 7, 2026

Specialized names are research leads. They do not automatically establish which
students participate. The distinction used here is whether DL serves the campus
student body or only part of it. Multiple partner-language pathways can
collectively form a whole-school model.

`name_screen.csv` is a dated triage snapshot of 94 school records in the
Fieldnotes cohort with multilingual, bilingual, dual language, or immersion in
their names. It records the data before this followup. It is not a completed
source audit of those 94 schools. Seventeen had allocation recorded and a missing
or conflicting delivery classification after incorporating the latest shared
reviews (through commit cb61795); see `priority_cases.csv`. Hayes Bilingual
and Riley Dual Language Montessori were removed from that priority list when
the new human reviews supplied their delivery values. The two records containing
"multilingual" (Yankee Ridge and Cincinnati's Academy of Multilingual Immersion
Studies) were already classified as whole school.

This followup corrects one campus: St. Louis Language Immersion School. Its
French, Spanish and Chinese programs were previously coded as strands because
they are distinct language pathways. The official program description instead
supports interpreting them as alternatives within one whole-school model.
All three program identities remain separate. The correction is marked
**provisional / researcher interpretation**, with quotations explaining the
decision. The statement about the model's philosophy of serving all students
and the description of all three instructional pathways support this
interpretation; the school name alone does not.

The canonical evidence is the archived November 5 official page. A fresh
November 7 fetch retained both quoted passages; the complete response bytes
were different. `sllis_official_program_excerpts.txt` extracts the cited content
embedded in the archived HTML application data. The original response and
capture receipt remain unchanged at their earlier paths.

New reviewed selections apply only to 2025-26. Original strand observations and
earlier selections remain in the history. This review changes no language,
allocation, population model, school identity, assessment data, or human review
credits. The informal Language of the Day statement is not an allocation ratio.

Other named schools in the screen remain research candidates. For example,
Hawthorn's 2021-22 strand record and an undated whole-school record conflict;
its name does not resolve the date of a program change. A bilingual school
name also does not override explicit evidence of monolingual classrooms, such
as the school plan for P.S. 189 The Bilingual Center.
