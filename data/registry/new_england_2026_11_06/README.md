# Final five-state New England structural inventory

Reviewed November 6, 2026, against a **2025–26 operating reference**. This partial inventory contains **101 programme records across 97 provisional school units**, including separately flagged plans, preschool-only starts and funding candidates. **85 school units** qualify for the public K–12 reference working set. It is not a statewide census. The archive contains **85 selected public documents**, including original Word/PDF/image/HTML/JSON responses and acquisition metadata.

School rows describe one provisional campus/institution unit; programme rows distinguish instructional-language strands. Brockton’s George school has Spanish, Portuguese and French records; Rhode Island’s International Charter School has Spanish and Portuguese records. These are not extra schools. Names and research keys are not official enduring programme IDs or automatic NCES crosswalks.

The [Massachusetts reconciliation CSV](extractions/massachusetts_directory_reconciliation.csv) and [JSON receipt](extractions/roster_reconciliation.json) preserve all **75 school paragraphs** from the original DESE Word directory, with XML paragraph positions, raw district headings, spellings, calendar-year claims and programme links/dispositions. It is an undated directory with later plans, not a 2025 annual census. Its Everett hyperlink points to Washington; that model is rejected for Massachusetts. “Pierce Middle School” under Millis remains an unresolved identity, without silently becoming Milton’s French programme or Millis Middle School.

The JSON files are reviewed offline build inputs. [sources.csv](sources.csv) and [sources.json](sources.json) locate original snapshots in [the source archive](../../raw/new_england_2026_11_06/). [retrieval_log.csv](retrieval_log.csv) records searches, failures and unselected acquisitions as well as selected evidence. Search results, similarly named schools in other states, failed pages and generic definitions do not prove programme facts. [Text extractions](extractions/index.json) support inspection; the original responses remain the source of record. The Mendon-Upton chart has a reviewed visual transcription corroborated by the programme’s text page.

[Grade-context decisions](grade_context_decisions.json) retain **92 reviewed school/district name matches or source-supported aliases** in the five complete 2024–25 CCD responses. Grade context does not date programme operation or allocation. Sarah Roberts, Lila G. Frederick, John Silber ELC, L’École Française du Maine and Sackett remain unmatched to that older CCD vintage. The new Boston school is not assigned a predecessor’s NCES identifier. Central Falls Raíces schools use their own directory entries, not a similarly named Robertson school in Warwick or a shared-building school’s identity.

Elementary model coverage includes any school serving a K–5 grade, including Miller’s 3–5 and Fowler’s 4–8 campus. Grade 6+ only hosts remain in the data without elementary entry allocations. Millis and Milton immersion starts in grade 1; their models count for elementary availability, while kindergarten is excluded. A model at one grade does not supply a full progression, every language strand’s timetable or tested-cohort exposure. Maine’s 100% means one of one reviewed qualifying primary schools has a numeric label; its named-share order remains flagged. Later or former policies, core-only percentages, unresolved conflicts and researcher estimates remain distinguishable.
