PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE Welcome to Raíces! We are proud to be the first dual language immersion school in Central Falls! We serve Kindergarten through 5th grade students at Raíces Dual Language Academy at Robertson. We also serve 6-8th grade students at Raíces Upper, located in Calcutt Middle School. We are excited to kick off the 2026-27 school year! At Raíces, your children will learn academic content in English 50% of the time and in Spanish 50% of the time. Our goals are for your children to become bilingual, bi-literate, excel academically, and develop high levels of cultural competence. We believe that all families bring a wealth of linguistic, cultural, personal, and professional knowledge, and family participation in the life of the school is integral to student success. You can expect to have an equal voice at the table and to be treated respectfully by our team. Please stay tuned for ways to become involved, as we need all families' involvement for the school to flourish!
