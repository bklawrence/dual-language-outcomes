Post-Spanish Immersion Program Language Development Opportunities The intent of the Spanish language development offerings after 5th grade are to prepare students to take higher level courses when they reach MMUHS, with the ultimate goal that students will take AP Spanish at some point during their time in high school. The middle school experience aims to build Spanish language speaking fluency, but also proficiency with reading and writing, which are critical for success in upper level high school Spanish courses. Furthermore, the middle school options were crafted within the strictures of the daily schedule and to ensure all students have equitable access to support resources (intervention, etc.). Fifth Grade In fifth grade, Spanish Immersion students will attend all core academic classes in English. For language instruction, students will have the following options: ●​ Enroll in the world language exploratory seminar, in which students experience multiple languages throughout the year (1 Semester French, 1 Semester Spanish). ●​ Participate in an onsite custom Spanish language course that provides an emphasis on conversational speaking. Sixth Grade For world language instruction, students will have the following options: ●​ Enroll in the world language exploratory seminar in which students experience multiple languages throughout the year (1 Semester French, 1 Semester Spanish). ●​ Participate in an onsite custom Spanish language course that provides an emphasis on conversational speaking. Seventh Grade For world language instruction, students will have the following options: ●​ Take French 1. ●​ Participate in an onsite custom Spanish language course that provides an emphasis on conversational speaking. Eighth Grade For world language instruction, students will have the following options: ●​ Continue with French 1. ●​ Take Spanish II/III (Onsite at CHMS/BRMS). ●​ Spring of 8th grade year students will take a language proficiency assessment. Ninth through Twelfth Grade Depending on student results from the language proficiency assessment in 8th grade, students will take a Spanish course at the level they are prepared for at MMUHS, e.g.: ●​ Take Spanish II, III, IV and/or AP. ●​ Continue with a new language offered at MMUHS. ●​ Dual enrollment at a local college or university (Only available after 10th grade).
