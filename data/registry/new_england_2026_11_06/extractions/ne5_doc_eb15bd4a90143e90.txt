Chelsea Public Schools - Caminos Dual Language Program Caminos Dual Language Program Celebrating Language. Empowering Culture. About the Program The Caminos Dual Language Program provides students with the opportunity to develop bilingualism, biliteracy, and multicultural competence in English and Spanish. Our program serves students from kindergarten through eighth grade, creating a pathway to global citizenship and academic excellence. EN ES Where Caminos Lives John Silber Early Learning Center 🐝 Building foundations for bilingual success Grades: Pre-K - K Kelly Elementary School 🐬 Nurturing bilingual learners Grades: 1 - 5 Browne Middle School 🦉 Preparing global citizens Grades: 6 - 8 The 3 Pillars Bilingualism & Biliteracy Students develop proficiency in both English and Spanish, gaining the ability to read, write, speak, and understand in both languages at grade-appropriate levels. Rigorous Grade-Level Academics All instruction meets or exceeds grade-level standards, ensuring students achieve academic excellence while developing their bilingual abilities. Sociocultural Competency Students develop cross-cultural understanding, respect for diversity, and the skills to navigate and contribute to our multicultural world. Seal of Biliteracy Students who complete the Caminos program earn the prestigious Seal of Biliteracy, which appears on their high school diploma and transcript. This recognition demonstrates their achievement in bilingual proficiency and multicultural competence, opening doors to college and career opportunities worldwide.
