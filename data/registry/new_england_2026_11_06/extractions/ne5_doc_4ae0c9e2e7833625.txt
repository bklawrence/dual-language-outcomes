Teaching & Learning » Dual Language Program Dual Language Program For more than 50 years, the Brockton Public Schools have proudly offered a dual language program that empowers students to pursue their education in multiple languages and prepares them for lifelong multilingual fluency. Today, BPS has three dual language offerings allowing students to pursue their learning in English and one of French, Spanish and Portuguese. The Amitiè (French), Unidos (Portuguese) and Juntos (Spanish) two-way immersion programs are all based at the Manthala George, Jr. Global Studies School. The Dual Language Program uses a lottery system for enrollment and is open to incoming kindergarten students. To enter the lottery, please complete the Dual Language Lottery Request Form while registering for kindergarten. You must fully complete your kindergarten registration in order to be eligible for the Dual Language Lottery. Mission The BPS Dual Language programs are collaborative learning communities that promote the growth of both English and one additional language, empowering students to become active and successful members of a global society. Vision The vision of BPS Dual Language programs is for all students from diverse cultural backgrounds to become bilingual, biliterate and multicultural Benefits of Being Bilingual Cognitive: Develop strong thinking and problem-solving skills Social-Emotional: Build community connections, create strong relationships and embrace empathy Global: Participate in the global community and pursue unique academic and career opportunities About Amitiè - Friendship Through Language The Amitiè program is Brockton Public Schools' French-English immersion program, which was first launched in 2018. Through the Amitiè program, students spend half of their academic time learning in French and the other half in English. Students read, write, listen and speak in French and English across all of their subject areas -- language arts, math, science and social studies. The program also engages students on the broader context of the French language by celebrating French heritage, history and culture. Families are invited to take part in their students' dual language journey through celebrations of French and Haitian holidays, activities and multicultural community projects. Why French? French is of the most commonly spoken family languages in Brockton French is the 5th-most widely spoken language in the world French is the official language of 29 countries around the world and is the first language of approximately 8 million people in North America French is spoken fluently as a second language by 6-11 million Americans French is the root of half of all basic English words French is an influential language in the diplomatic space and many international organizations, including the United Nations, European Union, the International Olympic Committee, the Red Cross and Doctors Without Borders About Juntos - Together, Side by Side Juntos is Brockton Public Schools' Spanish-English two-way immersion program serving grades K-5 at the George School. Through the Juntos program, students spend half of their academic time learning in Spanish and the other half in English. Students read, write, listen and speak in Spanish and English across all of their subject areas -- language arts, math, science and social studies. The program also engages students on the broader context of the Spanish language by celebrating Spanish and Latin American heritage, history and culture. Families are invited to take part in their students' dual language journey through celebrations of holidays, activities and multicultural community projects rooted in the Spanish-speaking world. You can find additional information about kindergarten registration at bpsma.org/enroll Students are accepted through a lottery that takes place every May for the upcoming school year. Seal of Biliteracy The Seal of Biliteracy is an award given in recognition of students who have studied and attained proficiency in two or more languages by high school graduation. Students must meet criteria that are indicative of their proficiency. At the elementary and middle school level, students are eligible for the Pathways to Seal of Biliteracy Awards in recognition of significant steps toward biliteracy, biculturalism and bilingualism from kindergarten into high school. Pathway Awards are conferred in grades 5 and 8, and students continue their foreign language studies in high school in order to be eligible for the Seal of Biliteracy.
