LANGUAGE IMMERSION is the teaching of the Massachusetts Curriculum Frameworks of all content areas in the target language. In the program, children learn to read, write and communicate naturally in two languages - English and Spanish. Info Immersion education has been used successfully with English speaking children for more than 20 years. Research shows an impressive development of both second language proficiency and academic achievement by participating students. Time children need Info Within 6-7 years, students in the program will acquire bilingual proficiency. THREE PILLARS OF DUAL LANGUAGE EDUCATION CROSS-CULTURAL COMPETENCE BILINGUALISM AND BILITERACY GRADE-LEVEL ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENT + ifo Students are able to speak, read, write, understand and translate in both languages. + info All stakeholders work to ensure equity by understanding and advocating for the culturally, linguistically and socioeconomic diverse needs of all. This is done through multiethnic curriculum resources. Students are able to achieve grade level expectations in all core content areas. EQUITY AND SOCIAL JUSTICE benefits of bilingual education Increased cultural awareness, understanding, and appreciation. Acquisition of a second language. Development of communication skills to support success in college, careers, and the community. Greater cognitive flexibility. Acquisition of a third language made easier by learning the second. Improved creative thinking and problem solving ability. Opportunity to receive the Seal of Biliteracy upon graduation. Enhanced vocabulary development. program goals Academic achievement at or above grade level in both languages. Positive perception of other cultures, language, communities and themselves as bilingual individuals. Mastery of oral and written skills in both languages. Is the curriculum taught entirely in Spanish? The Spanish content decreases as the students become increasingly bilingual. Name: MJ Date: 3/8/2022 Los lores y los siervos en la Edad Media Durante la Edad Media, los siervos tenían menos privilegios que los lores. En primer lugar, los lores no trabajaban en nada, y daban un poco de protección a los siervos, pero solo daban protección cuando los siervos trabajan. En segundo lugar, cuando los siervos estaban trabajando para los lores, se quedaban en casas muy pequeñas. Finalmente, los siervos compraban comida para los lores, pero los siervos tenían poca comida, especialmente en invierno. Para concluir, los siervos trabajaban para los lores, y vivían con menos comodidades que los lores. Nombre: JG Fecha: 8/3/2022 Durante la Edad Media, los siervos trabajaban para los lores. En primer lugar, los lores tenían mansiones o castillos, pero los siervos tenían casas sencillas y estaban hechas de lodo y madera. En segundo lugar, el trabajo de los lores era de espadachines, costureras y montar a caballo pero el trabajo de los siervos era cultivar los campos y cuidar a los animales.
