Principal Leigh-Ann Evans. (Milford Public Schools)MILFORD, MA — Milford Public Schools has officially launched a new dual language learning program for its students. La Comunidad, the school district's new dual-language learning program, will begin with two kindergarten classrooms at Memorial Elementary School, according to a news release. Students who are enrolled will receive half of their daily learning in English and the other half in Spanish. Kindergartners throughout the school district had the opportunity to enroll. The first class is 44 students, who will receive dual language instruction through fifth grade.
