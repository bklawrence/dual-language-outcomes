Spanish Immersion and International Baccalaureate We currently offer Spanish at the elementary level as part of Ocean Avenue Elementary School’s IB program and in Lyseth Elementary’s Spanish Immersion program. Both programs begin language exposure in the early elementary grades and at Lyseth, students in the immersion program are taught content and language simultaneously in Spanish.
