Dual Language Immersion Program Manchester is proud to be the first school district in New Hampshire to implement a Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program. Now enrolling Kindergarten & First Grade Students for Fall 2026! The goals of bilingual education align with the district’s strategic plan and portrait of a graduate by ensuring our students have access to transformative educational experiences to build cultural competency as well as the academic skills needed to succeed in global society. The district's program is built on the core pillars of Dual Language Immersion: biliteracy/bilingualism, high academic achievement and sociocultural competence. In our programs, students work toward grade level learning standards while learning to speak, read, and write in two languages. The DLI curriculum also supports cultural competency as students learn about different cultures.
