Vision Statement All students in HPS dual language programs will be recognized for having high levels of academic achievement and biliteracy through rigorous standards-based instruction in Spanish and English that enhances student motivation and engagement in their own learning. Dual Language Program Our Dual Language Program provides innovative learning opportunities for participating students, with all all grade-level subjects taught in both English and Spanish every day. The goal is for students to reach high levels of academic achievement, bilingualism, and biliteracy in a safe and engaging learning environment that values multiculturalism while preparing students for college and careers. Is it right for your child? Yes! The dual language program is right for all students. Students in dual-language immersion programs—including those who are native Spanish speakers—outperform their monolingual peers on standardized tests beginning in middle school. Dual language learning helps close achievement gaps for all students. (Thomas and Collier, "Why Dual Language Schooling"). E.N. White and Kelly School host the Dual Language Program for grades PreK-5. Sullivan Middle School hosts the middle school pathway, and Holyoke High School North hosts the high school pathway. Dual Language Program school tours are available by appointment. Please contact the school principal for more information. Enrollment Information for 2026-27 School Year For the 2026-27 school year, students entering preschool must be 4 years old on or before September 1, 2026. All students applying for grades 1 and up will need to be evaluated to determine their verbal, written, and reading level in Spanish. Students must meet the Spanish language criteria for the grade they are applying to in order to be accepted into that grade. Students are accepted into the Dual Language Program through three lottery rounds: Round 1, Round 2, and Rolling Admission. We aim to accept an equal balance of students that are Spanish-dominant speakers, English-dominant speakers, and bilingual speakers. Round 1 is open to all students across the district, with priority given to siblings currently enrolled in the Dual Language Program, students who live in the Kelly Elementary zone, and children of HPS employees who are Holyoke residents. The Round 1 lottery opens February 9 and closes March 20, 2026. Round 2 is open to all students across the district. The Round 2 lottery opens March 21 and closes May 1, 2026. Rolling admission is also open to all students across the district. Rolling admissions open June 1 and remain open for the rest of the year. Students who reside outside of Holyoke must first be accepted through the Holyoke Public Schools School Choice lottery before they are eligible for Dual Language placement. Once School Choice acceptance is confirmed, families may participate in the Dual Language lottery for children in grades K-12. HPS does not accept out-of-district placements for children in PreK. Families that are interested in enrolling their student into the Dual Language Program throughout the school year, for students in 1st grade and above are encouraged to email the Enrollment Deparment to request a Spanish language screening.
