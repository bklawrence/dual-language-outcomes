The impact of an immersion program takes years to build and thus a commitment to being in the program long-term is important for potential students. How quickly will my child acquire Spanish? Throughout Kindergarten and 1st Grade, students build strong listening skills and begin speaking in words, phrases and eventually sentences. By 2nd and 3rd grade students can produce Spanish in both academic and interpersonal conversations, presentations and writing. Will my child have the opportunity to interact with other students in the English classes? Spanish Immersion classes are integrated into all other parts of the school. Students eat lunch, have recess, and special events with their English peers. When will my child learn to read in English? Some students learn to read in English on their own or at home. All Spanish immersion students will begin English phonics instruction in Kindergarten. If there are any areas of growth identified, an educational support team may choose to introduce English intervention earlier on. Questions
