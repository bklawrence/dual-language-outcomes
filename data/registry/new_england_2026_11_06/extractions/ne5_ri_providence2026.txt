DUAL LANGUAGE DUAL LANGUAGE PROGRAM AT PROVIDENCE PUBLIC SCHOOLS WHAT IT IS WHY DUAL LANGUAGE? Providence Public Schools offers a One-Way and Two-Way • High proficiency in two languages Dual Language model enrolling students who are Spanish • Increased performance on speakers and English speakers.
