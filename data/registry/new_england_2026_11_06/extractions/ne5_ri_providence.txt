Dual Language Program WHAT IS A DUAL LANGUAGE PROGRAM? Providence Public Schools offers a One-Way and Two-Way Dual Language model enrolling students who are Spanish speakers and English speakers. Students receive instruction in English and in Spanish every day. Dual language Bilingual Education fosters bilingualism, biliteracy, cultural competence and high levels of academic achievement through instruction in two languages. Why Dual Language? High proficiency in two languages Increased performance on Standardized Test Better memory & attention control High School Seal of Biliteracy 21st Century Skills Prepared for the global community A step ahead in the job market Developes greater cognitive flexibility and problem solving College readiness Engagement Increased understanding and appreciation of all cultures Promotes higher self-esteem Higher levels of engagement Where is the Dual Language Program Offered?
