K-8 Mandarin Chinese/English Immersion Program Dr. Martin Luther King Jr., Principal: Gerald Yung Putnam Ave Upper School, Phanenca Babio-James 100 Putnam Street Cambridge, MA 02139 617.349.6562 mlk.cpsd.us The Preschool-8 Mandarin Chinese/English Immersion Program is one of only two Mandarin Chinese dual language programs in a Public School in New England. It is open to all students in the Cambridge Public Schools (subject to availability). The program is designed for native Mandarin Chinese speakers, bilingual and heritage Mandarin speakers as well as native English speakers to develop speaking, listening, reading and writing skills in both languages while realizing their academic potential in all of their academic subjects. The program delivers rigorous, grade level instruction in both languages daily.
