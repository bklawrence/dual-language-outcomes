View fullsize CurriculumLouisiana's French immersion schools offer students a unique educational experience through the integration of bilingual instruction and cultural enrichment. These schools provide two distinct curriculum options: French and American. The French curriculum, guided by the French Ministry of Education, emphasizes a rigorous and structured approach, with a focus on mastery of core subjects such as literature, mathematics, philosophy, and sciences. This pathway prepares students for internationally recognized diplomas, including the French Baccalauréat.The American curriculum, infused with French language instruction, offers a broader, more flexible educational framework.
