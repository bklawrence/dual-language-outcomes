Dual Language Program This is an opportunity for students to become multilingual by learning English and Spanish at the same time which will increase career and college opportunities in their future. We serve pre-K through 5th grade with the two-way language model. Dual language programs strengthen and sustain students’ connections to their culture and heritage - it is available for students of all languages and cultures. Two-Way Model Students learn to read first in their dominant language in pre-K - 1st grade In 2nd - 5th grade, students take spanish language arts and english language arts math in English Science and Social Studies in Spanish students build fluency and comprehension through daily interactions in both languages with both english and spanish dominant students Contact Us Felicitas Mendez International School 2703 N. At Felicitas Mendez International School, we implement the Tulsa Way for Dual Language model. PK - 1st grade: Literacy is taught in the student’s native language Math is taught in English Science and Social Studies are taught in Spanish 2nd - 5th grades: Literacy is taught 1 hour in English and 1 hour in Spanish every day Math is taught in English Science and Social Studies are taught in Spanish
