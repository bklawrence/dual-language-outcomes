Dual Language Immersion Program Give your child the gift of a bilingual education!! PreKindergarten through 8th grade Dual Language Immersion Program Spanish – English Majority of instruction is in Spanish; ELA is taught by a certified, English-speaking teacher Native Spanish Speaking, highly qualified/certified teachers Limited enrollment: PreKindergarten and 1st grade students do not need prior Spanish experience (Kindergarteners must be 5 years old by Sept. 30 of that year.) Limited enrollment: 2nd – 8th graders must have previously participated in an Immersion program. Open to all West Bank students. You do not have to live in Boudreaux’s attendance zone. If you do not live in Boudreaux’s attendance zone, you will have to submit a Magnet School Transfer form. Bus transportation is available to all students in this program.
