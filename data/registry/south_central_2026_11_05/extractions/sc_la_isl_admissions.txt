Admissions WelcomeThank you for your interest in International School of LouisianaThe International School of Louisiana (ISL) is a tuition-free, public charter school offering K-8 education with a focus on French or Spanish language immersion, international awareness, and diversity. Founded in 2000, ISL aims to break barriers in public education by immersing students in French and Spanish from kindergarten, resulting in multilingual graduates. With multiple campuses, the school serves a diverse student body to prepare them for a globalized marketplace.
