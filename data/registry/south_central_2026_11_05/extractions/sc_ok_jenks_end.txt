The program was established with great fanfare in 2010. It launched at Southeast Elementary School in Jenks and fed to the Sino-Trojan Academy at East Intermediate School. It began with a $1.5 million award through the US Department of Education’s critical language program in 2009. The program was meant to continue through high school and on to the University of Oklahoma’s Chinese language program. The Jenks Mandarin immersion program was the only one in the entire state of Oklahoma. Does anyone have information about why the program is being phased out? I can’t find much online.
