Dual Language Immersion Program The Dual Language Immersion (DLI) Program is an innovative language development initiative that was envisioned by our superintendent. This program will be the first of its kind in Alabama and will support the increasing number of English Language Learners in Birmingham City Schools. Dual language immersion (DLI) is a form of education in which students are taught literacy and content in two languages. The DLI Program at Booker T. Washington K-8 will also develop academic and literacy skills of both English and Spanish speaking students in two languages. Students in this program will receive the same instruction in regular classrooms with an infusion of cultural responsiveness. The program will grow each year and allow participants to earn the distinguished Seal of Biliteracy upon graduation. Research shows that students in well-run dual language programs do as well as or better than their peers on school and state tests, no matter which language they speak best. They also become highly skilled in both languages (Collier & Thomas, 2009; Freeman, Freeman, & Mercuri, 2005, xiv). Because learning in two languages challenges the brain in unique ways, language immersion is one of the most effective methods for helping close the achievement gap between struggling and high-achieving students. Numerous studies underscore the effectiveness of DLI programs: Cognitive Benefits: Research from the Center for Applied Linguistics shows that bilingual students exhibit improved problem-solving skills and cognitive flexibility. Long-Term Academic Success: A study published in the Journal of Educational Psychology found that students in DLI programs maintained higher levels of academic achievement through middle school compared to their monolingual peers. Cultural Competence: DLI programs enhance students' understanding of different cultures, preparing them for a globalized world. Thomas, W. P., & Collier, V. P. (2012). Purpose Dual language immersion programs are educational programs in which students learn academic content in English and in a partner language Objectives English language learners will be able to thrive in a language development program. Native English-speaking students and English language learners will learn academic and literacy skills in two languages through dual language immersion. Configuration: The program will begin with kindergarten and first grade and add a grade each academic year. There are two classes on each grade level at Washington. One class will be a part of the Dual Language Program and one class will be general education. The students will participate in all other school activities. The Dual Language Immersion Program will include one classroom for Pre-Kindergarten students who are zoned for Booker T. Washington K-8 or Glen Iris. There will also be one classroom for kindergarten students and one classroom for 1st grade students who are zoned for Booker T. Washington K-8 School. The K-1 classes will be composed of half English speakers and half Spanish speakers. Administrators will use a lottery if more than one class of students signs-up for the program. Choice: Parents will get the choice to opt into the program. However, parents will only be able to opt into the program while their child is in kindergarten or first grade unless they are coming from a biliterate program. In-zone students will get preference. There will be a lottery system in the event that there are more applicants than space provides. Curriculum: Students in both classes will be taught according to Alabama State Standards. Students will be expected to take the ACAP and ACCESS based on eligibility. Students begin the day with either the Spanish or English teacher. Students remain with this teacher for a major block of time (two or more hours). Specials may be taught in English or Spanish. Recess, lunch, and some specials are often integration times with non-immersion students.
