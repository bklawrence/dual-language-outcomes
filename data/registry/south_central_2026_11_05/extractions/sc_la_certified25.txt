World Languages State Certified Immersion Schools The Louisiana Department of Education is proud to announce the 2025 State Certified World Language Immersion Schools. These schools are establishing and maintaining high-quality, highly effective dual language immersion programming that leads to biliteracy, student achievement, and positive self-esteem. All schools were honored at both the Teacher Leader Summit and at the June 2025 BESE Meeting. Program effectiveness depends not only on the quality of its implementation but also on the understanding of specific non-negotiables, as well as the investment in and respect of all stakeholders. These elements form the cornerstone of a successful immersion pathway. In accordance with RS 17:273.2, the State Board of Elementary and Secondary Education (BESE) developed and implemented a process to designate DLI programs with at least three (3) years of evidence and that consistently meet the qualifying criteria as "Certified World Language Immersion Education Sites."
