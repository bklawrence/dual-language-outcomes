What is French Immersion? What is French Immersion? French Immersion is an educational approach where students are taught in French for a significant portion of their school day with the goal of achieving fluency in the language. This program is designed to immerse students in French from an early age, starting in kindergarten. Core subjects such as math, science, and social studies are taught in French rather than English. By surrounding students with the language in various contexts, they will develop a natural proficiency in speaking, reading, and writing French. What are the Benefits of Immersion?
