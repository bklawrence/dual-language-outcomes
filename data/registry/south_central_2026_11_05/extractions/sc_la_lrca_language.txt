World Languages World Languages AcademyFrequently Asked Questions Who Are We? World Languages Academy is an independently run language immersion school launching in August 2025, offering students the opportunity to learn through Mandarin Chinese and Spanish. Our mission is to provide a rigorous bilingual education that fosters academic excellence, cultural awareness, and lifelong learning. Students will engage in student-centered, inquiry-based learning, exploring core subjects through immersive language instruction while receiving English literacy support. Our goal is to develop globally minded, confident, and multilingual learners prepared for success in an interconnected world. Language Offerings: Mandarin Immersion: Grades K-5 Spanish Immersion: Launching Fall 2025 for Grades K-1, with plans to expand Students will also participate in cultural and artistic enrichment activities to deepen their understanding of global traditions. What is Language Immersion? Language immersion is an instructional approach where students learn subjects like math, science, social studies, and literacy in their target language. Research shows that immersion education enhances cognitive development, problem-solving skills, and overall academic achievement. Why choose language immersion for your child? ✅ Bilingual learners perform as well or better than traditional students. ✅ Bilingualism grows brain tissue (proven through MRI studies!). ✅ Delays dementia by an average of five years. ✅ Fosters executive function skills (time management, problem-solving, creative thinking). ✅ Improves career opportunities with additional language proficiency. Who Can Attend? ✅ Kindergarten & 1st Grade: No prior language experience required! Students naturally acquire a second language through exposure. ✅ 2nd Grade: Students should have high achievement in language arts and math OR demonstrate a Novice High proficiency level in the new language. ✅ 3rd-6th Grade: Students should have a working knowledge of the target language and must pass a screening assessment to determine placement. Curriculum Includes: Math: Eureka, iReadyScience: PhD ScienceSocial Studies: Bayou Bridges English Language Arts: CKLA What Does a Typical Day Look Like? In a typical day, students will learn math, science, social studies, and literacy in the target language with a language-proficient teacher. They will also receive a literacy block in English with an English-speaking teacher. “Specials” (PE, music, art…) may be taught in English. Recess is guaranteed and will never be taken away as a form of discipline.Kindergarteners will have an optional rest time during the day. Time is built in for learning extensions at every level. Does my child need to know the new language already? Is your child entering Kindergarten or 1st grade? They only need a base native home language. The brain of a young child is very ready to learn any additional language with effective exposure, and this is the right age to begin immersion programming. Is your child entering 2nd grade? Your child should have very high achievement in language arts and math AND be very interested in learning the new language OR have a proficiency level on the ACTFL Proficiency Guideline Scale of at least:Novice High in Speaking, Reading, and Listening skills andNovice Mid for Writing Is your child entering 3rd / 4th grade? Your child should have very high achievement in language arts and math AND be very interested in learning the new language OR have a proficiency level on the ACTFL Proficiency Guideline Scale of at least:Intermediate Low in Speaking, Reading, and Listening skillsNovice High in Writing Is your child entering 5th / 6th grade?
