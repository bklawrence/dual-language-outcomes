Frequently Asked Questions How does a dual language program help my student? 1. Bilingual Proficiency The Dual Language Program will include instruction that supports your child’s first language while learning a second language. Students will become fluent in both English and Spanish, giving them an advantage in future academic pursuits and career opportunities. 2. Cognitive Benefitis The Dual Language Program will kick off on the first day of school! Studies show that bilingual students develop enhanced problem-solving skills, improved memory, and stronger multitasking abilities. 3. Cultural Awareness The Dual Language Program will allow your child to develop self assurance, confidence and pride in their native and second language. Immersing students in two languages fosters appreciation for diverse cultures and perspectives, helping them become global citizens. 4. Academic Excellence Our dual language model ensures that students excel in core subjects like math, science, and social studies, while also mastering two languages. What will students learn? Students will receive instruction on the state mandated subjects and goals such as language arts, reading, math, science, and social studies. Our curriculum is designed to integrate both languages naturally throughout the day. Students receive instruction in both English and Spanish, developing literacy, speaking, and comprehension in each language. When will the dual language program open? In 2026, the program will be available to pre-k and kinder students 1st grade will be added at the beginning of the 2027-2028 school year Subsequent grade levels will be added each following year The Dual Language Program is open to all students and we encourage families to consider the many benefits of bilingual education.
