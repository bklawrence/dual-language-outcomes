(ARBELAEZ) At Elm Tree Elementary core content is delivered through a combination of Dual language and monolingual classes in all grades K-5th. In addition for grades 3rd-5th we have Transitional bilingual classes. Each model is designed to support language development and meet the Next Generation Learning Standards effectively. For Dual Language Classes (grades K-5), depending on the language of the day, instruction is delivered in either English or Spanish which is promoting bilingualism and biliteracy.
