Offerings Home > Our Academics & Programs > Academic Offerings Academic Programs at Wharton Dual Language Academy At Wharton Dual Language Academy, we are proud to offer a rigorous and inclusive academic program in which 100% of our students participate in the Spanish Dual Language Program. Additionally, all of our elementary students (Pre-K through 5th grade) are fully immersed in the International Baccalaureate (IB) Primary Years Programme (PYP). These two integrated frameworks provide students with a rich, transdisciplinary learning experience that supports their development as bilingual, biliterate, and globally minded learners. Our academic programs are designed to develop biliterate, confident, and internationally minded students who are ready to succeed in a global society. Spanish Dual Language IB PYP Middle School Program International Spanish Academy Spanish Dual Language Dual Language Program (PK–8) Wharton is the only Pre-K through 8th grade Two-Way Dual Language campus in Houston ISD, offering an 80/20 Spanish Immersion model in the early years that gradually balances with English instruction as students advance through the grades. This program is designed for both native and non-native Spanish speakers and promotes biliteracy, bilingualism, and biculturalism through content-based language instruction. Language Immersion Model Grade Spanish % English % PK–K 80% 20% 1st 80% 20% 2nd 70% 30% 3rd 65% 35% 4th–5th 50% 50% No direct translation – Content is taught in the target language to promote authentic acquisition. Emphasizes oral and written communication, collaborative learning, and critical thinking. Builds comprehensible input by integrating students’ background knowledge with literacy and content learning. This immersive, content-based approach develops confident speakers and readers in both Spanish and English while maintaining high academic standards across all content areas. IB PYP IB Primary Years Programme (PYP) Grades PK–5 Wharton has been an authorized International Baccalaureate World School since 2013. The IB Primary Years Programme is designed to develop the whole child as an inquirer, both in the classroom and in the world beyond. Key Features: Emphasizes the essential elements of learning: knowledge, concepts, skills, attitudes, and action. Supports a curriculum that is engaging, relevant, challenging, and significant. Encourages students to take initiative, explore global perspectives, and make meaningful connections across disciplines. Fully aligned with the Dual Language Program, reinforcing Spanish language acquisition through inquiry-driven, concept-based instruction. Middle School Program Middle School Program (Grades 6–8) Our middle school continues the Dual Language experience while preparing students for high school and beyond through a rigorous, college-preparatory curriculum. Middle School Highlights: Spanish Dual Language Magnet with academic instruction in both English and Spanish. High-level academic Spanish courses for all students, including AP Spanish. Advanced coursework opportunities such as Algebra I for high school credit. Continued focus on critical thinking, collaboration, and intercultural understanding. International Spanish Academy The International Spanish Academies (ISA) offer quality bilingual education in English and Spanish, with the support and recognition of the Spanish Ministry of Education and the Embassy of Spain. Their goal is for students to become bilingual, biliterate, and multicultural citizens, prepared to pursue higher education in either language after completing 12th grade. As an ISA elementary and middle school, Wharton plays a key role in this pathway, allowing students to continue developing their skills in both languages without interruption. For more information, please visit the International Spanish Academy website. You may also find the following information useful: · ISA schools are officially recognized as members of the ISA network by the Spanish Ministry of Education and the Embassy of Spain. · The program provides access to Spanish Language Assistants, professional development opportunities for teachers, educational materials and resources, cultural activities, and partnerships with Spanish schools. · Upon completion of their studies, students receive a bilingual certificate from the Embassy of Spain.
