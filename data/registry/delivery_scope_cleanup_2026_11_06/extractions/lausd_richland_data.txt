Richland Avenue is excited to be home to both a French Immersion Program and an English Program; with classes from Transitional Kindergarten through Sixth Grade. Richland provides a variety of instructional programs including the School for Advanced Studies (SAS), and a Gifted and Talented Program (GATE). The staff is compassionate and focused on providing a positive learning environment.We are a small elementary school located in a quiet neighborhood in West Los Angeles. While our campus is large, our school is small. The number of students we have allows staff members to get know almost every parent and child, giving our staff members the ability to have a deeper understanding of each student. Our campus includes an acre of land that is used as a Learning Garden. Our parent center doubles as a yoga studio for our students.
