City Terrace Elementary School offers both a General education and Mandarin Dual Language Program. We are one of the pioneers of Mandarin Dual Language Programs, with over 10 years experience. At City Terrace Elementary we strive to offer an environment that is safe, welcoming and hard-working. Teachers and staff are highly effective and focus on providing each student the best education possible. Teachers are patient, creative and focused on teaching students grade level standards. We believe in supporting the development of the "whole child" by providing Culturally Linguistic and Responsive instruction and tending to students' social and emotional development. Our goal is to support your child in becoming a productive, accomplished and socially aware citizen of Los Angeles. City Terrace is truly one of Los Angeles' "Hidden Gems."
