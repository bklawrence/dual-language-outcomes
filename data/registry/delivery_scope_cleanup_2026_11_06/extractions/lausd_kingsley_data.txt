Founded in 2005, Kingsley Elementary School is a TK-5 learning community that immerses students in Common Core aligned tasks to prepare students for the 21st Century.  Students have the choice of two program models:  The English Strand or the Dual Language English/Spanish Program which has earned international attention.  Our instructional program is rooted in the collaboration of best practices such as Reading and Writing Workshop, Literacy Academies, Eureka Math, NGSS and Technology integration.  Our program values Arts integration and a safe school climate. Our dynamic teaching practices have cultivated a partnership with UCLA.  UCLA pre-service teachers conduct their student teaching under the guidance of our faculty. We welcome visitors to our campus and believe it is the best way to get know our exemplary program.
