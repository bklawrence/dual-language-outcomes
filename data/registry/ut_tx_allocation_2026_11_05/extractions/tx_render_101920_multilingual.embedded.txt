We offer Two-Way and One-Way programs at designated campuses, providing students with opportunities to develop language proficiency, achieve academic success and build an appreciation for diverse cultures. Both programs follow a 50/50 instructional approach, with instruction delivered in Spanish and English. Two-Way Dual Language brings together Spanish-speaking and English-speaking students in the same classroom. Students learn academic content in both Spanish and English and have opportunities to learn with and from peers who bring different language strengths to the classroom.The following campuses offer the Two-Way Dual Language program within our district: Pine Shadows Elementary Cedar Brook Elementary Sherwood Elementary The program continues into middle school at Spring Forest Middle Spring Oaks Middle Westchester Academy Parents interested in enrolling their students in the Two-Way Dual Language Program must participate in the lottery application process, which opens in November. Families are encouraged to complete the application online during the application window. To learn more and apply, visit Choice Programs. Visit the Texas Education Agency (TEA) website for more information. The One-Way Dual Language program serves students whose primary home language is Spanish. Through instruction in both Spanish and English, students strengthen their Spanish language skills while developing proficiency in English. The program supports bilingualism, biliteracy, and academic success by valuing students’ home language as a foundation for learning and continued growth. English as a Second Language (ESL) SBISD provides English as a Second Language (ESL) services to students in pre-K through grade 12 whose native language is not English. These services support students in developing English proficiency while accessing grade-level academic content. Students identified as Emergent Bilingual (EB) receive English language support through instructional approaches tailored to their grade level and educational setting. ESL-certified teachers and/or teachers trained in ESL instructional methodologies support students in developing their English language skills and achieving academic success. ESL Instructional ApproachesSBISD implements different ESL instructional approaches based on grade level: Elementary – Content-Based ESLAll content-area teachers are ESL-certified and integrate English language development into academic instruction. This approach supports Emergent Bilingual students in developing English proficiency while learning grade-level content across subject areas. Secondary – Pull-Out ESLEnglish Language Arts teachers are ESL-certified and support Emergent Bilingual students in developing English language proficiency through English language arts instruction. These approaches support students in strengthening their listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills while progressing academically and developing English proficiency. Visit the Texas Education Agency (TEA) website for more information. Languages Other Than English (LOTE) Spring Branch ISD believes that all students can learn a new language which is an essential element in ensuring all individuals have the opportunity to become global citizens. MissionTo promote, motivate, and encourage students to acquire a level of proficiency in a world language by providing ample opportunities to enhance their communication and interpersonal skills, creativity, and critical thinking. Additionally, to foster cultural awareness by exploring the differences between themselves and people from other countries and backgrounds. VisionDevelop linguistically and culturally competent individuals who can communicate effectively in global communities.
