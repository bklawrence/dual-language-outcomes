Mission Statement We will empower and prepare students, to become bilingual and bi-literate, and to reach their full academic potential. Vision Our bilingual students will excel academically, linguistically, and socially, accept challenges, and become successful forever learners. Objectives Meet, succeed, or grow in all academic standards in both languages. Develop oral and written proficiency in both languages. Embrace and promote cross-cultural attitudes and perspectives. Greenville ISD abides by Chapter 89 by disclosing the Two-Way Dual Language Immersion program the candidate selection criteria and access to the program are not based on race, creed, color, religious affiliation, age, or disability. Furthermore, identified Emergent Bilingual students shall not be restricted access to the Two-Way Dual Language Immersion program based on any linguistic or academic achievement measure in the program’s partner language or English. Greenville ISD follows Chapter 89 by clearly stating the selection criteria for the Two-Way Dual Language Immersion program. The selection process will be included in our program flyers and during informational meetings which will highlight program goals and expectations. For more information or to see if your child is a candidate for the Two-Way Dual Language Immersion program, please check district communications for upcoming parent informational meetings. Our district language allocation policy is: Pre-Kindergarten through First Grade: 90% instruction in the primary language and 10% in the second language. Second Grade through Fifth Grade: 50% instruction in the primary language and 50% in the second language. Parents or guardians of identified Emergent Bilingual students who do not choose the One-Way or Two-Way Dual Language Immersion program will receive services through the English as a Second Language (ESL) program at their corresponding school. ESL Programs Texas school districts must offer English as a Second Language (ESL) programs if they have one or more students identified as English Learners. Greenville Independent School District currently provides ESL programs and support for students from Pre-Kindergarten through twelfth grade. ESL is an intensive program designed to develop proficiency in listening, speaking, reading, and writing in English. Instruction is given in English and follows the Texas Essential Knowledge Standards (TEKS) and the English Language Proficiency Skills (ELPS) to build English skills and promote academic success across all subjects. All classroom teachers must use the English Language Proficiency Standards (ELPS) along with the Texas Essential Knowledge Standards (TEKS) in their daily instruction for English Learners, as required by Chapter 74 of the Texas Administrative Code. At the elementary level, Greenville ISD uses both ESL content-based and push-in models. For grades PreK-2, Emergent Bilingual students receive services through the content-based model, which focuses on developing English language skills through academic content in English language arts, reading, math, science, and social studies, in a way that is responsive to their language and cultural needs. For grades 3-5, Greenville ISD uses an ESL pull-out model. This model also focuses on developing English language skills through academic content in English language arts and reading, tailored to the Emergent Bilingual students' linguistic and cultural backgrounds. At the secondary level, ESL programs use a push-in model of instruction. English Bilingual students receive linguistic accommodations to help them understand grade-level content while learning English. This model focuses on developing English language skills through academic content in English language arts and reading, in a way that is responsive to their language and cultural needs. Bilingual Dual Language Program The bilingual dual-language program is available to students identified as English Learners from pre-kindergarten through fifth grade. This bilingual program uses students' first language to teach academic content based on the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) in the primary grades, while gradually developing their proficiency in both English and Spanish through specific strategies and methods. Greenville Independent School District uses one-way and two-way models to meet the linguistic and academic needs of Emergent Bilingual students and Non-Emergent Bilingual students acquiring a second language. The One-Way Dual Language Model is for Spanish-speaking students with Limited English Proficiency. The program starts with instruction in both English and Spanish and switches to English-only instruction between six and seven years after the student enrolls. The goal of the bilingual program is to help Pre-K to 5th-grade English Learners become skilled in listening, speaking, reading, and writing in English, while also developing literacy in both their primary language and English.
