One-Way Dual Language Program (PK-6) SCUC ISD is proud to offer a One-Way Dual Language immersion model. This specialized bilingual program is designed for native Spanish-speaking students, allowing them to use both their primary language and English to achieve academic excellence. Our goal is for every student to graduate the program fully bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural. How the 90/10 Model Works: Instruction begins with 90% of the day taught in Spanish and 10% in English. As students progress through the grades, English instruction systematically increases until a balanced 50/50 split is achieved. Strengthening your child's native language while strategically adding English for lifelong biliteracy. SCUC ISD utilizes an ESL Pull-Out model, where participation is tailored to each student’s English language proficiency. While remaining in their general education classrooms for core subjects, students receive targeted, small-group instruction with a certified ESL teacher. This approach ensures every student receives dedicated, level-appropriate language support customized to their unique needs. How We Support Your Child’s Success: We teach English language skills dynamically through core content areas, including Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, and Language Arts. We design our instruction so that English Learners can participate fully and equitably in all school activities and academic opportunities. Our curriculum directly aligns with the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS), ensuring your child meets the same rigorous state academic standards as their peers. Languages Other Than English (World Languages) At SCUC ISD, we believe that learning a new language is the key to unlocking new perspectives, diverse cultures, and a world of endless opportunity. In today's interconnected global landscape, the ability to communicate across linguistic and cultural boundaries is one of the most powerful skills a student can develop. Our district is proud to offer robust, comprehensive programs in Spanish, German, and American Sign Language (ASL). Taught by a team of highly experienced, passionate educators, our courses go far beyond vocabulary and grammar. We immerse students in the living cultures, histories, and traditions associated with each language, preparing them to be empathetic, globally-minded citizens. Step into a bigger world.
