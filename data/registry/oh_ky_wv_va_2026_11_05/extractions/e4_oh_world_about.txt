About Guided by the principles of academic rigor and inclusivity, World Language Middle School continues and enriches the immersion pathway by bringing together a multilingual community of leaders. Our global educational environment will advance knowledge, critical thinking, and cultural awareness in all students. Our MiSSIOn What Makes World Language Outstanding We are an established program with a strong focus on language immersion, serving students since the 2021–22 school year. Our goal is to enrich and continue the immersion pathway from our feeder elementary schools—Columbus Spanish Immersion Academy and École Kenwood French Immersion Academy—while also offering the added benefit of an English immersion program. Together, our community continues to learn and grow through multilingualism, critical thinking, and cultural awareness, shaping diverse and tolerant leaders. Our school community represents a wide range of countries, cultures, and lived experiences, and we take pride in our multilingual community of leaders. Our commitment to diverse perspectives and language-focused learning makes us unique and exemplary in Columbus, Ohio, and beyond.
