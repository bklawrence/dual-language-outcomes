College- and Career- Readiness Standards for World Languages The West Virginia College and Career Readiness Standards for World Languages has set forth expectations for world language study for all West Virginia students in secondary schools. These standards reflect the shift toward life-long language learning. Can-Do Statements for Students College and Career Readiness Standards are written in the form of Can-Do Statements. By using self-assessments, students determine the tasks that they can complete and set goals for reaching a higher proficiency level in the future.
