Academics The goals of Buhrer Dual Language Academy are to provide opportunities for Cleveland children to: Achieve academic performance at or above grade level. Attain high levels of bilingualism in both English and Spanish. Develop cross-cultural, global understanding and positive communication skills. Promote meaningful parent and community participating in the education process. At Buhrer Dual Language Academy, our programmatic model in our dual language program is a 50-50 model, where students take language arts, mathematics, science and social studies in both the English and Spanish language. As an elective course, students are able to take music, art, physical education, media and reading intervention. Our teachers and staff collaborate each year to create our Academic Achievement Plan (AAP), which establishes our instructional focus for the school year.
