Columbus Bilingual Academy NorthOur schoolLearn about Columbus Bilingual Academy North, our mission, and the programs and values that shape our school community.About our schoolColumbus Bilingual North earned the Ohio Momentum Award from the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce, a state honor recognizing schools whose student results are on the rise. For your family, that momentum reflects a team that pays close attention to what students need and adjusts to meet them there. In a welcoming bilingual setting, your child will keep growing year after year.The school is built around bilingual education. Students develop in both English and Spanish throughout every school day. That isn't an add-on or an elective. It's the method. A bilingual school doesn't supplement one language with the other — it develops both at the same time, and students leave with real fluency in both. Children who speak Spanish at home get to learn in the language they already know while building academic English alongside it. Children who come in as English speakers gain a second language alongside their peers. Both languages grow together, in the same classroom, every day.Academy North pairs that rigorous, standards-aligned instruction with a genuinely warm school culture — the kind of place where a child's home language is an asset, not an obstacle, and where belonging and academic growth happen together. Strong family partnerships and a safe, welcoming environment give every student real opportunities to lead, both in the classroom and outside it.Academy North's day extends well beyond core instruction.
