Academics Luis Munoz Marin Dual Language Academy offers a rich academic curriculum to support our scholars in English Language Arts, Spanish Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies. In addition, our scholars recieve instruction in the Fine Arts, Health and Physical Education, Spanish, Financial Literacy, Media, and Technology. Our curriculum focuses on personalized learning to ensure all scholars meet rigorous expectations and demonstrate achievement on all assessments. Our school is expanding the dual language program this school year. What that means for your child is that they will now receive instruction in English for half of the day and Spanish for the other half of the day. There are no options for all English classes moving forward. As a parent, you have the option to have your child remain at Luis Munoz Marin or you may elect to attend another CMSD school of your choice. *Spanish Language Arts, Mathematics, and Science: Spanish *English Language Arts, Social Studies: English
