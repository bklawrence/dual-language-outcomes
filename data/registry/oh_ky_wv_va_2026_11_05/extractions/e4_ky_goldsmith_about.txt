Our School At Goldsmith Elementary, we believe every child can learn, grow, and thrive. Rooted in the strength of our vibrant, diverse community, we are committed to nurturing curiosity, cultivating resilience, and empowering excellence. Through an ongoing cycle of reflection, innovation, and action, we strive to improve every day - for every learner. Every student will leave Goldsmith ready to rise, ready to lead, and ready for what comes next. School Highlights: Offers a diverse and vibrant student body and staff representing 60 countries, speaking more than 25 languages Places a high value on student and teacher relationships. Vibrant tech, athletic, and fine arts offerings during and after school. Students can be in drama, band, orchestra, robotics, soccer, basketball, cheer, step, art, academic teams, and more. Home of a well-supported program for English Language Learners, which includes a Newcomer unit, parental support, and co-teaching experiences Home of a Gold-Level Positive Behavior Interventions and Support (PBIS) Program
