BRIDGEPORT DUAL LANGUAGE PROGRAMS Cesar Batalla and Luis Munoz Marin offer Dual Language Programs. The programs are a form of bilingual education in which students are taught literacy and content in two languages to develop bilingualism, biliteracy and biculturalism. This approach emphasizes equality of educational opportunity for both English and non-English speaking students through a unique educational process that validates and fully develops both languages and instills a mutual respect for both language learners.
