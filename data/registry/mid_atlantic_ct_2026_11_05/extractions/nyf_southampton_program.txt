× Close Dual Language/ Bilingual Program What is the Dual Language Program? Our Dual Language Program is designed to provide English speaking and Spanish Speaking students an exciting, enriching, and challenging educational opportunity for 21st century learning. Students will learn to collaborate, communicate and think critically in all academic content areas in both Spanish and English. By the end of twelfth grade, students will have obtained the bilingual skills and multicultural competencies needed to succeed in our global society and economy. Goals and Benefits: •Develop high levels of proficiency in English and Spanish. •Ensure academic excellence in the two languages •Cultivate an understanding and appreciation of other cultures and strengthen positive connections among students, their families, and the greater global community. •Develop flexibility in thinking through problem solving, conceptualizing, and reasoning in two languages. •Empower parents to actively participate in their child’s education. NYSED Bilingual Education Units of Study The Units of Study tables provided are guidelines for mandated services for ELLs as per Commissioner's Regulations Part 154-2 in both English as a New Language and Bilingual Education programs. As always, ENL instruction is an integral part of the Bilingual Education program.
