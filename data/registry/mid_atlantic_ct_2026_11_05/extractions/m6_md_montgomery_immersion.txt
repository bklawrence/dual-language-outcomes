Montgomery County Public Schools:Special Programs - Foreign Language Immersion Programs | Montgomery County Public Schools | Rockville, MD Skip to main content World Language Immersion Program MCPS offers immersion programs in three target languages at seven schools. Partial instruction in Chinese, and full instruction in both French and Spanish. Students may begin in Kindergarten and can continue through Grade 8. Some immersion programs are based on geographic location and/or give preference to local school students. (Families must reside in Montgomery County to participate.) Learn more about the MCPS Elementary Immersion Programs available for students entering Kindergarten in Fall 2026. The programs are also available for students in Grades 1-5 who are residents of Montgomery County and have experience in one of the three target languages: Chinese, French and Spanish. Hear from each school’s lead teacher, and learn about the admission process in the video below. Individual school presentation information can be found on the schools website. Click here to view the 2026 Meeting Dates. Any student entering grades K–5 in the following school year may participate in the immersion lottery. Students may choose to continue into the feeder middle school immersion program. The lottery is active for the following school year only. If your student remained on the waitlist for the current school year and you are interested in being considered for next school year, you will need to submit a new interest form. Apply Now ~ Aplica Ya The application process for the 2026-2027 school year is from February 2, 2026 to April 10, 2026. To submit an application please click on the Apply Now link below. All applications received after the 4/10/26 deadline will be added to the bottom of the wait list in the order they are received. An MCPS ID number is required to submit the immersion interest form. Please contact the home elementary school to submit the documents required for enrollment to obtain the ID number. Page Middle School Immersion Programs Application Process for Middle School Immersion Programs Current grade 5 immersion students will complete a Notice of Intent form to continue to the middle school immersion program. DCCAPS emails the Notice of Intent forms in December. The deadline to return the Notice of Intent form is early January.
