NYS Law and the ELL student Dual Language Programs In Port Chester, we strive to create classroom environments that support mutual respect, curiosity and student growth. By focusing on the whole child, our DL children have the opportunity to serve as language role models to their peers, to learn about different cultures and to strengthen traits such as empathy and attention span. Bilingual academic resources allow our students to follow a rigorous curriculum across the content areas, pushing them to reach their fullest potentials. With language programs that follow students up through the high school level, we pride ourselves in graduating students with the seal of biliteracy from New York State. Each of our four elementary schools currently has a Dual Language program. King Street school, Thomas A. Edison School and Park Ave. School all have a 50/50, Spanish- English, Two Way Dual Immersion program. John F. Kennedy School currently runs a 90/10 One Way Dual Immersion Program. Students learn content area in both languages and the program is designed to provide a structure for children from differing linguistic and cultural backgrounds to learn together in a collaborative environment. Both programs strive for academic excellence, high levels of language proficiency in both English and Spanish and its goal is for students to be bilingual, bilateral and bi-cultural. Our highly trained teachers create safe, culturally responsive classroom environments for our students, where they are comfortable taking language risks as they grow more confident in their second language. The bilingual school environment offered at each of our elementary schools, supports our goal of showing students that being bilingual means having a super power!
