Overview of Bridgeport’s “Unidos” Dual Language Program

A Dual Language Program or Two-Way Program, is an educational model
that integrates English dominant students and Spanish dominant students
in a classroom environment. The focus of the program is to promote
academic achievement, first and second language proficiency, and cross-
cultural understanding of all students.

Bridgeport’s Dual Language Program is designed to include:

¢ English dominant speaking students and Spanish dominant speaking
students learn together in two languages (English/Spanish) to
become bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural

e A focus on obtaining high levels of oral fluency and literacy in both

languages

¢ Academic/Content instruction in both English and Spanish

¢ Spanish dominant students and English dominant students, will
maintain and develop their language and culture while acquiring a

second language

e Anemphasis on cultural awareness, cultural appreciation, and
cultural acceptance

“Unidos” Dual Language Program Goals and Objectives

Bridgeport’s Dual Language Program advocates the following

goals and objectives:

Program Goals- Students Will:

¢ Become bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural in English and Spanish

Meet academic standards and expectations set by district and state
assessment requirements

e Experience and develop positive cross-cultural skills, relationships,
and attitudes toward their own culture and the culture of others

Program Objectives- Students Will:

¢ Develop high levels of oral and written proficiency in both English
and Spanish

¢ Perform academically, at or above grade level, in both English and
Spanish

¢ Develop positive cultural attitudes toward people of other language
communities and demonstrate a cultural awareness, appreciation,
and acceptance of diverse cultures

© Develop a positive self-image, self-esteem, and self-confidence in
themselves as bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural individuals

* Develop a positive perception of being bilingual, biliterate, and
bicultural, becoming good citizens and contributing to a global

society

Benefits of “Unidos” Dual Language Program

Research data establishes that Dual Language programs prove to be
successful and effective. Dual Language provides students with a number
of educational and personal benefits such as:

Engaging in unique and exciting educational opportunities for
students to learn to read, write and communicate naturally while
working and learning together in two languages

Learning a second language without the risk of losing native language

skills and culture

Building students’ cross- cultural skills/awareness, and sensitivity,
while enhancing their self-esteem and confidence toward learning

Learning both academic/content and social language simultaneously,
as well as meeting District and State requirements

Gaining the valuable and marketable asset of becoming bilingual,
biliterate, and bicultural, for future educational and career goals in a

diverse global society

Taking advantage of the opportunity to learn a second language at an
early age, making them more likely to achieve native-like fluency

“Unidos” Dual Language Program Design

Bridgeport’s Dual Language Program begins in kindergarten. It is based on
the 50/50 Dual Language model. The 50/50 model emphasizes the

following characteristics:
© Astudent enrollment balanced by language dominance with

students from both language backgrounds integrated throughout
the day (Approximately 50% English proficient students and 50%

Spanish proficient students)

¢ Abalanced academic instructional program (Approximately 50% of
the instruction will be in English and the other 50% in Spanish)

© Students enrolled in the Dual Language Program will have access to
the full range of educational services available to all students in the

Bridgeport School District

© If funding is available, a paraprofessional will be assigned and
shared at each grade level

“Unidos” Dual Language Program’s Instructional

Materials/Strategies

Bridgeport’s Dual Language Program follows curriculum and standards set
by the Bridgeport Public School District in the following manner:

e Implements district-wide literacy-based reading and language arts,
math, and other content area curricula in both English and Spanish

¢ Enhances curricula by utilizing texts and materials that are specific
for promoting and developing dual language proficiency

* Provides effective English as a Second Language (ESL) and Spanish as
a Second Language (SSL) curricula and materials to foster second
language acquisition

¢ Adheres to and establishes high expectations and standards for
student achievement, both linguistically and academically in English

and Spanish

The Dual Language teaching staff will implement the most current,
research-based and effective second language acquisition instructional

strategies, such as:

© Sheltered Instruction Observation Protocol (SIOP)
e Integrating language and content objectives

© Cooperative learning/thematic teaching
interactive and student-centered learning
Project-based learning and assessment

Hands on and manipulative learning activities
Technology-based learning

e Peer Tutoring

e Learning Stations

“Unidos” Dual Language Program Student Selection Process

Bridgeport’s Dual Language student enrollment policies and procedures

contain the following elements:

e Aschool program that gives first choice to neighborhood students
and siblings already enrolled in the program

e Student enrollment is based on parental interest, permission and

support

¢ A pre-screening process to assess the student’s oral and readiness
skills in English and Spanish for language dominance

© Aspecial effort will be made to have a balanced number of English
and Spanish dominant students

“Unidos” Dual Language Student Assessment and Evaluation

All students enrolled in the Dual Language program are held to the same
high expectations and standards as all students in Bridgeport Schools.
Students’ achievements are based on the same evaluations and assessment
measures implemented throughout the district.

in addition, Dual Language students are assessed in both English and
Spanish. The following is a list of assessment instruments:

Assessment Instruments in both English & Spanish:

© Pre-Las (K&1) for Initial program placement

¢ Language Assessment Scales (LAS)-(1-5)
e State & District Reading Assessments

¢ Portfolios, Teacher-made tests, Teacher observations/notations

“Unidos” Dual Language Program’s Parental Commitment

The success of the Dual Language Program relies greatly on parental
involvement and support.

Parents are encouraged to:

Choose a Dual Language Program for their child

¢ Participate in the program and interact with other parents and staff
Gain exposure to diverse cultural lifestyles, beliefs, and values

¢ Attend parent meetings and school functions

* Volunteer and visit their child’s classrooms on a regular basis

¢ Participate in school-based decision making policies and procedures

¢ Support and recognize the benefits for their child to become
bilingual, biliterate, and bicultural in today’s global society
