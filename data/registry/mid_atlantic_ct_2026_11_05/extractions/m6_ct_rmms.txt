Currently a grade 1-6 magnet school, RMMS will add grade 7 for the 2026-2027 school year. Over the next three years, RMMS will transition to a full grades 3-8 school! RMMS was established in 1992 to offer the diverse student population of our region an innovative program demonstrating best educational practices within a multicultural curriculum. Along with one class per grade in the Puentes program Puentes is a highly rigorous dual language program where children learn to read, write, and converse in English and Spanish by studying literacy, science, social studies and math in both languages.
