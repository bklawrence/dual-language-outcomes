Windham Center School to close at the end of the 2024-25 school year, and 5th Graders will return to Windham Middle School for the 2025-26 school year. On Wednesday, May 22, 2024, the Windham Board of Education voted to close Windham Center School at the end of the 2024-2025 school year and to return the 5th grade to Windham Middle School for the start of the 2025-2026 school year. These were extremely difficult decisions, but were necessary given increasing operations costs, declining enrollment, and our focus on providing a quality learning experience for all Windham students. In preparation for these transitions, the district and schools have been engaging in numerous informational and welcome activities for our students and families. You can read about these activities here below.
