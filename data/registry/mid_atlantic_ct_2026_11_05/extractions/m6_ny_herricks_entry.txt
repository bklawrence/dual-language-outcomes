Spanish Language Immersion Program: New Kindergarten Classes Forming for 2026-2027 The K–12 Spanish Language Immersion Program at Herricks is a voluntary program for parents who would like their children to learn the Spanish language while also following the traditional Herricks elementary curriculum. Students learn in English during one half of the day, and in Spanish the other half of the day. The program is housed at Denton Avenue Elementary School and is open to all district residents. There are only 48 spots available. Learn more about our program by visiting: https://www.herricks.org/departments/spanish- immersion Families who are interested in pursuing this option for their child, including those with a child currently in the program, must attend one of our mandatory meetings, held at Denton Avenue Elementary School. If more than 48 families are interested, a lottery is held. There is no need to sign up to attend one of the required meetings. Attendance will be taken at the meeting.
