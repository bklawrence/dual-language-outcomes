Monday, October 26, 20265:30 to 6:30 p.m.Virtual OnlyJoin via Zoom Wednesday, November 18, 20265:30 to 6:30 p.m.Virtual OnlyJoin via Zoom Dual Language, Two-way Immersion Program What is Dual Language Two-Way Immersion? It is an educational program that delivers grade-level content instruction in two languages, English and Spanish. The program begins in early childhood and lasts for a minimum of six years. The goal of a dual language immersion program is for students to be bilingual and biliterate rather than just fluent in English. What are the benefits of Dual Language Two-Way Immersion for English Learners? The U.S. Department of Education Office of English Language Acquisition identified several areas of learning in which multilingualism benefits students. This includes improved learning in various subjects, comparable or higher achievement of students in dual language programs as compared to their peers, increased levels of creativity, and promotes higher levels of abstract thought and reasoning. Why did CCPS create this program? The launch of the program is part of the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future legislation’s focal area to accelerate the achievement of English learners. Additionally, priority two of the CCPS Strategic Plan focuses on the expansion of access and opportunities for all students to better prepare them for post-graduation. The program started in August 2024 at Arthur Middleton Elementary School Charles County Public Schools (CCPS) launched its first dual language, two-way immersion program at the start of the 2024-2025 school year. The first two grade levels to participate include both prekindergarten and kindergarten students. These students will receive instruction in two languages – English and Spanish – throughout their elementary school years. After the launch of the program, the next grade level will be added each school year. The program will fully be implemented at Middleton at the start of the 2029-2030 school year. With the start of the 2029-2030 school year, all students who attend Middleton will participate in the program. Book: RulesSection: 5000 StudentsTitle: Students: Elementary and Secondary: Attendance: Enrollment in the Dual Language, Two-Way Immersion ProgramCode: 5128Status: ActiveAdopted: May 12, 2025Last Reviewed: May 12, 2025 Student enrollment in the Dual Language, Two-Way Immersion (DL, TWI) special program shall be determined according to the following conditions: Starting in school year 2024-2025, all students entering prekindergarten and kindergarten and who are zoned to attend Arthur Middleton Elementary School (AMES) shall be enrolled in the DL, TWI program for the duration of elementary school. The DL, TWI program will add one grade each year until all grades are participating in the program in school year 2029-2030. Grade School Year Prekindergarten 2024-2025 Kindergarten 2024-2025 First 2025-2026 Second 2026-2027 Third 2027-2028 Fourth 2028-2029 Fifth 2029-2030 During the initial implementation of the DL, TWI program, students whose grade is not participating in the program shall enroll at AMES regardless of student home language. Starting in school year 2025-2026, if a new student is zoned to attend AMES and the student’s grade is participating in the DL, TWI program, the student will be enrolled according to the following language and grade-based parameters: New prekindergarten and kindergarten students zoned for AMES shall enroll in the DL, TWI program regardless of student home language. New students zoned for AMES in first grade during first semester shall enroll in the DL, TWI program regardless of student home language. New students zoned for AMES in first grade during second semester or any grade between second and fifth grades may enroll in the DL, TWI program if the student demonstrates proficiency in Spanish according to a proficiency assessment selected by CCPS. Students who do not demonstrate proficiency in Spanish shall be placed at Dr. Samuel A. Mudd Elementary School or Dr. Gustavus Brown Elementary School based on their domicile.
