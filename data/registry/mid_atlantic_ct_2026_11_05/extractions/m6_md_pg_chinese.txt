Students learn Math, Science, Social Studies, and Language Arts in a second language (depending on the Immersion model of the school). Immersion Programs schools prepare students to bilingual, biliterate, and bi-cultural. Many of our graduates have earned the Maryland State Seal of Biliteracy on their diploma. Program Features Many students qualify for the Maryland State Seal of Biliteracy Immersion Programs schools prepare students to be college and career ready by learning another language and learning about another culture. Students can test in for any available spaces in Grades 1 and above for our lottery programs. Capitol Heights Elementary School, Paint Branch Elementary School, and Cool Spring Elementary school are neighborhood programs and students must live in those boundary areas to attend those programs. When to apply There is no lottery for Chinese Immersion at K and no testing for grades 1-8 Students must be assigned to Paint Branch Elementary School boundary to participate in the K-5 program. Students must choose continuity from the K-5 program to the 6-8 program at Greenbelt Middle School. The application for testing into the program for Grades 9 and 10 opens in October and closes in March for the next school year. Access Grade 9 and above Testing into the Chinese Immersion Program
