Dual Language Bilingual Education Programs (Compañeros, Dos Ríos & Compañeros Unidos) Windham Public Schools is a pioneer in Dual Language Bilingual Education. Compañeros was the first Two-Way Dual Language Program in Connecticut, launched in 1993 under the then Director of Bilingual Education, Mrs. Jean Romano. In the 2018-2019 school year, we additionally pioneered a One-Way Dual Language Program, Dos Ríos – the first of its kind in all of New England – to better service our English Learners. During the 2023-2024 school year, students who had been in both Compañeros and Dos Ríos came together in Windham MIddle School and chose the name Compañeros Unidos for the Seconday Dual Language Program .For more information about Windham Public School’s Dual Language Programs, please visit Offered Programs for English Learners/ Multilingual Learners. Please visit Compañeros Lottery for more information regarding that process. Please remember to follow the Compañeros & Dos Ríos Programs on Facebook! Frequently Asked Questions: Dual Language Programs Dual Language Programs are programs that promote bilingualism, biliteracy, and sociocultural competency through the instruction of academic content in two languages over the course of at least six years. High Academic Achievement in both languages Bilingualism and Biliteracy Sociocultural Competence Enhanced understanding of culture Social benefits- Ability to comminicate with more people Lower dropout rates for language minority students Competitive advantage when applying for college and/or jobs Greater ability to work in an increasingly global society Better executive function (Brain Power) Slower manifestation of Alzheimer's after onset of disease Faster recovery after strokes Separation of Languages (translation is not used as a strategy for understanding) At least 50% of academic instruction in the target language (Spanish) Commitment to the program on the part of district/ school/students/families (Kindergarten to 8th) Two-Way Dual Language Programs are comprised of balanced groups of English-dominant and Spanish-dominant (or other target language) students and result in both groups of students attaining the desired results. On the other hand, One-Way Developmental Dual Language Programs are comprised of only English Language Learners (as determined by testing) who have the target language (Spanish) as one of their home languages. Dual Language Programs are the only programs that fully close the opportunity (or achievement) gap between English Language Learners and monolingual, English-speaking students. We want all eligible students to have access to a research-based program that provides them with the best opportunity for academic success. At Windham Public Schools, we have both a One-Way Developmental and a Two-Way Dual Language Program. Compañeros is our Pre-Kindergarten to 5th grade, Two-Way Dual Language Program housed at Windham Early Childhood Center and North Windham Elementary School. Entrance is lottery-based. Our One-Way Dual Program, Dos Ríos, is available to K-5th grade, English Language Learners (as determined by testing) who have a home language of Spanish. Students from both Compañeros and Dos Ríos join together in Compañeros Unidos to continue their Dual Language Education in Windham Middle School. Please call the department of Equity and Multilingual Education if you have questions about your child’s eligibility.
