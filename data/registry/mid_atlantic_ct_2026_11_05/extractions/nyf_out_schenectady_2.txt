September 18, 2026 Dear Schenectady City School District Community, What an incredible start to the school year. Seeing our hallways filled with energy, enthusiasm, and smiles has been truly inspiring. I want to thank all of our students, families, teachers, and staff for helping us hit the ground running with such positive momentum. I have loved spending time in our school buildings over the past two weeks, seeing firsthand our students eager to learn, grow, and connect alongside the engaging environments our teachers are creating. I can't stress enough the importance of coming to school every day. Showing up to school every day is one of the most powerful predictors of a student’s success. Regular attendance does far more than just keep students up to date on lessons. It builds the habits, relationships, and confidence that shape their entire educational journey. Building Strong Academic Foundations: Learning is sequential. Each day’s lessons build directly on the last. When students miss school, even just two days a month, they miss crucial instruction, group discussions, and hands-on activities that cannot be replicated through make-up worksheets. Developing Social & Emotional Skills: School is where children learn to navigate friendships, collaborate in teams, and resolve conflicts. Consistent attendance gives students a sense of belonging and helps them feel connected to their teachers, peers, and school community. Creating Routines for Life: Daily attendance establishes healthy habits early on, teaching time management, responsibility, and reliability, skills that serve students well through high school, higher education, and their future careers. Preventing Chronic Absence: Missing just 10% of the school year (around 18 days) is considered chronic absenteeism and significantly impacts reading proficiency by 3rd grade, middle school performance, and high school graduation rates. Every day a student is in class is an opportunity to learn something new, connect with caring adults, and take another step toward a bright future. Attendance truly counts. Shoutout to the Dual Language Education Program at Paige Elementary School As a first-of-its-kind initiative for our district, the newly launched Dual Language Program follows a 50/50 language allocation model, delivering core content instruction in both English and Spanish to build biliteracy, bilingualism, high academic achievement, and cultural competence. Classes feature a balanced mix of native English and native Spanish speakers, fostering peer collaboration and reciprocal language learning. Expanding by one grade level each year, the program is on track for full K–5 implementation by the 2031–2032 school year, after which students will transition seamlessly into the district's secondary multilingual learner programs.
