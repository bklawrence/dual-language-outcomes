About Us Welcome to Fair Haven School! Fair Haven is the largest PreK-8 school in the New Haven Public Schools District. Fair Haven welcomes around 800 wonderful students each and every day. Fair Haven School has multiple unique programs that are used to meet the diverse and individual needs of our students. We are home to a Transitional Bilingual program, A One Way Dual Language program, and three Self-Contained classrooms. As a school, we celebrate diversity and welcome all cultures. At Fair Haven School we strive to be our best every single day! Mission Through an authentic education, Fair Haven School empowers passionate multicultural learners to cultivate their individual strengths and inspires them to be resilient and purposeful community members. ​Be Your Best!Be Respectful - Be Purposeful - Be Safe ​Students at Fair Haven School are respectful every day to each other and to school staff. In order to show their respect, students listen attentively in the classroom and use kind words. Students at Fair Haven School are respectful in all areas of the school including the classroom, cafeteria, and hallways. ​At Fair Haven School, our students are prepared for learning each and every day. Our students are active participants in their learning and come to school with a purpose. In every aspect of their day, the students at Fair Haven School are problem solvers and learn to work together. ​All Fair Haven School students are safe every day while at school. Students practice safety in all areas of the school building. Students maintain personal space and respect the personal space of others. In addition, all students show self-control throughout the school day in order to maintain their safety.
