Dual Language One Way Dual Language Dual Language Programs RCSD programs are designed to help native and nonnative English speakers achieve bilingualism (the ability to speak fluently in two languages), bi-literacy (the ability to read and write in two languages), cross-cultural competence, and academic proficiency equal to that of students in non-ELL programs. The One-Way Dual Language Program is primarily composed of students who come from the same home/primary language and/or background. In Rochester City School District, the following schools provide One Way Dual Language Programs: Abraham Lincoln, School # 22 Grades served K-6 James Audubon, School # 33 Grades served K-6 Two-Way Dual Language The Two-Way Dual Language Program includes both native English speakers and ELLs. The goal of these programs is for students to develop literacy and proficiency in English and in the home/target language.
