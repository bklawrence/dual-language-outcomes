Nuestra Escuela Pittsburgh Liberty K–5 is located in the heart of Shadyside, between Ellsworth Ave. and Walnut St. The school building was built in 1911. There are three floors available for instructional use. Our building has a total area of 52,071 square feet. Our two tree-lined spaces are used for outdoor class time and silent reading. We have two playground areas as well as a basketball court. Come learn and play with us!
