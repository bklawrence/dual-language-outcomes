February 28, 2025 Dear Parents and Staff, We are writing to inform you of an important health matter concerning the ongoing measles outbreak in Bergen County. Health officials have confirmed that an unvaccinated child in the county has tested positive for measles. Additionally, two of the child’s family members, who were also unvaccinated, have contracted this highly contagious disease. The child visited three different medical facilities while infected, potentially exposing others to the virus during the following dates and times:Englewood Hospital Emergency Department-350 Engle Street, Englewood, NJ 07631Date and Time of Exposure: February 9, 2025, from 11:30 a.m. – 3:45 p.m. The New Jersey Department of Health recommends that anyone who visited these locations during the specified times contact their healthcare provider immediately for guidance. Furthermore, individuals who were potentially exposed may develop symptoms of measles as late as March 6, 2025. In light of this situation, the Englewood Public School District is taking a proactive approach to ensure the safety and well-being of our students and staff. We are meeting with our school nurses and registration office to ensure that our immunization records are up to date and that we remain vigilant about monitoring the health of our community. This letter serves as a reminder that all students registering within the district are required to submit their immunization records. To help families understand the immunization requirements, we encourage you to review the following link on our district’s website: NJ Immunization Requirements. This page outlines the vaccinations your child must have before enrolling in school. The district actively monitors students' immunization records through our student management system, Genesis. Please be assured that our school nurses are working closely with the New Jersey Department of Health and the Bergen County Health Department to monitor this situation and to implement any necessary precautions in our schools. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our school community safe and healthy. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact your child’s school nurse or the district office.
