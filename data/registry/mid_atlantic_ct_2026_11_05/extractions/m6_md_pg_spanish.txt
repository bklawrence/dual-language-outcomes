PROGRAM DESCRIPTION Students learn Spanish, while learning the same grade level material as other PGCPS students. Immersion Programs schools prepare students to bilingual, biliterate, and bi-cultural. Many of our graduates have earned the Maryland State Seal of Biliteracy on their diploma. Program Features Many students qualify for the Maryland State Seal of Biliteracy Immersion Programs schools prepare students to be college and career ready by learning another language and learning about another culture.
