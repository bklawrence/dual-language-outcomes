Dual Language Information Session FAQs Program Overview: The Hoboken Public School District’s Dual Language Program offers English and Spanish speaking students an exciting, enriching, and challenging educational opportunity. The Dual Language Program, in a total of five Kindergarten classrooms across the district, is an educational model that provides content-based instruction to students in two languages. The goal is for students in the program to become proficient and literate in both English and Spanish, while also meeting high academic standards and succeeding in our multicultural society and global economy as a result of growing cross-cultural competencies. It is the intent of the Hoboken Public School District to expand this program annually, reaching the K-8 population by SY 2031-2032 . Curious about fluency- if a student has no previous Spanish experience, what is the expectation after kindergarten? Will they be able to hold a conversation in Spanish? Does Maravillas for SLA offer the same evidence-based approach to reading in Spanish for students (i.e. phonics, phonemic awareness, etc) or does that look different in Spanish? Yes, it is the same research and evidence-based program, which is rooted in the Science of Reading with heavy emphasis on phonemes and phonological awareness. Spanish is a phonetically regular language (27 letters map to 22-24 phonemes) compared to English, with 26 letters and 44 possible phonemes. While students learning to read in Spanish need to learn to decode single and multi-syllable words, they may not need the same level of intensity of explicit phonemic awareness and phonics instruction as when students are learning to read in English. Their acumen in Spanish can serve as a learning tool when trying to decipher words and meaning in English. Is this a new program or have been there for a few years already? Research shows that bilingual children have an easier time with the following brain functions: * Developing strong thinking skills; * Understanding math concepts and problems; * Increasing reading comprehension; * Focusing, remembering, and making decisions; * Greater executive control for cognitive tasks; * Higher level of social-emotional competencies.
