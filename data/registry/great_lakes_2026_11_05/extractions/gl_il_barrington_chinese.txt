Vision: We envision a learning community where every student develops bilingualism, biliteracy, academic excellence, and cultural competence through equitable, research-based instruction, authentic family partnerships, and meaningful connections to diverse languages and cultures. Barrington 220 Chinese Immersion from Barrington 220 School District on Vimeo. Chinese Immersion Goals: In our program, students will.. Develop high levels of proficiency in both languages while achieving high academic success in both English and Mandarin. Develop relationships with students and families across cultures and languages Develop biliteracy in both English and Mandarin. Build academic and career readiness through the Seal of Biliteracy and early college credit opportunities (Dual Credit and AP) Chinese Immersion Instruction: 50% Mandarin and 50% English in grades K-5, broken down into the subjects displayed below. All specials (music, art, PE) are taught in English. From 6th grade to 12th grade, students enrolled in the Chinese Immersion Program will take two courses in Mandarin: social studies and world language. Is open to all students; entry is permitted in kindergarten and first grade only. Logistics: Enrollment/Program Entry: Students may enter the program when starting kindergarten up through winter break of first grade with no prior background in or exposure to Mandarin. After winter break of first grade, students are welcome to join, but must prove proficiency in Mandarin. Class Composition: Our program is designed so that 50% of seats are reserved for the host school, while 50% will be filled by applicants from other elementary schools.
