A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal Discover a world of educational possibilities at Mozart Elementary, Chicago's premier dual language school. Nestled in the heart of Chicago, our school is a beacon of cultural richness and academic excellence. At Mozart Elementary, we believe in the transformative power of bilingual education, where students not only master two languages but also develop a deep appreciation for diverse perspectives. Our dedicated educators are committed to providing a nurturing and stimulating atmosphere, where children not only learn core subjects but also develop fluency in both English and Spanish. With a curriculum that seamlessly integrates language acquisition with comprehensive academic development, we prepare our students for success in a globally connected society. Join our community at Mozart Elementary, where the spirit of Chicago meets the richness of bilingual education, shaping the leaders of tomorrow.
