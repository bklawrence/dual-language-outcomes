Dual Language Program There are approximately 60 different languages spoken within our school district. Dual Language Program Our Hawthorn Dual Language Program is a two-way Spanish immersion program for students in Kindergarten through 8th grade. Instruction is provided in both Spanish and English, with the goal of promoting proficiency in oral and written aspects of both languages, cross cultural understandings, and high levels of academic achievement. Students interact in their native language with English dominant and Spanish dominant peers, as classes are composed of students with both language backgrounds. Content area instruction takes place in English and Spanish for all students within the classroom setting. This program is offered to Multilingual Learners and native English speakers. The Dual Language program at the middle school level consists of a Spanish Language Arts/ Literacy Course, as well as a Spanish Social Studies course with content instruction in Spanish. This extended program supports continued development of both academic and language skills. Additional information regarding the Middle School Dual Language Program is linked below. Other Programs for Multilingual Learners Transitional Bilingual Education (TBE) & Transitional Programs of Instruction (TPI) Illinois law requires school districts to provide multilingual services for qualifying Multilingual Learners (also called English Language Learners or ELLs). In District 73 we provide three different models of support: Transitional Bilingual Education (TBE), Transitional Program of Instruction (TPI) and Dual Language Two-Way Immersion (English/Spanish). Transitional Bilingual Education (TBE) or a Transitional Program of Instruction (TPI). These programs recognize that a student's home language can be an important resource for learning. Services may include: English language development Instructional support in the student's home language Academic vocabulary and literacy development Content-area support Collaboration between multilingual specialists and classroom teachers; consultative support Opportunities to use students' linguistic strengths to support learning In TBE settings, students may receive instruction in both English and their home language. The amount and type of home-language support is based on the student's needs and the program model. In TPI settings, students receive instruction in English. As students develop English proficiency, they increasingly access grade-level instruction in English while continuing to benefit from their multilingual skills. Who are the instructors for the dual language program? How are special education services provided? Hawthorn District 73 follows the 50:50 two-way, two teacher dual language program model. In this model, students learn core content through both English and Spanish instruction. Classrooms are generally composed of 50% English-dominant speakers and 50% Spanish-dominant speakers. The ratio enables all students to be language models and second language learners. The English and Spanish partner teachers are linguistic specialists in the areas of content they instruct. The Hawthorn District 73 Dual Language Program has been in existence for over 25 years with a goal of biliteracy and bilingualism, high academic achievement, and sociocultural competence. English speaking students will develop Spanish proficiency at an early age, while continuing to grow in their English skills. Spanish speaking children will develop English proficiency, while enriching their Spanish language skills. All children learn appropriate academic skills while developing target language proficiency. Brain research has shown that bilingual students have the opportunity to develop more cognitive flexibility through their bilingualism. Moreover, students develop social skills that allow them to establish cross-cultural friendships. The Hawthorn School District 73 Dual Language Program is housed at the School of Dual Language for students in grades K-5, and at both Middle School North and Middle School South for students in grades 6-8. The dual language program is open to all families in Hawthorn District 73, with priorities given to siblings of students currently enrolled in the program. Entry into the dual language program is not guaranteed, as there is often more interest than available spaces. A lottery process and wait list have been established that involve consideration of factors such as language proficiency in the home/native language and ratios in the classroom for both size and language distribution. Families are encouraged to attend an informational session to learn about our program. The Hawthorn District 73 Dual Language Middle School Program consists of a Spanish Language Arts/Literacy course along with content instruction in Spanish for Social Studies. These classes allow continued instruction in Spanish and support dual language students in their continued development of both academic and language skills. Students enrolled in the dual language program have the same opportunities as all other students in the district to participate in school activities. All students are encouraged to participate in extracurricular activities and athletics. Students participating in the Hawthorn 73 Dual Language Program have meaningful opportunities to use their Spanish language skills in a variety of academic and social contexts, and with content that is rigorous and cognitively complex. Information shared by the World Language Directors at our local high schools indicates that our dual language students enter into high school language programs with higher proficiency and fluency levels. Students participate in both the Spanish Social Studies and Spanish Language Arts courses. The only exceptions to this may be for students who require special education services as described in their Individualized Education Plan (IEP). Our reporting system is designed to inform parents/guardians of their child’s progress toward achieving specific learning standards. Students in the dual language program are graded in the same way as other elementary and middle school students. In addition, students receive progress reports on their linguistic development. Teachers in dual language classrooms have the appropriate State of Illinois certification. Target language teachers have native or near native proficiency in both English and/or Spanish. Dual language partner teachers work as a team to implement the most effective teaching strategies in their classrooms. Students who require support through special education services receive the support outlined within their Individualized Education Plan (IEP).
