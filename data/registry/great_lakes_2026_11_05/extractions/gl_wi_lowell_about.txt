Honoring Our History, Building Our Future: Building on Lowell’s history and traditions, we welcome the Hawthorne and Bethesda communities in 2026–27. Our Diversity Is Our Strength: Our students, staff, and families bring languages, cultures, and experiences that enrich our school. We celebrate our diversity through family events, classroom learning, and everyday connections.
