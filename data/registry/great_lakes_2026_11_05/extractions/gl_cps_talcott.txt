A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal Talcott Fine Arts and Museum Academy is a Dual Language school that promotes multilingualism through multimodal learning approaches to achieve biliteracy and bilingualism with a Fine and Performing Arts integration. We are dedicated to fostering an enriching and equitable learning environment centered on inquiry and high standards for all. We prioritize inclusivity, ensuring equitable opportunities for all students where mutual respect and growth mindsets prevail while fostering robust social-emotional support and culturally responsive active learning through a partnership among students, faculty, and families. We cultivate a professional, nurturing, and joyful atmosphere which prepares students with competitive skills for the 21st Century to ultimately have a positive impact on our global society.
