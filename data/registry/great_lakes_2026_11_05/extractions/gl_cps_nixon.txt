A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal Nixon is a proud neighborhood school situated in the heart of the Hermosa community. We offer a rigorous curriculum focused on supporting students in engaging in complex tasks, making their thinking visible, and synthesizing learning through writing. We are a proud bilingual school with a Dual Language program beginning in Pre-K. Our goal is to promote a bilingual and bicultural environment that affirms all cultural and linguistic backgrounds. We aim to expose our students to varied enrichment opportunities, including, but not limited to: sports, fine arts, dance, and music . We pursue these goals through multiple and varied partnerships, including partnership with parents, elected officials, and community organizations, such as Family Focus, Hermosa Neighborhood Association, and many more. Visit our site or our school to learn more!
