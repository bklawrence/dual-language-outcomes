Solicitud de Legado Two-Way Immersion/Inmersión Bilingüe de Dos Vías Pathway Model Information/Modelo de Trayectoria Pathway Features/Características de la Trayectoria Parent Participation/Participación y Compromiso de los Padres Frequently Asked Questions/Preguntas Frecuentes What is Two-Way Immersion? Two-way immersion, also referred to as dual language immersion, uses two languages for instruction. It is the best educational model to produce graduates who can speak in two languages, as well as read and write at or above grade level in both Spanish and English. Our program is known as “two-way” because some of the participating students are learning English for the first time, while others are learning Spanish. It is called “immersion” because for part of the day, students are completely immersed in one or other of the target languages for all instruction and interactions in the classroom. Inmersión Bilingue de Dos Vías La Inmersión Bilingüe de Dos Vías (también conocida como inmersión dual) es un modelo educativo que promueve el bilingüismo al enseñar a los estudiantes en dos idiomas, generalmente inglés y otro idioma, como el español. El objetivo es que los estudiantes sean competentes en ambos idiomas, incluyendo hablar, leer y escribir a o por encima del nivel de grado en cada uno. La característica clave de la Inmersión de Dos Vías es que generalmente involucra un grupo mixto de estudiantes: algunos son hablantes nativos de un idioma (por ejemplo, inglés) y otros son hablantes nativos del otro idioma (por ejemplo, español). El programa es "de dos vías" porque los estudiantes interactúan en ambos idiomas, y "inmersión" se refiere al hecho de que, durante parte del día, la instrucción se da completamente en uno de los idiomas objetivo. La idea es que los estudiantes aprendan ambos idiomas de manera natural e inmersiva, al igual que un niño aprende su primer idioma. Pathway Model Our pathway is based upon research showing that students are not only capable of learning in two languages at once, but in fact can excel under these conditions. In the Legado Spanish Immersion pathway, Clifty Creek Elementary utilizes an 80/20 ‘two-way’ immersion language model of instruction. This means that 80% of the instruction in Pre-k through 2nd grade is in Spanish while 20% is in English. Each year the instruction in English increases, leading to a balance of teaching 50% of the day in English and 50% of the day in Spanish by fourth grade. Classrooms are made up of approximately 50% students that speak Spanish at home and 50% students that speak English at home to facilitate language acquisition. Modelo de Trayectoria Nuestra trayectoria se basa en investigaciones que demuestran que los estudiantes no solo son capaces de aprender en dos idiomas a la vez, sino que, de hecho, pueden sobresalir bajo estas condiciones. En la trayectoria de Inmersión en Español Legado, Clifty Creek Elementary utiliza un modelo de instrucción de inmersión “de dos vías” 80/20. Esto significa que el 80% de la instrucción en Pre-Kindergarten hasta 2.º grado es en español, mientras que el 20% es en inglés. Cada año aumenta la instrucción en inglés, llegando a un equilibrio de enseñar el 50% del día en inglés y el 50% del día en español para el cuarto grado. Las aulas están compuestas aproximadamente por un 50% de estudiantes que hablan español en casa y un 50% que hablan inglés en casa para facilitar la adquisición del lenguaje. Pathway Features Students learn to read and write in both languages. Reading and writing are taught in Spanish in the primary grades. As students reach 3rd grade level reading and writing proficiency, formal literacy instruction in English begins. Many students self-transition to English reading and writing before this time. Families must choose to be in the program. Students need the full support of their families to succeed. Their family does not have to be bilingual in order for the student to succeed and become bilingual and bi-literate. Languages are not taught as in a traditional American Spanish or French class. Instead, students are immersed in each language and study the same subjects as all students at the school. Características de la Trayectoria Los estudiantes aprenden a leer y escribir en ambos idiomas. La lectura y la escritura se enseñan en español en los grados primarios. A medida que los estudiantes alcanzan el nivel de grado de 3.º grado en lectura y escritura, comienza la instrucción formal de alfabetización en inglés. Muchos estudiantes hacen la transición al inglés por sí mismos antes de este momento. Las familias deben elegir participar en el programa. Los estudiantes necesitan el apoyo total de sus familias para tener éxito. No es necesario que la familia sea bilingüe para que el estudiante tenga éxito y se vuelva bilingüe y bi-literario. Los idiomas no se enseñan como en una clase tradicional de español o francés en Estados Unidos. En lugar de eso, los estudiantes están inmersos en cada idioma y estudian las mismas materias que todos los estudiantes de la escuela. Esperamos que todos los estudiantes, las familias y los maestros trabajen juntos para asegurar el éxito. Frequently Asked Questions What is a Two-Way Immersion (TWI) pathway? A TWI pathway is a form of Dual language, specifically two-way immersion programs, are designed for students, native and non-native speakers of Spanish, to learn two languages; one language does not replace the other. It is the best educational model to produce graduates who can speak in two languages, as well as read and write at or above grade level in both Spanish and English. The program is known as "two-way" because some of the participating students are learning English for the first time, while others are learning Spanish. It is called "immersion" because for part of the day, students are immersed in the target language for all instruction and interactions in the classroom. How do I apply for my child to participate in the TWI pathway? The application will be available on this page starting in December! Does the TWI pathway span grades Pre-K - 12? Yes, the TWI pathway will span from Pre-K - 6th grade. Once students reach middle school, the continuation of the program will continue at Northside Middle School. Students will have opportunities to take additional classes during high school to receive the Certificate of Multilingual Proficiency on their high school diploma. How is the selection process determined for the TWI pathway? The selection process will be carried out as follows: Students will be divided into two groups: native Spanish speakers and non-Spanish speakers. This group may include students of Hispanic descent who are primarily English speakers and students whose native language is other than English or Spanish. Selection from each of the groups will be made until 50% of the class roster is comprised of native Spanish speaking students and 50% of the class roster is comprised of non-Spanish speakers. Does a student have to apply for the TWI pathway if a sibling is already in it? Yes, however siblings will be given priority consideration. An application for each child must be submitted by the deadline in order to be considered for the pathway. Can I apply for the TWI pathway if I don’t live in the Clifty Creek Elementary School district? Yes, the application process is open to all BCSC families. Can I apply for the TWI pathway if I don’t live in the BCSC boundaries? Yes, consideration will be given to all applicants, but BCSC families will be given first consideration. What are the parent/guardians requirements for my child to be part of the TWI pathway? Parents/Guardians of students accepted into the pathway are required to attend an orientation meeting further describing the pathway model and providing question/answer sessions. Students whose parents do not attend an orientation meeting will not be enrolled. Parents/Guardians must also sign a Family Commitment Form. Will transportation be provided? Transportation will be provided to students who reside within BCSC boundaries. What model will BCSC be using for the Two-Way Immersion Pathway? BCSC will be using the 80/20 model.
