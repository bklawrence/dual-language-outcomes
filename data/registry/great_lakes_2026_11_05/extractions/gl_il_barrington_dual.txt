Vision: We envision a learning community where every student develops bilingualism, biliteracy, academic excellence, and cultural competence through equitable, research-based instruction, authentic family partnerships, and meaningful connections to diverse languages and cultures. Barrington 220 Spanish Dual Language Program from Barrington 220 School District on Vimeo. Dual Language Goals: In our program, students will.. Develop high levels of proficiency in both languages while achieving high academic success in both English and Spanish. Strengthen family connections and maintain their heritage language. Develop relationships with students and families across cultures and languages Develop biliteracy in both English and Spanish. Build academic and career readiness through the Seal of Biliteracy and early college credit opportunities (Dual Credit and AP) Dual Language Instruction: Begins in kindergarten with 80% Spanish and 20% English, gradually adding more English in subsequent grades First grade instruction is delivered 70% in Spanish and 30% in English. Second grade instruction is delivered 60% in Spanish and 40% in English. Third - Fifth grade instruction is delivered 50% in each language. Provides the highest quality, research based instruction for students learning English as a second language Has been shown to close the achievement gap for all underserved populations while offering enrichment to English speaking students Allows students to gain true, marketable fluency in two languages while engaging in challenging curricula. Is open to all students; For English learners, they can enter at any time. For students whose home language is English, they can enter in Kindergarten and 1st grade For students whose home language is English, students need to demonstrate Spanish proficiency for enrollment Logistics: Enrollment/Program Entry: All students in the district are eligible for Dual Language. The enrollment process varies for English Learners and Spanish Learners. English Learners: English learners at Sunny Hill will automatically be offered One-Way Dual Language. They can apply to enroll in Two-Way based on availability. English learners at all other Elementary Sites can apply for Two-Way Dual Language based on availability or receive part time ESL support at their home school. Non-English Learners (Spanish Learners): For students whose home language is not Spanish, they are invited to join the program in Kindergarten/1st grade, in order to develop a base of the target language (Spanish). Students who are coming from outside the district and have attended Spanish/English Dual Language programs previously will be considered to enter after 1st grade. We refer to students for whom Spanish is not their home language as Spanish Learners.
