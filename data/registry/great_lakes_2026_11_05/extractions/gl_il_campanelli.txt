Springinsguth Road, Schaumburg, IL 60193 About Campanelli Home to more than 500 vibrant kindergartens through sixth-grade learners, Campanelli School is a dynamic educational hub where neighborhood spirit meets world-class opportunity. We proudly offer a unique tapestry of programs designed to spark curiosity, build global citizens, and instill a lifelong love of learning. At Campanelli, families connect and students thrive across three specialized academic pathways; neighborhood classrooms, the District 54 Chinese Immersion Program and the District 54 Magnet Program. All students engage in a robust, innovative curriculum that empowers every student to reach their full potential. Our highly acclaimed immersion model fosters authentic bilingualism and biliteracy while cultivating a rich foundation in Chinese culture. Students don't just learn the language, they live it through core academics. In Chinese, students master Mathematics, Science, and Literacy, building incredible cognitive flexibility and problem-solving skills. In English, students dive into Social Studies and Literacy, ensuring a beautifully balanced, comprehensive academic profile. Education at Campanelli extends far beyond the classroom. Our exceptional faculty is dedicated to nurturing well-rounded individuals by inspiring leadership, teamwork, and intellectual curiosity through high-energy extracurriculars including: Student Ambassadors & Safety Patrols: Cultivating tomorrow's leaders and campus role models today. Art & Garden Club: Empowering students to express themselves artistically and positively impact our Earth. Math Olympiad & Battle of the Books: Challenging analytical thinkers and avid readers to collaborate and compete. Check out our club offerings for opportunities for your children to enjoy! Our partnerships with parents are vital to the success of the whole child. Campanelli staff work collaboratively with families to support the academic and social emotional needs of all children. Together we ensure student success. Through our partnership with the Schaumburg Park District, Campanelli offers the KASPER program; providing safe, engaging, and convenient before- and after-school childcare right here on campus. You can learn more about Kasper through the Schaumburg Park District or by calling our school office.
