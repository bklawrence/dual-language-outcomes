Two-Way Dual Language Program Bilingual Minds, Boundless Potential - Mentes bilingües, Potencial sin límites Two-way dual language is a unique educational model where children learn to think, read, write, and communicate naturally in two languages. Approximately half of the students are Spanish speakers and the other half are English speakers. Our instructional model in Kindergarten through 5th grade is approximately 50% Spanish instruction and 50% English instruction. The goal is full bilingualism and biliteracy for all participants, while promoting cross-cultural understanding.
