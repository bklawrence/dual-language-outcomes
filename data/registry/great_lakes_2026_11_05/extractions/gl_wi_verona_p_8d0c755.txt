Two-Way Spanish Immersion The Verona Area School District’s Two-Way Immersion (TWI) program is a dual language program in which Spanish and English are used for instruction to develop language proficiency in both languages. Developed yearly since it started at the Kindergarten level in 2013, the TWI program has reached 10th grade at Verona Area High School as of 2024. In elementary school, TWI students spend half their day with a teacher who teaches in Spanish and the other half with a teacher who teaches in English. They learn literacy in both languages, math primarily in English with numeracy and concept practice in Spanish, and science and social studies primarily in Spanish with concepts integrated into literacy in both languages. Students may continue learning in Spanish in middle and high school by taking two courses in Spanish and the rest of their courses in English. Purpose Who Can Enroll Where is the TWI Program Offered The goals of TWI are biliteracy and biculturalism, high academic achievement, and sociocultural competency. TWI classrooms maintain a balanced number of multilingual learners and students who primarily speak English in their homes. TWI students learn academic content in Spanish and English from their teachers while they simultaneously learn both languages socially from each other. VASD resident families can submit a request for their child to attend the TWI program and selection will be based on a lottery system to help ensure the TWI program maintains a balanced class composition. Families apply and may be accepted to TWI for incoming Kindergartners from mid-January through March 9th at 4pm for the following Fall. The elementary TWI program is offered at Glacier Edge Elementary School for students who reside in the Glacier Edge and Stoner Prairie attendance areas and Sugar Creek Elementary School for students who reside in the Sugar Creek and Country View attendance areas. The secondary TWI program is offered at Badger Ridge Middle School and at the Verona Area High School. Beginning in the 2026-27 school year, TWI will be offered for Glacier Edge TWI students entering 6th grade at Savanna Oaks Middle School. Over the following two years (2027-28 and 2028-29), 7th and 8th will be added at Savanna Oaks to provide TWI at all three grades.
