A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal Schubert, located in the Belmont-Cragin Community, offers a general education program that includes a Dual Language Program from PK-5. Universal Breakfast is provided to students and the school community. Schubert partners with various community organizations from Chicago Fire to Vocel for Early Childhood. We also have a partnership with Brightpoint that provides wrap around supports through two community navigators and an onsite therapist for students and families. Schubert’s vision is to nurture students so they become lifelong learners.
