A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal Welcome to Sabin Dual Language Magnet School—home of the Sabin Hawks! For over 110 years, our PreK–8th grade school has proudly served our community through robust English Magnet and district-recognized Dual Language tracks, preparing students for lifelong success and the Seal of Biliteracy. At Sabin, our classrooms are sanctuaries founded on the ideas of love and joy. We meet students where they are at and work closely with every child to ensure their innate greatness is reached. Through small class sizes, high academic expectations, embedded music and visual arts, and championship-caliber sports, we foster a dynamic environment where every learner feels safe, valued, and inspired. To support our families, we offer free before- and after-school programming Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 5:45 PM, rooted in academics, enrichment, arts, sports, and mentorship. By honoring every student's unique identity, we are proud to raise the next generation of game-changers and compassionate leaders ready to transform our world.
