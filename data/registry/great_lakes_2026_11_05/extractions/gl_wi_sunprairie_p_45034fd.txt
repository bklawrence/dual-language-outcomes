To prepare our learners to be biliterate, culturally inclusive, global citizens. ¡El Distrito Escolar del Área de Sun Prairie se enorgullece de ofrecer un programa bilingüe de doble inmersión (DLI) en escuelas selectas! Actualmente se ofrece el programa a tres generaciones de estudiantes en kindergarten a segundo grado. Para el año escolar 2026-27, se ofrecerá el programa DLI en las escuelas Northside Elementary y Meadow View Elementary. La nueva generación de kindergarten se formará con familias interesadas de ambas escuelas. Además, todos los estudiantes de kindergarten de hogares donde se habla español en todo el distrito ahora en adelante también tendrán la oportunidad de participar en estos programas en Northside o Meadow View. SPASD tiene un compromiso firme con el desarrollo de la educación bilingüe desde kindergarten hasta el 12.º grado. Como parte de esta visión, recientemente anunciamos nuestra trayectoria de secundaria en Central Heights Middle School, la cual recibirá a su primera generación de estudiantes DLI en el año escolar 2029-30. Síguenos en facebook The Sun Prairie Area School District is proud to offer a bilingual Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program in select schools! Currently the program is offered to three generations of students in Kindergarten to Second Grade. For the 2026-27 school year, the DLI program will be offered at Northside Elementary and Meadow View Elementary. The new generation of kindergarten will be formed with interested families from both schools. Additionally, all kindergarten students from Spanish-speaking households in any attendance area across the district will now also have the opportunity to participate in these programs at Northside or Meadow View. SPASD has a firm commitment to the development of bilingual education from kindergarten through 12th grade. As part of this vision, we recently announced our middle school pathway at Central Heights Middle School, which will welcome its first cohort of DLI students in the 2029-30 school year. Meeting Details: Agendas, Presentations, and Summaries Middle School Announcement The Bilingual Education Task Force is a collaborative partnership of families, staff, and community members dedicated to shaping the future of high-quality bilingual education in the Sun Prairie Area School District. The group provides essential leadership and diverse perspectives to guide our bilingual Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program, with a current focus on recommending a permanent middle school site for the 2029-30 school year. By fostering this community connection, the task force ensures that our programs continue to grow and prepare students to be biliterate, culturally inclusive global citizens. Reach out to Tony Jennaro, Coordinator of Multilingual Education, if you are interested in joining the task force.
