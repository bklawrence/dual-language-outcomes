A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal Welcome to Belmont-Cragin Elementary School and Early Childhood Center, nestled on Chicago's vibrant Northwest Side. Our school is dedicated to the growth and development of approximately 525 students, spanning from PreK through 8th grade. This includes students from diverse backgrounds, including English Language Learners and those with disabilities. At Belmont-Cragin, we take pride in offering a wide range of educational programs to meet the unique needs and interests of our students. Such as our Reggio Inspired Preschool, Established Dual Language Program, and our Fine and Performing Arts Education Program. As a member of our school community, your son/daughter will experience a supportive environment where the unique talents of every student are celebrated. Our commitment extends beyond providing a strong academic foundation to fostering a sense of community and belonging. We are dedicated to ensuring that every students not only excels academically but also feels a deep sense of connection and inclusion within our school.
