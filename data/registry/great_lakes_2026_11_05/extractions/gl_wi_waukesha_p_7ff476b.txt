We believe that ALL students shall be provided with a high quality comprehensible education that values and validates students’ languages and cultures to ensure academic, linguistic and socio-cultural access in the global 21st century. What is Dual Language? The Dual Language program brings together students learning English and students learning Spanish with the shared goal of developing strong bilingualism and biliteracy at high academic levels. Dual Language programs value and leverage linguistic and cultural diversity as essential assets for success in 21st-century college, career, and community settings. Program Goals To graduate Multiculturally and Globally Competent students Biliteracy and Bilingualism: Students will read, write and speak at or above grade level in TWO languages (English and Spanish) Students will demonstrate Academic Achievement at or above grade level in math, science and social studies in TWO languages (English and Spanish) Benefits for ALL Exposure to a second language early on Exposure to other people who may not look the same (cross-cultural friendships) Deeper literacy skills Enhances executive functioning and metalinguistic awareness Competitive edge with college and career opportunities Native Spanish Speakers Dual Language serves as the ESL model for Native Spanish Speakers and is required by federal law Strong identify, pride and cultural connection Preserving family ties Positioned as language models Native English Speakers Natural language acquisition of a second language Empathy and perspective taking Sometimes a learner, sometimes an expert Develop native-like pronunciation (accent) The Dual Language Bilingual Program is an Immersion Program. Immersion in the program’s community minority language (Spanish) with a gradual increase in the amount of instruction in English is key to developing bilingualism, biliteracy, and academic achievement for all of our learners. Research is conclusive that English Learning students who learn in their first language, have the greatest likelihood of being proficient bilinguals in English and Spanish. English proficient students have a great opportunity for bilingualism in the dual program, as we can capitalize early brain development in acquiring a second language.Our 80:20 Model of Language Allocation is detailed below: Language Allocation Plan Framework: Gr. 5K - 11 We emphasize building a community of learners. Students work in partners called Bilingual Pairs, are prompted to practice speaking, interacting, and collaborating before engaging individually, and are always seeking connections between how their languages interact in their brains. Starting in 4-year-old kindergarten, students engage with their teachers and classmates in an environment where Spanish is the modeled instructional language and the mode of communication between the teacher and students and between the students. Students attend Monday to Thursday full days with their bilingual teacher and a full-time bilingual teaching assistant. All foundational lessons are conducted in Spanish to build early learning skills in Spanish literacy and numeracy, social-emotional wellbeing, and community culture. In our elementary schools, kindergarten through 5th graders increase their Spanish instruction from 80:20 in Kindergarten and 1st grade to 60:40 in 2nd grade through 5th grade. The dual program continues through middle and high school. See the language allocation plan above for details. The following schools offer our dual language program: Banting, Heyer and Lowell Elementary Schools, Les Paul and Butler Middle Schools and North, South and West High Schools.
