Dual Language Education What is Dual Language Education (DLE)? In a Dual Language Education (DLE) setting, students learn literacy and content in two languages. At Talcott, our program integrates Spanish and English from Pre-K through eighth grade, guided by three pillars of bilingualism: High Academic Achievement Ensuring students meet or exceed grade-level standards in both languages Bilingualism andBiliteracy Developing fluent speaking, reading, and writing abilities in Spanish and English. SocioculturalCompetence Fostering multicultural awareness, strong intergroup skills, and a positive self-image. The Only CPS School UsingReggio Emilia Philosophy Our approach to learning with our DLE program incorporates the fine arts, inspiration from the Reggio Emilia philosophy, and hands-on activities. We empower students with bilingual skills and cross-cultural understanding, preparing them for a diverse world. This award celebrates students who demonstrate proficiency in a language other than English and are enrolled in a language education program leading to the State Seal of Biliteracy in 12th grade. The State Seal of Biliteracy is an honor awarded to high school seniors who can communicate effectively in two or more languages, including English. Displayed on the high school diploma, this Seal showcases students' multilingual abilities, enhancing their college applications and employment prospects. Learn More
