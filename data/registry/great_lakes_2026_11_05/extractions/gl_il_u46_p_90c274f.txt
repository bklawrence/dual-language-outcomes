U-46 Dual Language Program School District U-46 is proud to offer an award-winning Spanish-English Dual Language program that develops bilingualism, biliteracy, high academic achievement, and cross-cultural competence. The program brings together students from diverse linguistic backgrounds, including English Learners and native English speakers, to learn academic content while developing proficiency in both English and Spanish. U-46 implements an 80:20 Dual Language instructional model, beginning with 80% Spanish and 20% English instruction in kindergarten and gradually increasing English instruction as students progress through the grades. This research-based approach intentionally develops both languages while providing students with opportunities to make meaningful connections across languages and cultures. Offered across elementary, middle, and high schools, the program provides students with a pathway toward bilingualism and biliteracy, preparing them for future academic, college, and career opportunities, including the opportunity to earn the Illinois State Seal of Biliteracy. At its core, U-46's Dual Language program reflects our commitment to educational equity, academic excellence, and preparing students to thrive in an increasingly multilingual and multicultural world. Día de Logros El día de logros is a Multilingual and Multicultural Education (MME) celebration in which MME seniors are celebrated for their achievements. During this celebration, MME seniors are awarded a certificate and a stole that they can wear during the celebration and their graduation ceremony. Additionally, selected high-school freshmen, sophomore, and junior students in the MME programs are celebrated for their accomplishments. Each comprehensive U-46 High School designates a date and time for this celebration.
