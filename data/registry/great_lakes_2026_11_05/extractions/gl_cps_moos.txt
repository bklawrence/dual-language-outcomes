A Message from the Principal A Message from the Principal At Moos, we are curious and inclusive problem solvers, committed to academic and personal growth striving to make a positive impact on the world. Moos is a PreK- 8th grade school offering Dual Language and full day Pre-K. As an International Baccalaureate World School, Moos Elementary delivers a strong, core academic curriculum, richly enhanced by fine and performing arts classes - including Art, Spanish, Music, and Physical Education.
