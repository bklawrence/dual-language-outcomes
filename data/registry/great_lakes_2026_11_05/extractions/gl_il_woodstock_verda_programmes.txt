Programs The Verda Dierzen Early Learning Center (VDELC) serves children from birth through Kindergarten. All children in District 200 attend Kindergarten at VDELC before moving on to their neighborhood schools for first grade. Our programs include: Full-Day Kindergarten Dual Language Kindergarten PreK Tution-Based PreK Early Childhood Special Education Celebrate Families Birth to 3 Program Explore the pages of this website for information on each of our programs, links to resources for parents, and details about our school and our district.
