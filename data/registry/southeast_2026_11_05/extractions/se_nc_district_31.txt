It’s all about what you and your child need and what kind of learning environment excites and supports them. Our offerings make GCS a national leader in educational choice and unlock every student’s potential with academic and extracurricular paths that build each student’s success. What we mean by choice Every family has choices at GCS, whether your student is gifted, interested in a trade, homeschooled, loves a specific subject like music or robotics — and even if they want to fly! From PreK/Kindergarten through senior year, students have all kinds of paths open to them. Take time to learn about the many possibilities — from your outstanding neighborhood community school to exploring our Choice Programs and Schools, CTE programs, dual enrollment offerings and more. What are the main types of schools and programs available to me? Get to know the different types of schools and paths we offer. Then, see what’s available for your child’s age and grade level.
