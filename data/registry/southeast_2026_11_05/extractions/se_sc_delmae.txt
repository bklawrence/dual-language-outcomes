Contact Us Spanish at Home Apply The Spanish Language Immersion Program began at Delmae Elementary School in the Fall of 2016. We are the first school in Florence One Schools to offer this type of language instruction to students. Students entering kindergarten are eligible to be enrolled into the program based on availability. Students have the opportunity to grow with the program throughout their years at Delmae, becoming increasingly fluent in both English and Spanish. Dual Language Immersion Model The Dual Language Immersion Program focuses on three core pillars: bilingualism and biliteracy, high academic achievement, and cross-cultural competence. Under Florence One’s 50:50 elementary model, students spend 50% of their academic school day learning core subject content in the target language (Spanish). All immersion instruction remains fully grounded in South Carolina academic standards. Co-Teaching Structure Elementary instruction relies on a collaborative, two-teacher team. One teacher, fluent in the target language, leads math and science. The second teacher delivers English language arts and social studies in English. The two instructors swap student groups at a scheduled point during the day, ensuring every child receives daily instruction across both languages to build dual-language fluency. Literacy & Learning Goals Students develop the 4 domains of language—listening, speaking, reading, and writing—in both English and the world language by graduation through exploration of core subjects in elementary school and language-specific literacy instruction in middle and high school. K–12 Pathway & Graduation Florence One supports a complete 5K–12 immersion pathway. As students transition to middle and high school, they follow their designated cohort's school track and engage in advanced interdisciplinary studies in their second language through high school honors Spanish courses and dual credit courses in partnership with Francis Marion University. Why Choose Language Immersion? ● Sharper Cognitive Skills: Navigating two languages strengthens brain function—improving focus, memory, problem-solving, and adaptability while simultaneously enriching native language skills. ● Superior Academic Performance: Immersion students consistently match or outperform their peers on standard English and math assessments, regardless of the test language. ● Closes Achievement Gaps: The mental rigor of dual-language learning makes immersion one of the most effective educational models for closing performance gaps among diverse student groups. ● Fluency & Biliteracy: Immersion delivers unmatched language mastery, empowering K–12 students to read, write, and speak with fluency in a second language by graduation. ● Global Career Readiness: With over 1,100 international businesses based in South Carolina, bilingualism gives students a clear competitive edge in tomorrow’s workforce. ● Cross-Cultural Mindset: Immersion builds empathy, cultural appreciation, and effective communication skills, preparing students to collaborate seamlessly across borders.
