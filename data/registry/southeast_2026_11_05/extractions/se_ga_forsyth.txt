Preference will be given to students that are zoned to the school. If there are slots available we will consider applications from students zoned to other FCS elementary schools for out-of-district. Applications for all interested families will be posted on April 10, 2023 on the district website: www.forsyth.k12.ga.us/dli. Parents can fill out this interest form to receive regular updates on the application process. Forsyth County Schools Offering DLI: Brandywine Elementary Cumming Elementary Kelly Mill Elementary Mashburn Elementary DLI Parent Information Nights DLI schools will host combined Kindergarten Parent Orientation and Dual Language Immersion Parent Information Nights in May 2023. Parents interested in Dual Language Immersion are encouraged to attend one of the events and bring their questions! DLI Parent Advisory Council Each DLI school has elected parent representatives to serve on the district DLI Parent Advisory Council. The DLI PAC meets twice a year to learn and share about the DLI programs at each school. Parent representatives also meet with administrators to plan events to build the DLI community at their schools. Recent topics at the DLI PAC meetings included development of language proficiency in the second language in DLI and the DLI model for articulation into middle school. DLI parents are encouraged to reach out to their school's DLI PAC representatives to learn more and to share ideas and suggestions. Planning for DLI Transition to Middle Schools The planning for DLI students to transition to select middle schools has begun. The first cohort of DLI students at Brandywine, Cumming, and Kelly Mill are currently in 4th grade and will transition to DeSana, Otwell, and Hendricks in 2024-2025. The first cohort of DLI students from Mashburn is currently in 2nd grade and will transition to Lakeside in 2026-2027. Middle school principals have begun meeting regularly this year to learn more about DLI, engage in a book study, visit current middle school DLI programs in other counties, and decide on the Forsyth DLI middle school model. Impressions from DLI at Brandywine Elementary First Grade DLI at Brandywine has a mission to lead with a kind heart! Each month we use ARK to come up with ideas to help our school, community, and each other. In September, we donated snacks to kids who do not have them at snack time here at our school. In October, we focused on being happy and making others smile. We collected items and at the end of the month we were able to make 50 happy bags filled with little things that could make someone else smile on their hard days. This current month, November, we made placemats for firefighters to use during the holidays when they have to work and be away from their family. This will remind them that we are thinking of them and grateful for their service in our community. Impressions from DLI at Kelly Mill Elementary Sra.
