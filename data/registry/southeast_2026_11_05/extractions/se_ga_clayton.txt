Students, regardless of native language proficiency, are provided with ample exposure to languages, allowing them to achieve academic excellence while becoming bilingual and bi-literate. The program also emphasizes cultural awareness and sensitivity. Middle School - The Spanish Dual Language program offers a continuation for students who have been enrolled in Kilpatrick Dual Language Program and Unidos Dual Language School, or a similar Spanish Dual Language program at the elementary level or who have a high level of Spanish language proficiency. Highly motivated students are engaged in a challenging academic environment.
