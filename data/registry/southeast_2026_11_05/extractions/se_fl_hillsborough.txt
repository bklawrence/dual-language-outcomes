This program adheres to high expectations and state standards. Program Goals - To develop Bilingual, biliterate and bicultural students High levels of academic achievement An appreciation and understanding of diverse cultures. Research Findings Students who acquire advanced levels of proficiency in second languages often experience certain cognitive and linguistic advantages when compared to monolingual students. (Cummins,1981;Lambert, Genesee, Halolbow & Chatrand 1993) Students who are exposed to a second language at an early age have a better chance to learn and be more successful in the acquisition of a second language (Cognitive Neuroscientist Laura Ann Petitto, 2004) There is increasing evidence that phonological awareness transfers across languages. (e.g. Durgunoglu & Verhoeven, 1998; Geva, 2000) Instructional Program Design 50/50 model - students will receive 50% of their instruction in Spanish and 50% in English English-dominant and Spanish-dominant students are integrated for instruction throughout the day Skills are taught in one language and reinforced in the other language through thematic teaching and cross-linguistic development Cognitive Benefits Increases: Intellectual growth Creativity in thinking and listening Higher order reasoning skills Socio-Cultural Benefits Strengthens: Effective communication in both English and Spanish Literacy in both languages Appreciation for cultural diversity Pride in own culture Skills necessary in a global economy College and career readiness Schools Bellamy Elementary VPK-5Cannella Elementary VPK-4Crestwood Elementary VPK-5Dawson Elementary K - 4Deer Park Elementary K-5Reddick Elementary K-5Woodbridge Elementary K-4 Davidsen 6Pierce Middle 6-8Shields Middle 6-8Smith Middle 6-8 Dual Language Program Screenings for Teachers View Flyer Dual Language in the News World Language Camp immerses students in three languages Campamento de verano anima a estudiantes de Hillsborough a aprender 3 idiomas – Telemundo Tampa (49) Hillsborough Schools introduces new summer camp Sorprenden a estudiantes del programa Dual Language de Hillsborough con una parranda navideña (telemundo49.com) Some Tampa Bay schools teach in English and Spanish.
