Dual Language Immersion Spanish & German Dual Language Immersion Classrooms in Dalton, Georgia! Dalton Public Schools offer dual language immersion classrooms at two elementary schools. In these DLI classrooms, students spend part of their day learning in English and the other half learning in another language. Students learn their regular academic content in both English and their target language. Westwood Elementary offers Spanish as the target language and Brookwood Elementary offers German. Students who enroll in a dual-language immersion program in Dalton Public Schools will be provided the foundations for bilingualism and biliteracy. Students are equipped with the necessary skills to achieve the level of high levels of academic, social and language success. German Immersion Brookwood SchoolKindergarten - Fifth Grade Hammond Creek Middle SchoolSixth & Seventh Grade Spanish Immersion Westwood SchoolPre-K - Fourth Grade How Do I Enroll in a Dual Language Immersion Program? If you are interested in enrolling your pre-k student in a dual language immersion program, please contact our enrollment center for more information at 706-876-4000, then select option 2 for enrollment. While it is preferable for students to join the dual language immersion program in pre-kindergarten, students are able to join in the later grades. If as student is native in the second language, they can join the program at any time. If they are not fluent in the second language, they can join in the second grade at the latest.
