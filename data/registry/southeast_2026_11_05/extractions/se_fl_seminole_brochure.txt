WHAT IS A DUAL LANGUAGE PROGRAM? • Dual Language is a form of education in which students are taught literacy, Science and Math content in two languages. In Seminole County, the two languages are English and Spanish. • Dual Language education is an effective approach to developing language proficiency and literacy in English and Spanish. The program develops student proficiency in four domains: listening, speaking, reading, and writing in both languages. GOALS Students will develop high levels of language proficiency in English and Spanish as well as high levels of academic achievement and literacy in both languages. OBJECTIVES • Students will develop and attain multiculturalism awareness. • Students will become bilingual and biliterate. • Students will achieve academic excellence in two languages. • Students will meet the Florida State Spanish World Language Standards. • Students will meet the standards for foreign language learning in the 21st century: Community, Culture, Connections, Comparison DID YOU KNOW? COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT Children who develop two or more languages confer significant benefits such as: • Development of cognitive flexibility • Solving logic problems and multi-tasking effectively EARLY LANGUAGE LITERACY Children who develop two or more languages also develop resilience in: • Transferring knowledge about literacy content onto their second language • Learning additional languages Reference: www.wida.us DUAL LANGUAGE SCHOOLS (K-5) ADMISSION CRITERIA If you are interested in enrolling your child in a Dual Language school, please contact your neighborhood school.
