Dual Language Immersion for English Learners (ELs) Dual Language Immersion for ELs in Fulton County Schools Dual language education programs provide academic instruction in two languages, fostering both social and academic development in an environment that respects and values the language and culture of all students. Dual Language Immersion (DLI) programs set high standards for academic achievement in both Spanish and English. This DLI program follows an ESOL-approved model recognized by the Department of Education for English Learners (ELs).
