Dual Language What is Spanish Immersion? Spanish Immersion is an exciting program where students are taught by a bilingual teacher for the majority of the school day in the Spanish Language, hence the term immersion. Students are immersed in the Spanish Language. Spanish Immersion is also known as Dual Language Immersion (DLI) because students are taught in Spanish and English with English instruction beginning in 2nd grade. Students learn to understand, read, and write in Spanish as well as English. The Program begins in kindergarten or first grade and continues through middle school. Student Testimonials Dual Language/Immersion classes are an exciting option for parents who want their children to have the advantage of a rigorous academic program while also learning a second language. The language instituted in all of our dual language/immersion classes is Spanish. Across our nation and in North Carolina, language immersion programs have been thoroughly studied and present a comprehensive body of research that demonstrates significant benefits for our students. All Spanish Immersion students are taught in the same standards, concepts and skills as other elementary students. The pacing and sequencing will vary during the K-2 years. Students learn from research-based materials that are specifically tailored to the Spanish language as well as English resources beginning in 2nd grade. You want your child to have an edge in tomorrow's global economy. You want your child to experience productive challenges in a diverse classroom setting. You are willing to commit to 3 years in the program to see long-term benefits. Students will become bilingual and are proficient in two languages. Students in DLI programs tend to score better than non-DLI peers on standardized tests. Immersion students are comfortable interacting with students of other backgrounds due to exposure to other cultures. DLI students have a positive sense of self. DLI students will have more career advantages and opportunities. All students entering Kindergarten or First Grade Students (grades 2 - 4) whose first language is Spanish and wish to transfer into a DLI class.
