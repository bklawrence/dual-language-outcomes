Welcome Congratulations, Alfred I. duPont Middle SchoolEmbassy of Spain Colegio del Año Contest Winners2021 The Dual Language Program is designed to prepare students to succeed in a world that is culturally diverse and in a global economy through academic achievement and proficiency in a second language. Program Overview DCPS offers the 50/50 Two-Way Immersion Dual Language Program in elementary and middle school, while the high school model follows a 70/30 Two-Way Immersion model. Two-way bilingual immersion programs are additive programs in that a second language is acquired while maintain the first language of the students. The program consists of classes as close to 50% Native English speakers and 50% of Native Spanish speakers as possible. Both groups of student serve in the role of language model and language learner at different times of the day. English language learners and Spanish language learners spend 50% of their classroom time receiving academic instruction in each language. Students learn mathematics, science, social studies and language arts in Spanish and English. Dual Language Schools The Dual Language Program is a strand within the school. All schools provide a general education program for neighboorhood students.
