All students in South Carolina schools are instructed according to the same set of educational standards, also referred to as academic or learning standards. These standards establish specific learning objectives and provide clear guidance for teachers, students, parents, and the broader community. South Carolina’s educational standards comprehensively delineate the knowledge and skills students are expected to attain at each grade level and in every subject area to promote their academic success. SC Standards and Learning ​ What is the difference between standards and curriculum? Standards outline the knowledge and skills students should acquire, while curriculum refers to the instructional resources and the planned sequence teachers use to help students learn those standards.​ Who is a global citizen?
