Two-Way Dual-Language Immersion Program The Dual Language Immersion Program for rising 5K students is based at Riverbank Elementary. If you are interested in this learning opportunity for your child, please fill out an application. This program allows students to be immersed in English for 50% of the school day and Spanish for 50% of the school day. This program is for both native English speakers and native Spanish speakers. Reach out to Denise Quickel, Coordinator of Multilingual Services, or Noah Price, Assistant Principal at Riverbank, for more information.
