What is the Retake Policy? Our Spanish Partial Immersion students take a variety of classes in their academic day. Blythe Spanish Partial Immersion Students are automatically enrolled in this program unless a parent or guardian notifies us. 6th graders in this program take Spanish Language Arts for an extended period of time. 7th and 8th graders take HONORS Spanish Language Arts (for Honors Elective High School Credit) and Social Studies in Spanish. Students read Spanish novels in 6th, 7th, and 8th grade Spanish Language Arts and study grammar. What if my son/daughter is zoned for Hughes and the Spanish Partial Immersion Program seems too rigorous for him/her? We do offer another pathway for zoned students that attended the Blythe Spanish Partial Immersion Program and do not wish to take Spanish Language Arts and Social Studies in Spanish. If you wish for your zoned student to take any of the classes listed below and not be involved in the Spanish Partial Immersion Program at Hughes - Please email Mrs. Marsha Patry, Spanish Department Chair, mpatry@greenville.k12.sc.us Hughes Academy Spanish Program for (non-immersion) Students We encourage all of our Spanish Partial Immersion students to continue their studies at home, on the weekend and during the summmer.
