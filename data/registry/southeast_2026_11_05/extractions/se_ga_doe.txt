World Languages education in Georgia includes the study of modern languages, classical languages, American Sign Language, and Dual Language Immersion. High school students can complete a World Languages Pathway. Additionally, high school seniors may earn both the Georgia Seal of Biliteracy and the International Skills Diploma Seal. The student’s course history in one world language includes:3 distinct high school courses in the same world language, or2 distinct high school courses in the same world language plus a third course reflecting an Advanced Placement (AP) language course, an International Baccalaureate (IB) course, or a post-secondary course. World Languages Pathway Guidance at a GlanceGaDOE offers two recognitions for high school seniors: the International Skills Diploma Seal and the Georgia Seal of Biliteracy. The seals are awarded to students who demonstrate proficiency in English and another language as well as those who are engaged in intercultural communication and global studies. Both seals aim to promote language skills and cultural understanding among students, preparing them for continued education and successful careers.
