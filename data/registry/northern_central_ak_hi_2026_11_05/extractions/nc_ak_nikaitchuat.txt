Mission To instill the knowledge of Iñupiaq identity, dignity, and respect while cultivating a love of lifelong learning. Through language, culture, and traditional teachings, the Native Village of Kotzebue is investing in the future of our children and strengthening the foundation of our community for generations to come. About the Program Nikaitchuat Iḷisaġviat is the Native Village of Kotzebue’s Iñupiaq language immersion and cultural education program. The program was established to preserve, strengthen, and revitalize the Iñupiaq language, culture, and traditional ways of knowing for future generations. Students learn through language immersion, storytelling, cultural instruction, subsistence activities, traditional knowledge, and hands-on experiences that connect them to the land, sea, and community. Guided by Iñupiaq values and supported by Elders, families, and educators, students develop a strong sense of identity, respect, responsibility, and belonging. Our goal is to help children build a strong cultural foundation while preparing them for lifelong success in school, their careers, and their roles as future leaders within our communities. By strengthening language and cultural knowledge at an early age, we help ensure that future generations continue to carry forward the traditions, values, and wisdom of our ancestors. The Native Village of Kotzebue continues to expand opportunities for students to continue their language and cultural education beyond the preschool years, creating pathways for future generations of fluent Iñupiaq speakers and cultural leaders. Johnson O’Malley (JOM) Program Nikaitchuat Iḷisaġviat is supported in part through the Johnson O’Malley (JOM) Program administered by the Bureau of Indian Education (BIE). The JOM Program was established to address the unique educational and cultural needs of American Indian and Alaska Native students through supplemental educational services and opportunities. Through Johnson O’Malley funding, the Native Village of Kotzebue is able to support Iñupiaq language instruction, cultural enrichment activities, educational materials, family engagement, student activities, and other programs that strengthen both academic achievement and cultural identity. These resources help ensure that students receive educational experiences that reflect their heritage while supporting their success in school and throughout life. Looking Forward The Native Village of Kotzebue remains committed to preserving and revitalizing the Iñupiaq language and culture through education. As the program continues to grow and expand, our focus remains on providing students with meaningful learning experiences rooted in Iñupiaq values, language, culture, and traditional knowledge. By investing in our children today, we are strengthening our community, preserving our identity, and building a brighter future for generations to come. “Our language carries our knowledge, our history, and our identity.
