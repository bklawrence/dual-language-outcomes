SIOUX FALLS — One of the more popular and unique programs in the Sioux Falls school district is going to expand in the fall of 2025 to two more city middle schools.The School Board, which needed to make a decision this summer on the Spanish Immersion Program for planning purposes, has decided to add Ben Reifel and George McGovern middle schools to the program currently offered only at Edison Middle School. ADVERTISEMENT The immersion program provides students an opportunity to be bilingual in a changing world where it can be beneficial to job and communication skills.“It opens many opportunities,” Assistant Superintendent Teresa Boysen told the board at its Monday meeting when the decision was made. Teresa Boysen, assistant superintendent for the Sioux Falls School District. In all, about 1,750 of the district’s 24,000 students have experienced one of two options of the programs offered.One of the options is the one-way immersion program offered at Sonia Sotomayor Elementary School in the central part of the city near Sanford Medical Center. Currently, there are 625 students enrolled in that program where students spend 80 to 90% of their day in Spanish and start English literacy in third grade.The two-way immersion program, the other option, is offered at Rosa Parks and Hayward elementary schools on opposite sides of the city where students spend 50 percent of their day in Spanish and 50 percent in English allowing for simultaneous literacy instruction in both languages. There are about 500 students at the two schools currently in that option. Jun 13, 2024 · By Barry Amundson There are an additional 625 students who continue their immersion experience in grades six through 12.Boysen told the board about 100 more fifth graders in 2025 will move into sixth grade at the middle schools and Edison can’t accommodate the additional students. ADVERTISEMENT She pointed out that in 2019 the district began offering the two-way immersion program at Rosa Parks and Hayward with those students now set to advance into the middle school program The one-way immersion program dates back to 2008 at Sonia Sotomayor.She said there was a lot of parental input about the expansion before the plan was approved.‘“We had good feedback,” Boysen said, including a lot of phone calls and comments on the district’s Let’s Talk website.There were also listening sessions held with parents and residents by the administration.With the additional students in the program, the decision means that all students won’t have to go to Edison but rather can also go to one-way or two-way programs that will be offered at George McGovern on the west side of the city and Ben Reifel on the east side.There will be a cost to the expansion as teachers will have to be hired at both of the middle schools with the same programming offered at all three middle schools.Another benefit will be that Sonia Sotomayor one-way immersion students can also attend middle school programs closer to their homes if space is available, Boysen said.
