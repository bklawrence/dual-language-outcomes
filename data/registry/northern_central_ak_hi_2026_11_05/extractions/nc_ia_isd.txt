How we succeed.Every day.Through passionate, specialized staff, small successes within students occur every day, becoming large accomplishments every year. We invite you to explore our website and learn how our students thrive in a language-based academic and social environment.Iowa School for the Deaf is not just a ‘special’ school. It is an extraordinary language and learning environment for children who sign, talk or use both communication methods. We welcome children who have cochlear implants, hearing aids or who use no assistive hearing devices. We value our students’ strengths and design individual learning goals centered around each student’s unique needs.Upcoming ISD EventsThere are no events to display.
