K-12 EDUCATION SAVINGS ACCOUNTS (ESAs) The Students First Act makes state funding available to support the success of every K-12 student in Iowa. ESAs provide funds for eligible families from the State of Iowa. All Pella Christian K-12 students are eligible, regardless of income, grade or transfer status. The amount allocated for the 26/27 school year is $8,148 per student. The amount for the following school year will be announced in the spring by the State of Iowa. The ESA application opens each spring. Applications are due on June 30, 2026 for the 2026-2027 school year. Applications are valid for one year; families must apply annually to remain eligible for the program. Log in or create an Odyssey/ESA Account here to apply for an Iowa Education Savings Account. K-12 IOWA 529 ACCOUNTS Iowa 529 accounts can be used to fund K-12 tuition paid to an accredited State of Iowa school. A 529 Plan is an after-tax investment account that grows tax free if distributions are used for qualified educational expenses. Pella Christian Schools are accredited State of Iowa schools and our K-12 tuition is a qualified 529 account expense. The State of Iowa guidelines state that the maximum 529 distribution per K-12 student is $20,000 per calendar year and payment can only be applied towards tuition. If you participate in the Iowa 529 educational savings plan, please have qualified distributions made and sent directly to Pella Christian Schools. Visit the Iowa 529 website here: www.isave529.com EE- 12 TUITION REDUCTION INCENTIVE PROGRAM (T.R.I.P.) The Tuition Reduction Incentive Program (TRIP) is a program in which you purchase and use gift cards instead of cash or credit cards at places you normally shop, locally and nationally. The businesses involved gives a percentage of that purchase back to the school as a reduction in your tuition the following year. This program is run by Pella Christian Grade School. Contact the PCGS office at trip@pceagles.org or 641-628-2414 for enrollment instructions. Who can participate? A family with children currently enrolled at PCGS or PCHS A family who plans to have children at PCGS or PCHS someday A grandparent who wants to help their children reduce their tuition payment A supporter PCGS or PCHS who wants to help the General Tuition Fund to lower tuition for everyone Anyone may participate in the TRIP program. How often do I have to use it?As frequently or infrequently as you choose. Of course, the more you use it, the more beneficial it is to you or the school. K-12 IOWA TUITION & TEXTBOOK CREDIT Families can claim a credit of up to $500 for tuition and textbook payments on their State of Iowa income tax return. Use your tuition statement as proof of payment. Please ask your accountant for more information on your specific tax situation and eligibility. Families who are in need of additional assistance to pay their tuition obligation are eligible to apply for assistance through this fund. Application for these funds opens March 1. The priority deadline is April 30 with the awards being made during the summer. However, families are welcome to apply for financial support at anytime throughout the year as we are aware of circumstances that can arise at anytime that might create a financial need. How do we apply? Application for tuition assistance is made through FACTS Grant & Aid Assessment. Go to FACTS APPLICATION FOR TAP to fill out the application and attach all the supporting documentation needed. There is no fee to apply. Priority deadline is April 30. For questions about or assistance with this form, please contact the school office. What is the amount of the scholarship? The PC finance committee reviews the application and financial information and makes the determination of the award. Families are welcome to visit with a finance committee member to discuss their unique financial situation. K-12 SCHOOL TUITION ORGANIZATIONS (STO) SCHOLARSHIPS Pella Christian K-12 students are eligible to apply for needs-based scholarships from Legacy of Grace School Tuition Organization. Families may be eligible for assistance through this state-funded tax credit program if their income as shown on line 9 of their IRS Form 1040 for the 2025 tax year is not more than four times the poverty rate (based on the size of their family).
