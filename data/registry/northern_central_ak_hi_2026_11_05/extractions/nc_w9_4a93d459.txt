ROCHESTER — Riverside Elementary is getting ready to host more students who are not just learning Spanish, but learning other subjects in Spanish through Immersion.That’s because Rochester Public Schools is in the process of moving its middle school Spanish immersion program into the Riverside building, which already hosts the district's elementary Spanish immersion program. The Rochester School Board heard a presentation on the effort on Tuesday, March 18. ADVERTISEMENT As a result, Riverside will continue to function as a traditional elementary school, but it will also serve as a K-8 school for the district's spanish immersion program — with both the elementary and middle school components under the same roof for the first time. The middle school Spanish immersion program, which is only a handful of years old, is currently housed out of Willow Creek Middle School.Starting in 2025-26, current fifth graders in the immersion program will remain at Riverside, beginning the first cohort of middle school students at Riverside as sixth graders. Meanwhile, sixth- and seventh-grade immersion students currently at Willow Creek will finish their time there until moving on to high school. During that process, there will be middle school students in the Spanish immersion program at both buildings until all the immersion students at Willow Creek move on to high school. In addition to transitioning the middle school students in the immersion program to Riverside, district leaders are also continuing to hone the curriculum of the program.There are 276 students projected to be in the immersion program for 2025-26. Of that total, 214 will be in elementary school, and 59 will be in middle school.The elementary school Spanish immersion program used to be housed out of Gage Elementary in northwest Rochester. It moved to Riverside Elementary as part of the district’s attendance area redesign.
