Spanish Immersion Park Spanish Immersion School (PSI) is one of St. Louis Park Public Schools' four elementary schools. Spanish Immersion is a great option for multilingual students and English Learners. At PSI, students are immersed in the Spanish language while they learn classroom content. St. Louis Park Public Schools is working to increase the diversity of the student body at PSI by simplifying the admissions process and shifting enrollment priorities. While students at PSI are primarily instructed in Spanish, PSI is an excellent option for multilingual students. PSI offers English classes and programming to support students whose home language is not English. Visit the District's Enrollment Page Visit the Park Spanish Immersion School Website Important Enrollment Information Per the admissions policy, PSI has certain number of kindergarten spots available each year, with a limited number of those seats available for open enrollment. If more applications are received than spots available, the district conducts a lottery to determine enrollment per School Board Policy 545. The updated policy gives priority to siblings of currently enrolled students, students whose applications are related to an approved integration and achievement plan, children of the school district’s staff, and students who are native Spanish speakers. Additionally, the district gives priority to St. Louis Park residents. Siblings of students already attending PSI are not automatically enrolled; sibling preference for residents will be considered only if your family meets criteria outlined in the PSI Admissions Policy and if the PSI Lottery Request Form is received by stated deadlines.
