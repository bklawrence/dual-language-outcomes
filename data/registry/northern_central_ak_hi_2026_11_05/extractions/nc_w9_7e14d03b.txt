Hmong International Academy (HIA) is a Hmong culturally specific school with grades Pre K-Grade 5. Students have daily opportunities to engage in activities that enhance their understanding of the Hmong culture and language. At HIA, we have a 50% One-Way Dual Language Program that offers Grade Kindergarten through 5th grade 120 minutes of Hmong literacy per day. We are a proud Northside school!
