Immersion learning means students are taught core subjects in the target language, either Mandarin or Spanish, which fosters natural bilingualism. Research consistently shows that learning a second language early boosts cognitive development, enhances academic performance, and strengthens future opportunities—in high school, college, and careers. Through the International Baccalaureate (IB) Primary Years Program (PYP) and Middle Years Program (MYP), our mission goes beyond classroom education. We strive to nurture curious, compassionate, and globally aware individuals who are prepared to lead with character, insight, and responsibility. With a foundation in life skills and leadership development, our students are empowered to create meaningful change and foster intercultural understanding in an increasingly connected world. Choosing a Spanish immersion program for elementary students offers a wealth of lifelong advantages. These programs allow children to develop fluency in a second language during their prime language-learning years, making bilingualism feel natural and intuitive. Beyond linguistic skills, students gain cognitive benefits such as improved memory, problem-solving abilities, and mental flexibility. Immersion also fosters cultural awareness and global perspective, helping kids appreciate diversity from a young age. Plus, early exposure to another language can open doors academically and professionally in the future. Choosing a Mandarin immersion program for elementary students unlocks unique cognitive and cultural benefits. Mandarin is one of the most spoken languages in the world, and learning it early builds impressive linguistic skills and sharpens mental agility, especially because of its distinct tones and characters. Kids in these programs tend to develop strong memory, pattern recognition, and even increased creativity. Beyond brainpower, Mandarin immersion introduces children to the rich history and traditions of Chinese culture, fostering openness and global citizenship. Plus, fluency in Mandarin can be a powerful asset in future international careers. Language immersion is an educational approach where learners are immersed in a target language. The primary method of instruction is through the target language and cultural immersion. It is a non-negotiable that classroom teachers instruct in the core language everyday. The expectation is that by eighth grade, students will graduate bilingual and bi-literate in the areas of reading, writing, and verbal communication. Students do not remain in one location for more than 20 minutes, regularly transitioning between the group rug, desks, chairs, and learning centers. A range of flexible seating options is used to support comfort and engagement. Learning centers are incorporated into the daily schedule to reinforce skills, explore unit concepts, and encourage social interaction. Our school is proud to offer the International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme (PYP), emphasizing an integrated, inquiry-based curriculum. Through transdisciplinary Units of Inquiry, we intentionally create meaningful connections across subject areas. Literacy instruction often merges with science and social studies to deepen understanding and engagement. Phonics in the target language is taught through immersive experiences with letters and sounds using music, gestures, and vocabulary. Writing is interwoven throughout both phonics and reading instruction. In phonics, students learn correct letter formation and apply sound-letter correspondence to begin writing words. During reading, students are encouraged to express themselves by responding to literature or developing their own stories through illustrations and emerging written language. Nuestra escuela se enorgullece de ofrecer el Programa de la Escuela Primaria (PEP) del Bachillerato Internacional, que promueve un currículo integrado basado en la indagación. A través de las Unidades de Indagación transdisciplinarias, buscamos establecer conexiones significativas entre las distintas áreas del conocimiento. La instrucción en lectura y escritura a menudo se combina con temas de ciencias y estudios sociales para enriquecer la comprensión y el compromiso de los estudiantes. La enseñanza de fonética en el idioma meta se realiza mediante experiencias inmersivas con letras y sonidos, empleando música, gestos y vocabulario. La escritura está integrada en ambas áreas, fonética y lectura. Durante las actividades de fonética, los estudiantes aprenden a formar las letras correctamente y utilizan la correspondencia sonido-letra para comenzar a escribir palabras. En las sesiones de lectura, se les anima a expresarse a través de respuestas literarias o creando sus propias historias mediante ilustraciones y lenguaje escrito emergente. Instruction occurs in both large and small group settings. A whole group setting is used to develop the concepts of the Units of Inquiry and to introduce phonics and grade level math concepts to the entire group. Teachers also work with small groups based on ability to fill in gaps or challenge and extend students’ learning. La instrucción ocurre en entornos de grupos grandes y pequeños. Overall, we believe that homework can be used for reinforcing skills, but we do not rely on homework for instruction. As students get older, the homework may increase a bit, but again, it is not meant to replace learning that takes place in school. Reading should be part of every child’s at-home routine. Each child should read or be read to each night for at least 20 minutes. The reading skills they develop in their home language will apply to any language they are learning. The child’s teacher may also send classwork home for the student to complete, if they did not complete it during class time, as well as additional materials, such as flashcards, for the child to practice at home. Please make sure to communicate with your child’s teacher to learn how you can best support your child’s learning at home. The first 30 minutes of each school day are intentionally reserved for our “Morning Meeting,” a time focused on building social-emotional skills, including conflict resolution. Teachers may lead general lessons on social skills or use this time to address specific situations emerging in the classroom. By explicitly teaching these strategies, we equip students to navigate strong emotions and make positive choices. We treat behavioral challenges as learning opportunities, recognizing that while choices carry consequences, they also open the door for growth. Our team includes two full-time school counselors who, in collaboration with administration, provide support to both teachers and families. Students may work with counselors individually or in small groups, and personalized plans are developed when needed. Additionally, counselors offer access to community resources to further assist families.
