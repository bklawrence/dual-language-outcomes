Ríos Spanish Dual Language Immersion Program The goal of Ríos Spanish Immersion program is to provide educational experiences that support bilingualism and biliteracy in Spanish and English, as well as reinforce high academic standards and increase socio-cultural competency. The program begins in kindergarten and is continued through Grade 12. There are Ríos classrooms at the following school locations: Kindergarten at Goodview School Grades 1-4 at Washington-Kosciusko Elementary Grades 5-8 at Winona Middle School Grades 9-12 at Winona Senior High School It is the only dual language immersion program in Winona County. All Winona Area Public Schools students will be offered the same rigorous core curriculum in reading, writing, math, science, and social studies. Immersion students will study the same core curriculum and Minnesota academic standards which connect subject areas. Ríos students will also have the opportunity to graduate with a Minnesota State Seal of Biliteracy, which earns credits in the Minnesota State University system. Instructions on how to apply are posted at winonaschools.org/rios and sent to families through the WAPS messaging system. At the end of the application window, district leadership determines if there is enough interest in the program to add an additional section (classroom) of kindergarten for the following year. If a second kindergarten section is added, additional applicants will be accepted on a first-come, first-serve basis. If there is only one section offered, and there are more applicants than spots, a lottery will be held. Because the circumstances surrounding each application year may differ, please reach out to the building principal or fill out the question form if you need additional information. Expanded employment opportunities for bilingual students — From the Minnesota Department of Education Grades K-4 | Goodview School & W-K Elementary Grades 5-8 | Winona Middle School Grades 9-12 | Winona Senior High School Students in Grades K-2 receive approximately 70% of their daily instruction in Spanish. A typical day will include Spanish Language Arts, Math, Science and Social Studies lessons in Spanish. Students will also have the following subjects taught in English: Music, Art, Physical Education, Counseling, Library/Media and a 20-30 minute English Language Arts lesson daily. In Grades 3-4 students will receive 60% of their daily instruction in Spanish and more English Language Arts minutes are added to the day. In Grade 5, students will receive about 150 minutes of instruction (44% of the day) in Spanish in Spanish Language Arts, Science and Social Studies. As students advance through middle school and take more electives, there will be more instruction delivered in English than Spanish. In Grade 6, students will take courses in Spanish Language Arts and Math. In Grades 7-8, students will take only a Spanish Language Arts course as they begin to explore a wide variety of electives offered in English. Students in Grades 9-10 are offered a Spanish Language Arts course, while students in Grade 11-12 can take AP Spanish. A dual language immersion elective in Spanish Language Media & Film will be offered for Grades 10-12. As of the 2023-24 school year, the first cohort of Ríos students is in Grade 10, so the high school offerings will continue to evolve as more students enter high school. Students can also earn college credit through demonstrating proficiency on the Seal of Biliteracy assessment. Students are encouraged to enroll in the Service Learning class, and they will receive a community placement where their Spanish skills can be used in an authentic environment. The Ríos Dual Language Spanish Immersion program started in the 2014-15 school year and has continued to evolve since Day 1. The journey began with a vision to create a community of bilingual champions, and over the years, we’ve refined our approach to ensure a dynamic, effective and sustainable learning experience. We are committed to hearing from our families about what’s working well and what can be improved to make a program that continues to flourish. Studies have shown that native Spanish speakers enrolled in dual language programs tend to achieve academic English proficiency at a faster rate and have higher academic achievement rates compared to their peers of similar language backgrounds.
