Language Immersion A World of Possibilities Begins in Kindergarten Give your child the opportunity to learn, grow and connect in two languages. Minnetonka’s Chinese and Spanish Language Immersion programs combine strong academics with daily opportunities to develop language skills, build friendships and discover new perspectives. Beginning in kindergarten and continuing through high school, students build a foundation for communicating across cultures and pursuing opportunities close to home and around the world. Along the way, they experience the caring teachers, engaging classrooms and rich learning opportunities that make Minnetonka Schools a special place to grow. Language Immersion at Minnetonka Children learn a language by using it. In Minnetonka’s immersion classrooms, Chinese or Spanish is the language of everyday learning. Students explore math concepts, investigate science, read stories and share ideas while building the skills to understand and communicate in another language. Teachers make learning engaging and understandable through visuals, movement, songs, demonstrations and familiar routines. With daily practice and encouragement, students develop confidence in speaking, listening, reading and writing as they grow academically. Chinese Immersion Discover the possibilities of learning Mandarin Chinese. Through classroom learning and cultural experiences, students develop communication skills and explore the traditions, stories and perspectives of Chinese-speaking communities. Spanish Immersion begins in kindergarten at: Clear Springs Elementary Deephaven Elementary Groveland Elementary Minnewashta Elementary Explore Spanish Immersion A Pathway from Kindergarten Through Graduation Your child’s first words in a new language are just the beginning. Minnetonka provides a pathway that helps students deepen their skills and put their learning to use throughout their academic journey. Elementary school: Build a foundation in language and academics through engaging, everyday learning. Middle school: Strengthen communication, reading and writing skills through continued immersion coursework. High school: Pursue advanced language and literature courses, with opportunities to earn recognition for bilingual proficiency, including Minnesota’s Bilingual Seal and the International Baccalaureate Bilingual Diploma.
