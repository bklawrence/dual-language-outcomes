Spanish Immersion The mission of the Sioux Falls School District Spanish Immersion Program is to successfully educate each student while developing biliteracy and fostering global competency in an ever-changing world. The High School Spanish Immersion program is the culmination of the Spanish Immersion program that begins in elementary and progresses to Edison Middle School. Located at Lincoln High School, the High School Spanish Immersion program continues the process of ensuring that students are bi-literate in Spanish and English. Students take at least one Spanish Immersion course each year. Students may attend a different Sioux Falls high school and travel to Lincoln High School for Spanish Immersion courses as their schedule allows. Immersion Advisory Group A collaborative dialogue involving multiple stakeholders—such as parents, teachers, administrators, students, and community members—aimed at gathering diverse perspectives to enhance the Sioux Falls School District (SFSD) Spanish Immersion program. This discussion will focus on identifying strengths, addressing challenges, and exploring innovative solutions to improve the program’s curriculum, instructional methods, student support services, cultural integration, and long-term sustainability. By fostering open communication, stakeholders can work together to ensure the program meets the needs of students, aligns with educational goals, and promotes bilingualism and cultural competence effectively.
