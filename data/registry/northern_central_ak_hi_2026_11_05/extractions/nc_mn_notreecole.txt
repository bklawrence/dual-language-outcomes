Notre École ☼ Golden Valley, MN Free, STEAM-Focused, French Immersion Education At Notre École, we equip students (Grades K–5) with a strong foundation for success both in and out of the classroom through hands-on learning in the sciences and a rich immersion in the French language and culture. ABOUT US > Benefits of Notre École Small Class Sizes Notre École is dedicated to keeping our classes small to ensure individualized attention that helps our students learn, participate, and get support. STEAM Focused An educational approach that uses Science, Technology, Engineering, the Arts, and Mathematics to guide student inquiry, critical thinking, and collaboration, encouraging experiential learning and creative problem-solving.
