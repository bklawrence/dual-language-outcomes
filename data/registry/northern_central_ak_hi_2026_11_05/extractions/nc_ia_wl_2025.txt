Fill Out Your 2025-26 Kindergarten Dual Language Application Here Before April 4th!Kara KruseFebruary 12, 2025Please help us spread the word regarding the Kindergarten Dual Language Application for 2025-2026 incoming Kindergarten families! We ask that all families who are interested submit their forms by April 4, 2025, to ensure that all applicants are considered.Filling out the form linked below allows us to identify students registering for Kindergarten whose parents are interested in the Dual Language program. The Dual Language program model provides 50% of the instructional time in English and 50% of the instructional time in Spanish. Please note that submission of the Dual Language Program Application does not guarantee placement in the program.Click the link below to fill out the application: Dual Language Program Interest Form 2025-2026This form is also posted on our website home page:https://www.wl.k12.ia.us/ look for the button on the home page that says Dual Language Kindergarten App. If you know someone who does not have access to fill out the form electronically paper forms are available at the District Office, 1103 N. Elm St, West Liberty, IA, and must be turned back in at the District office once filled out.If you have any questions please contact Ms. Brenda Arthur Miller, Director of the Dual Language program via email: barthurmiller@wl.k12.ia.usPlease note that submitting this form does not register your child for Kindergarten, it only indicates your interest in the Dual Language program. Parents of new or returning students must follow the district's registration process for the 2025-2026 school year, see this link for registration information: https://www.wl.k12.ia.us/article/2024547
