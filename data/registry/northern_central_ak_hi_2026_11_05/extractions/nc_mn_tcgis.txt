Apply Today! ​​Innovative Education of the Whole Child through German Immersion. Twin Cities German Immersion School is a (free) K-8 public charter school. We are the largest German immersion school in North America, and are open to all Minnesota families. Students meet their learning targets using German, learning language through the content. Additional support is provided for students who don’t speak German yet, as well as English-language support for students who speak a language other than English at home.
