FRENCH IMMERSIO IMMERSION FRENCH N 01 WHO WE ARE WHO WE ARE French Immersion is an Anchorage School District alternative program for students in grades K-6. The program will expand each year until it includes students from Kindergarten through 12th grade. 02 HOW HOW IT IT WORKS WORKS Elementary School: Students have two teachers. They spend ½ day learning in English and ½ day learning in French. Core subjects are divided: English: Mathematics & English Language Arts French: Social Studies, French Language Arts, & Science Middle School: Students receive French instruction in 2 out of 7 classes (Social Studies & French LOCATIONS Language Arts). LOCATIONS 03 Elementary: O’Malley Elementary alongside the O’Malley neighborhood program (K-5) Middle School: Goldenview Middle School (6-7) WHO WHO CAN CAN APPLY High School: South High School starting in 2028 04 APPLY Students are selected through ASD’s lottery program. Kindergarten through first semester of 1st Grade: open to all students, no French language proficiency required. Second semester of 1st Grade and later: students are evaluated to ensure their French skills support success. Placement is dependent on available space. 05 HOW HOW TO TO APPLY APPLY Applications are submitted through the ASD lottery system which opens February 1, 2026 for fall 2026 enrollment.
