The JSON files are reviewed build inputs. CSVs are generated by the offline build and retained for browsing; do not hand-edit generated CSVs. Multiple programme rows at one school commonly mean different language strands. School/campus keys are provisional, not NCES IDs.

`sources.json` and [sources.csv](sources.csv) identify complete saved HTTP responses, source URLs, retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 hashes. The raw bytes are in `data/raw/northern_central_ak_hi_2026_11_05/`; metadata distinguishes stored bodies from text/image transcriptions. [retrieval_log.csv](retrieval_log.csv) also retains failed and unselected retrievals. HTTP 200 does not by itself mean substantive evidence; bot/challenge pages were not selected as primary sources.

`reviews.json` records bounded continuity assessments. `identity_decisions.json` records named renamings, moves and shared-site distinctions. `*_screening.json` preserves preschool, historical, out-of-state and unresolved discovery leads without automatically creating confirmed programmes. `extractions/index.json` tracks saved text and reconciled Hawaiʻi/Anchorage rosters for offline rebuilds. The FLA image has a separately labelled manual visual transcription; no numerical daily ratio was read from its full-core-immersion wording.

Academic Native-language immersion is retained. Funding, operation, language intensity and school-grade eligibility are separate fields. ASL-English concurrent literacy does not imply a numerical clock-time split. Future launches, reference-year funding candidates and secondary-only continuations must be filtered before calculating elementary coverage.

New proficiency collection remains deferred. Original earlier research and assessment pilots are preserved.
