# Texas allocation coverage after Pass 51

November 4, 2026. The denominator remains **1,610 provisional campuses** from 2023–24 TAPR reporting groups. This pass reviews La Joya, Austin, Aldine, Ysleta and Spring. It does not add campuses or establish a statewide DL census.

| Within-inventory measure | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Campuses with any allocation/model label | 848 (52.7%) | 848 (52.7%) |
| Campuses with at least one cited allocation/model | 823 (51.1%) | 848 (52.7%) |
| Campuses with a cited allocation and provisional language-policy link | 562 (34.9%) | 622 (38.6%) |
| Campuses without an allocation/model label | 762 | 762 |

**26 missing row citations were recovered**, leaving one uncited Govalle one-way record. Twenty-five campuses gain their first cited allocation; Govalle already has a cited two-way row. These counts include mixed source periods and policy/model labels that require further review. They are not counts of verified K schedules, tested-cohort exposure or confirmed 2025–26 operating campuses.

**83 unknown language evidence records gain support**: 27 Aldine, 31 Ysleta, 23 Spring and 2 Austin. Three have campus-document scope; 80 have district-policy scope. Four additional Aldine two-way records gain fresh policy corroboration. The total of 1,863 provisional records remains unchanged; 965 still have unverified language pairs.

The cleanup explicitly retains 27 Aldine pre-K proxies without calling them verified K allocation; flags 31 Ysleta model/minute discrepancies; and rejects 23 historical Spring citations for one-way K 50/50 while adding 23 separate current policy observations. All 970 original allocation IDs and labels remain, giving 993 observations across the same 848 campuses. No additional language-specific outcomes are created.

The shared allocation table now exposes these qualifications in `evidence_review_status` and `grade_support_status`. Use these fields before filtering for supported grade allocations. Citation presence or a policy-to-candidate link alone does not establish an exact timetable.
