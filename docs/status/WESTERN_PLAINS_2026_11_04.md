# Six-state structural first pass

Research and integration date: **November 4, 2026**. States: **Utah, Colorado,
New Mexico, Arizona, Nebraska and Kansas**. Reference school year: **2025–26**.
New proficiency, assessment/context, EL exit and SEDA collection remains deferred.

This pass integrates **650 provisional programme evidence groups at 568 school
units**, supported by **96 archived public sources**. **177 qualifying programme
groups at 174 schools** are confirmed for 2025–26 using the recorded direct or
user-authorized continuity method. Other records retain earlier/later listings,
plans, language-course continuations and unresolved content/funding/history.
These are working-set counts, not verified statewide totals or distinct enduring
programme counts. School identities have no new inferred NCES matches.

The shared tables now represent **15 states**. The other **35 states** still lack
an integrated programme working set, and the represented states have outstanding
collection and verification work. Earlier research inputs, the nine-state
milestone report and all 12 assessment/definition/link tables are preserved.

## Consequential source decisions

**Utah.** The USBE September 2026 PDF yields 280 school rows and 348 language
cells, including Russian. Its **2026–27** listings are not wholesale confirmation
of 2025–26. The roster also includes secondary language continuations; those do
not inherit elementary ratios or automatically qualify as academic immersion.
Many elementary models begin in **grade 1**, so they do not create kindergarten
allocations. Cache County's original 2025–26 form names Heritage/Spanish,
Cedar Ridge/Chinese, Providence/French and Sunrise/Portuguese. The later magnet
arrangement is separate: Spring Creek Elementary is not the earlier middle
school, and Mountain Crest explicitly has no DLI in 2025–26. South Davis Junior
High French operates in 2025–26, then closes/consolidates after that year; the
Lincoln Spanish closure proposal was rejected.

**Colorado.** The voluntary CDE list has 52 observations, including the exact
University Hill duplicate and the reviewed Pioneer alias. These resolve to one
school identity each. AXIS is assigned to its stated CSI authorizer, not Poudre
from the list's placement. Campus/operator sources distinguish German, French,
Mandarin, Russian and Spanish rather than defaulting everything to Spanish.
Lasley is **two-way** and Lumberg **one-way**; Eiber's future two-way kindergarten
start is not assigned to its older grades. Stetson's official notice ends DLI
after **2025–26**, while its early-grade policy is **80% Spanish / 20% English**.
The later Denver guide's 2026–27 rollouts and conflicting charter-section/grade
headers remain visible. Denver Language School's French track is **grades 6–8**.
June Creek and Thompson Valley require further school/programme history checks.

**New Mexico.** Only structural Appendix F of the state annual report is imported.
Its 419 school-name rows contain **137 DL flags**, reconciling to every printed
district total after the page-104 merged-cell repair. Narrative totals say
415 schools and 525 programmes, whereas the printed model totals sum to 524;
those discrepancies are preserved, not resolved by forcing row counts. The
report data are **2024–25** even though it was published/released later. The
December 2025 public university map supplies additional continuity and screening
evidence, with its stated non-exhaustiveness and verification limits. Mixed
Navajo/Spanish/Zuni/Keres/Tiwa cells do not establish DLI for every language.
Navajo Elementary in Albuquerque is a **Spanish** programme and is distinct
from the Gallup school of the same name. Farmington identifies Apache Navajo
DL in **K–1** and McCormick Spanish DL in **K–2**, separately from K–5 language
and culture pull-outs. ASLA explicitly teaches **ASL/English** as a public
academic programme. Corrales' primary schedule gives Spanish **90% in K–1**
and **50% in 2–5**, differing from the map's summary; unequal Armijo ratio
ordering remains unquantified.

**Arizona.** District/school sources support 47 programme evidence groups at
46 schools. The 138 extracted third-party directory entries remain discovery
records. Puente de Hózhó has separate **Diné/English** and **Spanish/English**
academic tracks at a K–5 school; no three-language pupil schedule or K–8 campus
is manufactured. Its approximate early Diné percentages remain approximate
labels, while its grade-2-onward 50/50 target is a plan. Lowell explicitly offers
K–1 in 2025–26; its **160+160 minutes** cover core instruction and exclude English
specials. Hermosa Vista's whole-kindergarten participation does not establish
whole-school DLI. Tucson's older district pathway starts **90/10**, not universal
50/50; the model date and current campus links stay separate. Sahuaro is marked
as a later **2026–27** offering. Tarwater is Mandarin; San Tan is Spanish; a
Mandarin subject-assignment description at Coronado is not converted into a
50/50 time claim. Secondary language-only and elective continuations retain
separate eligibility questions.

**Nebraska.** The state roster is dated **July 2026**. Omaha's ten named
English/Spanish pathways are retained with its high-school content-credit rules;
a district-wide “half coursework” description is not a verified daily schedule
at every grade. Bryan has direct 2025–26 kindergarten evidence. Washington has
earlier academic English/Spanish reporting and current continuity, but its 50/50
**pupil mix** is not an instructional percentage. The state also lists private
Japanese and Hebrew candidates. Connely advertises calendar-2027 intake and
foreign-language subjects; Friedel describes Hebrew within Jewish Studies.
Academic immersion scope and qualifying public funding remain unresolved.

**Kansas.** Blue Valley's elementary programme is **Mandarin**. Valley Park
explicitly serves **grades 3–5 in 2025–26** during phase-out; it gets no reference-year
K schedule. The later October 2026 end announcement is retained without inventing
an academic implementation year. Blue Valley's flexible middle-school electives
are not assigned to unnamed campus DLI programmes. New Stanley's proposed
closure was reversed in December 2025. Frances Willard's implementation table
remains a **plan** pending evidence of actual target-year operation; Morse's
hosting webpage does not make Morse a programme campus. Olathe's adopted
Black Bob building closure after 2026–27 and conditional immersion transfer to
Arbor Creek are distinct from a programme ending. Topeka retains subject-language
assignments without converting its equal pupil language mix into time shares.
Missouri schools returned by Kansas City searches are not added to Kansas.

## Remaining work and reproduction

The [backlog](../../data/reports/western_plains_2026_11_04_backlog.csv) records missing
reference-year confirmation, language-pair proof, one-/two-way organization,
strand/whole-school structure and numerical allocation plans. Utah historical
roster reconciliation and New Mexico grade-specific allocation collection are
substantial follow-up tasks. Arizona/Colorado district expansion and Nebraska
historical confirmation remain partial; tribal/private funding and secondary
content-course scope need targeted primary evidence. A missing field is not
evidence of an ended programme.

Builds are offline from saved inputs. Schema **1.3.0** keeps 28 tables and adds
state-level screening claims, allowing unresolved directory observations to be
retained without invented school or LEA matches. The audit protects the earlier
nine states' school/programme identity sets, original proficiency outputs, source
row totals, language roles, reference assessments and allocation periods.

```sh
dl-outcomes build
dl-outcomes validate
python scripts/validate_western_plains_inventory.py
python scripts/validate_structural_integration.py
python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

Local verification passed: **94 regression tests**, national and source-scope
validation, the preserved Texas/five-state validators, deterministic offline
rebuild and immutable-release checks, and row-count/field checks across all
**28 Parquet/DuckDB tables**. The [dated verification receipt](../../data/registry/western_plains_2026_11_04/validation_results.json)
identifies the exact tested build.
