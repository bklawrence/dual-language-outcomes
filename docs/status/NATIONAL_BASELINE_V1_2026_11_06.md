# National structural baseline v1

Frozen November 6, 2026. This consolidation adds a usable national structural dataset and completeness audit from research already in the repository. It adds no school/programme discoveries, source acquisitions, proficiency observations or identity merges.

The [baseline guide](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/README.md) links the exports and frozen evidence. Start with [programmes.csv](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/programmes.csv), [schools.csv](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/schools.csv) and [state_summary.csv](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/state_summary.csv). There are **5,131 programme-site evidence records** and **4,617 existing school research identities**. These are provisional research units, not counts of verified distinct programmes or a national census.

All 50 state research passes remain represented, including Mississippi and West Virginia’s screening-only status. A missing programme list or zero eligible denominator is not statewide absence. The baseline preserves candidates, future starts, preschool-only phases, discontinued hosts and secondary continuations in its full inventory.

The **2025–26 working reference file has 2,383 programme-site records**, comprising **2,194 with explicit qualifying reference-year reviews** and **189 Washington source-year listings** without a separate operation-status review. Flags distinguish these groups. **2,072 Texas/Oregon source-vintage rows** are exported separately: Texas retains its 2023–24 working reference and Oregon its 2026–27 directory. Neither is represented as independently confirmed 2025–26 operation.

[programme_fields.csv](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/programme_fields.csv) contains **43,218 field selections** across the target and supplementary reference years. Each selection retains its source IDs, actual evidence dates, inference/provisional/conflict status and temporal method. [field_evidence.csv](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/field_evidence.csv) links selected and excluded records to their source URLs and locations. Original configurations, languages, statuses and all 2,823 allocation schedules remain in the frozen evidence.

Population models and delivery structures retain the existing standard categories. The saved Lynn district description supports selecting Connery’s one-way classification while retaining DESE’s contradictory two-way code. Language aliases normalize explicitly identified Mandarin variants; unspecified Chinese is not converted to Mandarin, and English is never added automatically. Allocation normalization uses partner/English order only when the named shares establish it. Raw labels, percentage bases, source passages and grade/service differences remain available.

Secondary-only elementary entry models are not applicable, not missing. Schools serving any K–5 grade remain elementary-containing, including Miller’s grades 3–5 and Fowler’s grades 4–8. Millis/Milton grade-1 entry does not become kindergarten instruction. Unknown applicability is separately tallied. School-grade context retains its own year, with programme-policy or later-directory proxies marked explicitly. Campus-only allocation/delivery evidence is not assigned to every language strand.

[Completeness by state](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/completeness_by_state.csv), [language](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/completeness_by_language.csv), [school level](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/completeness_by_school_level.csv) and [district](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/gaps_by_district.csv) separates evidence availability from resolved classification. The [review queue](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/review_queue.csv) and [priorities](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/research_priorities.csv) support the next research pass. Counts rank the number of unresolved records; they do not invent a probability that research will succeed. Language groups can overlap and must not be summed as independent programmes.

[Coverage reconciliation](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/coverage_reconciliation.csv) retains comparisons with the previous audit. All state elementary denominators and all non-Texas model numerators remain unchanged. Texas retains **1,420 elementary school units**, while models supported before its source-year cutoff fall from **875 to 818**. The **57 affected units** are listed in [coverage_changes.csv](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/coverage_changes.csv): later policies are preserved without being counted as 2023–24 policy evidence. This is a date-selection correction in a new derived version; earlier files and underlying observations are not rewritten.

[Two potential alias/co-location groups](../../data/baselines/national_structural_v1/identity_review_queue.csv) remain for Theodore Roosevelt and Eleanor J. Toll middle schools in California. Canonical identities are retained. Grade-context NCES matches remain context, not automatic outcome joins. Native IDs preserve namespace, source/review status and known years.

The artifact includes the structural tables, claims, source catalogue, schema, rules, pinned requirements, executable pipeline and per-file checksums. Its independent reproduction reads this snapshot, not future live tables. Creating an existing baseline ID fails. Baseline checks run in normal validation and CI, and portable releases include registered baseline artifacts. The canonical evidence tables, prior reports/state inventories/raw archives and twelve assessment/context outputs are preserved. Further source collection and missing-data recovery remain deferred to the next task.
