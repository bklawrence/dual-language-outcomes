## Denominators and limits

- California, Idaho, Montana, Nevada and Wyoming use documented qualifying programs confirmed operating in 2025–26, including the accepted continuity rule. Schools are deduplicated using an official identifier when available, otherwise the stable school research key. An allocation at one track does not establish completeness for every track at that campus.
- California's high percentage applies to 215 confirmed, curated schools. At least 1,310 historical school leads form a substantially larger research backlog and are not a verified statewide DL-school denominator.
- Oregon excludes the directory's separately classified non-DLI rows. Matched NCES identifiers are preferred for school deduplication; the unmatched new school retains its directory key. Three grade-band identities at Four Rivers collapse to one NCES school, giving 194 school units rather than 196 directory identities. Numerical grade-varying ratios count; generic “other” labels do not.
- Texas uses a provisional campus inventory based on TAPR reporting groups, not a confirmed census of campuses hosting DL programs. There are 848 campuses with numeric allocation/model labels, of which 823 have at least one citation. Only 562 campuses (34.9% of the working set) have both a cited allocation and an unambiguous provisional language-policy link. None establishes verified tested-cohort exposure.
- Washington's 100% means a published annual program-model label for every school listed as a DL program in the 2025–26 source. It does not establish grade-specific/K schedules or an explicitly reported English partner language.
- Illinois is a 12-school provisional candidate seed with one published allocation plan, not a statewide inventory. Four excluded school records are omitted.

## Calculation inputs

The [machine-readable table](../../data/reports/allocation_coverage_by_state_2026_11_04.csv) records counts, percentages, denominator definitions and limits. The source files are the five `state_dl_programs_*_2025_26.csv` inventories, Oregon's reconciled program observations, Texas Pass 50 language/policy exports, Washington's saved public program JSON, and the compatible Illinois seed. The initial shared national tables currently import Illinois, Texas and Washington; the other state research remains in preserved earlier exports.

These figures describe a saved status snapshot. New collection or identity reconciliation should produce another dated report rather than silently change this one.
