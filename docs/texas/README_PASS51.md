## Preferred files and filters

- [Campus language inventory](../../data/registry/texas/texas_campus_language_inventory_pass51.csv): one row per campus; campus evidence and district policy support remain separate.
- [Language evidence records](../../data/registry/texas/texas_language_program_tracks_pass51.csv) and [language changes](../../data/registry/texas/texas_language_classification_audit_pass51.csv): original IDs and raw history retained; `pass51_previous_*` fields identify this pass's baseline.
- [Allocation evidence](../../data/registry/texas/texas_k_allocation_evidence_pass51.csv) and [allocation change ledger](../../data/registry/texas/texas_allocation_cleanup_audit_pass51.csv): before/after citations, dates, classifications and policy links.
- [Source register](../../data/registry/texas/pass51/source_register.csv), [review decisions](../../data/registry/texas/pass51/), [archived public evidence](../../data/raw/tx/pass51/) and [hash manifest](../../data/registry/texas/manifest_pass51.json).
- [Summary counts](../../data/registry/texas/cleanup_summary_pass51.json), [coverage comparison](../../data/reports/texas_cleanup_coverage_pass51_2026_11_04.csv) and [current file pointer](../../data/registry/texas/current_inventory.json).

Use `pass51_review_status`, `pass51_k_support` and `pass51_observation_origin` when selecting research allocation records. `NOT_REVIEWED` means this pass did not assess the row. `PREK_PROXY_NOT_K_QUANTIFIED`, `MODEL_LABEL_MINUTES_DIFFER`, `SOURCE_DOES_NOT_SUPPORT_PROGRAM_GRADE_PERIOD` and `UNRECOVERED` must not be treated as verified K schedules. A policy link is not a verified cohort or reporting-model assignment, including at a campus with more than one TAPR category.

The shared schema advances additively to **1.1.0**, adding nullable `evidence_review_status` and `grade_support_status` fields to `allocation_schedules`. They carry these distinctions into CSV, Parquet and DuckDB without parsing prose. Every Texas schedule still has `schedule_status=reported_policy_requires_review`; none is converted to named-language numerical shares. The earlier frozen schema 1.0.0 release remains intact.

## Time, outcomes and remaining work

The requested program-confirmation reference remains **2025–26**. The Texas reporting universe remains **2023–24**. A 2026 retrieval, 2019 handbook upload path, model rollout date or retrospective report is not silently converted to a campus validity year. District-policy pairs receive no new campus-specific historical year. The 2022 Spring change blocks treating its 2021 citation as uninterrupted one-way 50/50 evidence.

The four saved TPRS groups still produce the same eight subject observations: all tested grades, current-EL one-way DL, with one district aggregate. No outcomes are assigned to newly supported languages. The Ysleta study is recorded as an [assessment source lead](../../data/registry/texas/pass51/assessment_source_leads.json): district comparisons by grade in 2021–22, using English/Spanish Istation BOY-to-EOY gains. It is a vendor study, not a state report; its dual-assessment inclusion criterion does not itself establish membership in a particular DL cohort. It has not been imported as a DL-only outcome dataset.

**762 campuses still lack any allocation/model label.** The stop rule for this pass is to leave those broader gaps for later collection. Govalle's one-way ratio, Aldine K schedules, Ysleta's minute/model discrepancy, secondary language evidence and dated campus rollout records remain listed in the [gap/change ledger](../../data/registry/texas/pass51/change_and_gap_reviews.json). Existing other-state snapshots have not been integrated or recollected during this pass.
