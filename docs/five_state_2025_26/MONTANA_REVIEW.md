# Montana: academic immersion versus language classes

Reference school year: **2025–26**. Rechecked November 4, 2026.

The sources establish real academic immersion at several sites; they also show
why a Native-language class, cultural activity, or “immersion” label cannot by
itself describe a school's entire instructional model. Under the revised rule,
five publicly supported qualifying programs are confirmed: Crow Agency and
Hardin Primary have 2025 announcements; Bergan, Wyola and Pretty Eagle use the
documented continuity assessment. Nk̓ʷusm and Cuts Wood have confirmed continuing
school operation by that assessment, but remain outside strict public-program
counts because program-level public funding is unresolved.

## Consequential distinctions

**Bergan's teacher workday is not a complete student language timetable.** The
2016 exhibit includes a morning kindergarten immersion block, time with the
immersion cohort for math, and three afternoon “specials” blocks for students.
Adding every block would confuse different cohorts and would not establish a
student's daily instructional-language split. The selected historical 50/50 K
model comes from the separate explicit half-day account, not arithmetic on that
teacher schedule. The exhibit supports strand because it explicitly compares
the immersion cohort with the rest of the school.

**Nk̓ʷusm's 70/30 figures describe curriculum categories.** Its brochure says
70% of curriculum concerns tradition, culture and the Salish worldview through
language, and 30% concerns math and English. Those are neither a kindergarten
schedule nor 70% Salish / 30% English language-time shares. K allocation remains
unknown. Its FY ending July 2025 Form 990 reports $5,667 government grants at
Part VIII line 1e, but does not identify their purpose. The inventory therefore
does not automatically treat its K–8 program as publicly funded.

**Pretty Eagle's grade scope needs care.** The state report describes Pre-K–4
field/lab work across several schools and names Pretty Eagle teachers; it does
not allocate each grade or classroom to each campus. The Lodge Grass preschool
is not silently treated as proof of K–8 immersion at the academy. The retained
record is a documented partial content-immersion project with these limits.

**Temporary closure differs from program termination.** Wyola resumed classes
on October 17, 2023 after a short shutdown; that does not demonstrate an end of
its language immersion. Chickadee's operator, by contrast, explicitly announced
nonreopening after funding ended, so its old academic description is not promoted
to current operation by continuity.
