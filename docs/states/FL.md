# Florida

The November 5, 2026 structural pass integrates **146 programme records / 138 provisional school/campus units**, including reference-year confirmations, separately flagged candidates and dated continuations or future starts. This is a partial source-reviewed inventory, not a statewide census.

Among confirmed qualifying 2025–26 elementary hosts in the reviewed working set, **55/107 (51.4%)** have numerical allocation-model information across cited source periods. Secondary-only schools remain in the inventory and outside this denominator. Later policies, grade rollout and funding are separately dated/flagged; this percentage does not establish each campus’s 2025 K model or observed exposure.
