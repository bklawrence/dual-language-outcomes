# Texas national-model pilot

For K-allocation coverage, use the [revised school view](../../data/reports/texas_kindergarten_allocation_coverage_2026_11_04.csv) and [denominator/evidence rules](../status/KINDERGARTEN_ALLOCATION_COVERAGE_2026_11_04.md). Secondary continuations and other non-K campuses remain in the inventory with K allocation marked not applicable. The archived 2024–25 campus directory provides dated grade-range context; K-serving-campus proxies remain separate from programme-grade confirmation, and unknown grades remain a review category. Earlier all-campus Pass 48–51 snapshots are preserved.

Inputs are the additive Pass 51 language/linked-policy exports, Pass 44 structural evidence and the four saved Pass 45 TPRS observations. The [Texas cleanup report](../texas/README_PASS51.md) documents the evidence, dates and unresolved cases. Pass 50 and earlier snapshots remain intact.

The pilot migrates 1,610 campus identities and 1,863 **provisional evidence groups**. The `TX_P50_language_track_id` namespace and existing program/site identities stay stable across the cleanup; these are not asserted to be 1,863 distinct programs. Pass 51 resolves 83 unknown pair records at their stated evidence scope, largely district policy. District policy is not backdated to a campus/year, and later Wooten evidence remains later evidence. Unknown pairings receive no invented language rows.

All 970 original policy observations remain in `allocation_schedules`, with 23 separate current Spring policy observations added. Together with the Illinois plan, the shared table has 994 rows. Source dates and grade support remain explicit: 27 Aldine K entries are pre-K proxies, 31 Ysleta model labels disagree with listed subject minutes, and 23 older Spring citations do not support one-way K 50/50. Nullable `evidence_review_status` and `grade_support_status` fields in schema 1.1.0 expose these distinctions for filtering. No Texas label is converted to named-language numerical shares or tested-cohort exposure. Structural claims remain school-level evidence; whole-school DL does not imply a whole-school Spanish track.

Seventeen newly reviewed public sources are archived under `data/raw/tx/pass51/`, with content hashes, capture method and retrieval timestamps. The configured source register and cleanup manifest include portable decisions, raw source snapshots and extracts. This pass does not independently establish 2025–26 operation for every reporting campus.

Four TPRS reporting groups produce eight subject observations: ELA and mathematics, 2023–24, all tested grades, **BE-Dual One-Way current emergent bilingual/EL**. Three reporting units are campuses and one is Round Rock ISD. Numeric denominators are absent in the saved extract. Grade-specific, partner-language-specific and non-EL DL participant results cannot be recovered from these values.

Reporting-group/candidate links remain unresolved or combined; none establishes direct track attribution. The district aggregate has no individual-campus links. Original public report URLs, source references and saved-extract hashes remain available in provenance.

The first assessment layer is therefore a small, explicitly scoped pilot. Statewide academic/context data, English-language proficiency results, EL exit criteria/rates and SEDA have not yet been collected into the shared Texas tables. This status does not mean Texas fails to publish them.
