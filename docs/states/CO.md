# Colorado

Partial structural working set: 80 provisional programme evidence groups at
68 school units. Ten qualifying groups at nine schools are confirmed for
2025–26 under their recorded evidence/continuity method.

The [CDE list](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/clde/coloradoduallanguageimmersion/districtsandschoolsimplementingdli)
is voluntary and undated. Its 52 observations retain reviewed Pioneer/University
Hill aliases. Primary sources add campuses and distinguish French, German,
Mandarin, Russian and Spanish. Stetson ends after 2025–26; later Denver guide
rollouts and mixed section years are preserved. June Creek and Thompson Valley
still require school/programme history checks.

See [batch findings and limits](../status/WESTERN_PLAINS_2026_11_04.md),
[current state counts](../../data/reports/western_plains_2026_11_04_by_state.csv)
and [saved sources](../../data/registry/western_plains_2026_11_04/sources.csv).
District expansion, target-year evidence and grade allocations remain partial.
New proficiency collection is deferred.
