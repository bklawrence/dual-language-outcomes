# Oregon structural research integration

The 2026–27 directory integration preserves 223 observations:211 DLI-directory observations,209 provisional program keys across 194 school units, plus 12 separate heritage/revitalization screening records. These are listings and reviewed evidence groups, not verified distinct-program or participant counts.

Approved NCES identities retain their 2024–25 reference year. Four Rivers’ three grade-band keys share one school; McDaniel’s four observations retain two program keys. Columbia Vista remains unmatched. Composite language cells do not establish independent tracks or track-specific whole-school/population models. English pairing and K allocations are not inferred from annual model labels.
