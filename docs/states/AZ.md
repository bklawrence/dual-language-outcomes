# Arizona

Partial structural working set: 47 provisional programme evidence groups at
46 school units. Six qualifying groups at five schools are confirmed for
2025–26 under their recorded evidence/continuity method.

Primary district/school sources distinguish Spanish, Mandarin and Diné/English
programmes. Puente de Hózhó has two separate K–5 tracks. Lowell offers K–1 in
2025–26; its 50/50 core-minute denominator excludes English specials. Tucson's
district model starts 90/10 and has a later Sahuaro offering. Language-course
continuations are screened separately from academic content immersion. The 138
third-party directory records remain discovery leads, not verified DL campuses.
