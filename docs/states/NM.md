# New Mexico

Partial structural working set: 143 provisional programme evidence groups at
143 school-name units. Of these, 117 qualifying groups/schools are confirmed for
2025–26 through the recorded evidence/continuity rule.

The [state annual report, archived by ERIC](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED680535.pdf)
contains **2024–25** structural data. Appendix F yields 419 school rows and
137 DL flags, separate from maintenance, heritage, enrichment and transitional
models. Printed district/model totals reconcile; narrative counts differ and
remain documented. The [university map](https://lobolanguage.unm.edu/community-outreach/multilingual-education-programs-in-new-mexico-interactive-map.html)
is non-exhaustive and has verification limits. Composite language/model cells
do not make every listed Indigenous language an immersion track. ASLA is an
ASL/English public academic programme; Farmington distinguishes early-grade
Navajo/Spanish immersion from later language/culture pull-outs.

See [batch findings and limits](../status/WESTERN_PLAINS_2026_11_04.md),
[current state counts](../../data/reports/western_plains_2026_11_04_by_state.csv)
and [saved sources](../../data/registry/western_plains_2026_11_04/sources.csv).
Grade-specific allocations, school structure, language/model mapping and native
school/LEA identities remain priority gaps. Report scores are not imported.
