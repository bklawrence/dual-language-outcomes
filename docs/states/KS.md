11 school units. Eight qualifying groups/schools have 2025–26 confirmation
under their recorded direct/continuity method.

the 2025–26 phase-out. Its later end announcement and optional middle electives
remain distinct from that year's elementary offering. New Stanley's closure
proposal was reversed in December 2025; Frances Willard's rollout remains a plan
pending implementation evidence. Olathe's Black Bob closure/conditional Arbor
Creek transfer is later. Topeka's pupil mix is not an instructional percentage;
Missouri schools and Morse's hosting webpage are not Kansas programme campuses.
