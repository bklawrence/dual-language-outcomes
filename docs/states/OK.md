Status: partial structural inventory, reviewed November 5, 2026, with a 2025–26 operation reference. [State CSVs and sources](../../data/registry/south_central_2026_11_05/README.md); [findings and limits](../status/SOUTH_CENTRAL_2026_11_05.md).

The inventory retains 25 programme records at 21 provisional school/campus units, including uncertain mappings, phaseout evidence and later starts. Fifteen qualifying hosts are confirmed in the working set: 14 elementary and one secondary. Three elementary units have numerical allocation models (21.4%).

Eisenhower's French and Spanish programmes and Zarrow's Spanish programme publish K–2 90/10. Truman Primary's September-2025 implementation account gives 50/50. Tulsa's other dual-language strands quantify literacy time without a total-day ratio. Cherokee, Osage and Yuchi sources distinguish academic-medium instruction from language classes, with separate funding and governance qualifications. Greasy K operation is explicitly confirmed; Kenwood remains a satellite implementation question.

Western Heights school mapping, Jenks' phaseout and final continuation year, Yuchi's public funding and Osage's actual reopening remain review work. Adelaide Lee starts in fall 2026; announced Zarrow/Felicitas Mendez building moves apply in 2027–28. No new proficiency observations are collected.
