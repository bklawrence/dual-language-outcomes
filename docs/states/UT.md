# Utah

Partial structural working set: 351 provisional programme evidence groups at
282 school units. Reference year is 2025–26; 34 qualifying groups at 33 schools
are confirmed by the stated direct/continuity method.

The [USBE directory](https://schools.utah.gov/curr/dualimmersion/resources/DLI-Schools_2026-27.pdf)
is **2026–27**, not a 2025–26 census. Its 280 rows / 348 language cells include
Russian and secondary continuations. Elementary grade-1 models do not supply K
allocations. Cache magnet moves and the South Davis French consolidation remain
separate from reference-year operation.

See [batch findings and limits](../status/WESTERN_PLAINS_2026_11_04.md),
[current state counts](../../data/reports/western_plains_2026_11_04_by_state.csv)
and [saved sources](../../data/registry/western_plains_2026_11_04/sources.csv).
Historical roster reconciliation, allocations, population models and school
structure remain substantial collection tasks. New proficiency collection is deferred.
