# Nebraska

Partial structural working set: 18 provisional programme/candidate groups at
18 school units. Two qualifying groups/schools have 2025–26 confirmation.

The [state directory](https://www.education.ne.gov/worldlanguage/dual-language/)
is dated July 2026. Omaha names ten English/Spanish pathways, but its district
description does not establish one daily grade-level schedule. Bryan has direct
target-year K recruitment; Washington has earlier academic teaching evidence
and a current continuation. Equal pupil language mix is not instructional time.
Private Japanese, Hebrew and Catholic bilingual candidates retain separate
content-scope, public-funding and timing questions.

See [batch findings and limits](../status/WESTERN_PLAINS_2026_11_04.md),
[current state counts](../../data/reports/western_plains_2026_11_04_by_state.csv)
and [saved sources](../../data/registry/western_plains_2026_11_04/sources.csv).
Historical confirmation and programme-grade allocations remain major gaps.
New proficiency collection is deferred.
